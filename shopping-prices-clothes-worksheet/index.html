1 · Shopping Expressions & Prices in Won · 쇼핑 필수 표현과 가격 읽기
KoreanEnglishUsage / ContextReading tip
이거 얼마예요?How much is this?Asking the price of an item—

조금만 깎아 주세요Please give me a discountAsking politely for a discount깎아 [까까]
입어 봐도 돼요?Can I try this on?Asking to try on fitting room clothes입어 [이버]

Prices in Korea always use Sino-Korean numbers + 원: 1,000원 = 천 원, 10,000원 = 만 원, 50,000원 = 오만 원.

2 · Clothing Items & Specific Wearing Verbs · 옷 어휘와 착용 동사
ItemKoreanWearing verbCounter unit
Shirt / T-shirt셔츠 / 티셔츠입다 (to put on clothes)한 벌, 두 벌 (벌)
Pants / Trousers바지입다 (to put on clothes)한 벌, 두 벌 (벌)
Skirt치마입다 (to put on clothes)한 벌, 두 벌 (벌)
Coat / Jacket코트 / 재킷입다 (to put on clothes)한 벌, 두 벌 (벌)
Shoes / Sneakers신발 / 운동화신다 (to put on footwear)한 켤레, 두 켤레 (켤레)
Socks양말신다 (to put on footwear)한 켤레, 두 켤레 (켤레)
Hat / Cap모자쓰다 (to put on headwear)한 개, 두 개 (개)

Korean distinguishes wearing verbs by body part: 입다 (body/clothes), 신다 (feet/shoes), 쓰다 (head/hats/glasses), 끼다 (hands/gloves).

3 · Size, Fit & Compliments · 크기와 어울림
Word / PhraseEnglishOpposite / RelatedExample sentence
크다Big / Loose작다 (Small / Tight)바지가 너무 커요.

딱 맞다Fits just right크거나 작지 않다사이즈가 딱 맞아요.
사이즈 / 치수Size대(L) · 중(M) · 소(S)더 큰 사이즈 있어요?
잘 어울리다Looks great on you안 어울리다이 옷이 잘 어울려요.

Polite present forms: 커요 (it is big), 작아요 (it is small), 맞아요 (it fits), 잘 어울려요 (it looks good on you).

How to use it

  • Read Korean prices using Sino-Korean numbers: 천 원 (1,000), 만 원 (10,000), 오만 원 (50,000).
  • Remember the wearing verb rules: 입다 for clothing, 신다 for footwear, and 쓰다 for headwear.
  • Solve Practice A to D and verify your answers using the answer key.
