-기에

뜻 · Meaning

문어적으로 근거나 이유를 말할 때 씁니다.

Used in written style to state a reason or ground for an action.

형태 · Form

Verb/Adj stem + 「-기에」

주의할 점 · Watch out

The subject of the following clause is usually the speaker.

예문 · Examples

  • 하늘이 맑기에 창문을 활짝 열었다.

A: 왜 갑자기 창문을 열었어요?

B: 날씨가 너무 맑기에 환기 좀 시키려고요.

② 빈칸에 알맞은 말을 써요 · Fill in the blank (-기에)

  1. 바람이 선선하 산책을 나섰습니다.Because the wind was cool, I went out for a walk.
  2. 음식이 맛있어 보이 주문했어요.Because the food looked delicious, I ordered it.
  3. 불빛이 환하 다가가 보았습니다.Because the light was bright, I approached it.

③ 한국어로 써요 · Write it in Korean (use -기에)

  1. Because I heard the sound of water, I stopped my steps.
  2. Because the sky was so clear, I went out for a walk.
