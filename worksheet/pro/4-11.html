-길래

뜻 · Meaning

구어적으로 이유를 말할 때 씁니다. 앞 절의 주어는 보통 내가 아닙니다.

Used in spoken Korean to give a reason. The subject of the first clause is usually not the speaker.

형태 · Form

Verb/Adj stem + 「-길래」

주의할 점 · Watch out

Used in place of 「-기에」 in everyday spoken conversation.

예문 · Examples

  • 맛있어 보이길래 하나 샀어요.

A: 웬 우산이야?

B: 나가는데 비가 오길래 하나 사 왔어.

② 빈칸에 알맞은 말을 써요 · Fill in the blank (-길래)

  1. 날씨가 춥 따뜻하게 입었어요.Since weather was cold, I dressed warmly.
  2. 줄이 길 저도 서 봤어요.Since the line was long, I also stood in it.
  3. 책이 재미있 단숨에 다 읽었죠.Since the book was interesting, I read it all at once.

③ 한국어로 써요 · Write it in Korean (use -길래)

  1. Since the phone rang, I answered quickly.
  2. Because it rained, I took an umbrella and went out.
