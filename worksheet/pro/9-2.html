-고도

뜻 · Meaning

그렇게 하고서도 오히려 반대로 행동함을 나타냅니다.

Indicates acting in an opposite or contradictory way even after doing that action.

형태 · Form

Verb stem + 「-고도」

주의할 점 · Watch out

The subject must be identical in both the preceding and following clauses.

예문 · Examples

  • 알고도 모른 척했어요.

A: 걔 진짜 몰랐대?

B: 알고도 모른 척하는 것 같던데.

① 바꿔 써요 · Change the word — -고도

걷다→
부르다→

② 빈칸에 알맞은 말을 써요 · Fill in the blank (-고도)

  1. 듣 못 들은 척했습니다.Even after hearing, I pretended not to hear.
  2. 보 믿기지 않는 광경이었어요.It was a sight unbelievable even after seeing.
  3. 밥을 배불리 먹 또 먹네요.Even after eating full of rice, he eats again.

③ 한국어로 써요 · Write it in Korean (use -고도)

  1. Even after committing wrong, he doesn't even apologize.
  2. Even knowing the truth, I maintained silence.
