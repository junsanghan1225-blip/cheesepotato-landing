-(으)려다 말다

뜻 · Meaning

하려다가 도중에 그만두었다는 뜻입니다.

Indicates intending to do something but giving it up midway.

형태 · Form

돕다→도우려다 말다
쓰다→쓰려다 말다

Verb stem + 「-(으)려다 말다」

주의할 점 · Watch out

Used when you changed your mind and decided not to do the action.

예문 · Examples

  • 전화하려다 말았어요.

A어제 나한테 전화하려고 했어?

B응, 전화하려다 늦은 것 같아서 그냥 말았어.

TOPIK 에서는 · In TOPIK-style sentences

회의 중에 의견을 말하려다 말고 분위기가 너무 무거워서 참았다.

I was about to state my opinion during the meeting, but stopped because the atmosphere was too heavy.

② 빈칸에 알맞은 말을 써요 · Fill in the blank (-(으)려다 말다)

  1. 물어보고 참았습니다.I intended to ask, but stopped and held back.
  2. 외출하고 돌아왔어요.I intended to go out, but stopped and returned.
  3. 사고 그냥 놓았습니다.I intended to buy, but stopped and put it down.

③ 순서대로 써요 · Put the words in order

  1. 꾹 / 말고 / 내려다 / 화를 / 참았어요I was about to get angry, but held it in.

④ 한국어로 써요 · Write it in Korean (use -(으)려다 말다)

  1. I intended to argue, but stopped and saved words.
  2. I was about to make a call, but stopped and checked the watch.
