## 영어 소개 (붙여 넣기)
- **TOPIK II Writing 51-52: 100 Practice Questions**
  The two fill-in writing questions of TOPIK II, in one workbook. 100 original questions (50 for Question 51, 50 for Question 52), a guide for every text type, scoring points, model answers and the mistakes that lose marks. Includes a reader bonus code for free Pro access on everykoreans.com. All questions are newly written; none is a past exam question. TOPIK is a test name of the National Institute for International Education. This book is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NIIED or the TOPIK administration.
- **Korean First Steps Workbook**
  A beginner workbook built from our most-used free worksheets: Hangul and final consonants first, then greetings, numbers and dates, family, food, shopping and the basic particles. Every unit has a short explanation, tables to copy, practice and an answer key at the back. Each unit has a QR code to free online practice on everykoreans.com, and the book includes a reader bonus for Pro.
- **Korean Particles Workbook**
  Particles are the small words that glue Korean together. Each unit takes one particle: its meaning, its form, examples, the trap to avoid, and practice. A "Choose the right particle" part sets look-alikes side by side (은/는 vs 이/가, 에 vs 에서 and more), and review tests mix everything. Answer key at the back, QR codes to free online practice, and a reader bonus for Pro.
