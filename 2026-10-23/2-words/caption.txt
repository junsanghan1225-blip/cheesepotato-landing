Korean words: Working life 🇰🇷
직장생활 — 한국어 단어 5개

1. 직장 (jikjang) — workplace, job
2. 퇴근 (toegeun) — leaving work
3. 수고 (sugo) — trouble / effort
4. 출근 (chulgeun) — going to work
5. 수고하다 (sugohada) — to work hard
