Beginner — 112 patterns

PatternMeaningExample
N이다Used to say what something is. Ends in 「이에요」 if the noun has a final consonant, and 「예요」 if it does not.저는 학생이에요.
N이/가 있다·없다Says whether something exists or not. Also used for having something.시간이 있어요?
숫자 (한자어 · 순우리말)Korean has two number systems: Sino-Korean (prices, dates, minutes) and native Korean (counting items, age, hours).커피 두 잔에 팔천 원이에요.
날짜와 요일Dates are read using Sino-Korean numbers. Days of the week attach 「요일」 to the day name.제 생일은 오월 삼일이에요.
시간Hours are read in native Korean numbers, while minutes are read in Sino-Korean numbers.세 시 삼십 분에 만나요.
A/V-(스)ㅂ니다The most formal speech style. Used in presentations, meetings, news broadcasts, and when meeting someone for the first time.저는 한국어를 공부합니다.
A/V-아/어요The most common everyday polite style. It sounds polite and soft, making it suitable for almost any daily situation.매일 아침에 커피를 마셔요.
A/V-았/었어요Used to talk about actions or states that have already ended in the past.어제 친구를 만났어요.
V-(으)ㄹ 거예요 ①Used to talk about future actions or plans that have already been decided.내일 도서관에 갈 거예요.
V-고 있다 ①Indicates that an action is currently in progress right now.지금 밥을 먹고 있어요.
A/V-았/었었어요Used to emphasize that a past state or event is no longer true today.예전에는 담배를 피웠었어요.
단어 부정 (있다↔없다, 알다↔모르다)Words that have their own distinct antonyms instead of taking the negative prefix 「안」.그 사람 이름은 몰라요.
안 A/V-아/어요Expresses simple negative action or your intention not to do something.저는 커피를 안 마셔요.
못 V-아/어요Expresses that you want to do something but cannot due to lack of ability or circumstances.매워서 못 먹겠어요.
N이/가Subject marker pointing out who or what performs the action. Often introduces new information.친구가 왔어요.
N은/는Topic marker used to introduce what you are talking about or to draw a contrast with something else.저는 한국 음식을 좋아해요.
N을/를Object marker indicating the target of an action.저녁마다 한국 드라마를 봐요.
N와/과, N(이)랑, N하고Joins two nouns, or says you did something together with someone. All three mean the same and differ only in tone.친구하고 영화를 봤어요.
N의Possessive marker indicating ownership or belonging.이건 동생의 우산이에요.
N에 ① (장소·시간)Indicates the location where something exists or the time when an event occurs.책상 위에 안경이 있어요.
N에 ② (방향·단위)Indicates a destination you are heading toward, or marks a unit of measurement/price.한 개에 천 원이에요.
N에서Marks where something happens, or where it starts from.도서관에서 공부해요.
N에서 N까지, N부터 N까지Expresses 'from where to where' for locations, and 'from when to when' for time.집에서 회사까지 삼십 분 걸려요.
N에게/한테Marks the person you give something to or say something to.친구한테 선물을 줬어요.
N도Particle meaning 'also' or 'too', indicating addition or inclusion.저도 그 영화를 봤어요.
N만Particle meaning 'only' or 'just', indicating exclusivity.저는 물만 마실게요.
N밖에Nothing but that — and it feels like too little.천 원밖에 없어요.
N(으)로Indicates a tool, method, direction, or material used.지하철로 가면 빨라요.
N(이)나 ① (선택)Particle expressing an option between choices, meaning 'or'.커피나 차를 드릴까요?
N(이)나 ② (많음)Attached to numbers to express surprise that an amount is more than expected.세 시간이나 기다렸어요.
N쯤Attached to nouns to indicate an approximation or estimate.한 시간쯤 걸려요.
N처럼, N같이Compares something to what it resembles.가수처럼 노래를 잘해요.
N보다Compares two things and says which one is more.오늘이 어제보다 더 추워요.
N마다Every one without exception, or repeating at that interval.주말마다 등산을 해요.
A/V-고Connects two actions or states side by side, or lists sequential actions.이 식당은 싸고 맛있어요.
V-거나One or the other — a choice between two actions.주말에는 책을 읽거나 영화를 봐요.
A/V-지만The first half and the second half go against each other.비싸지만 정말 맛있어요.
A/V-(으)ㄴ/는데 ①Smoothly connects contrasting clauses. It sounds far more natural in conversation than 「-지만」.동생은 키가 큰데 저는 작아요.
N 전에, V-기 전에Before that — you do this first.밥 먹기 전에 손을 씻어요.
N 후에, V-(으)ㄴ 후에After that thing has finished.퇴근한 후에 운동해요.
V-고 나서You finish the first thing completely, then do the next.숙제를 하고 나서 놀았어요.
V-아/어서 (순서)Expresses that the first action is completed and directly connected to the following action.친구를 만나서 밥을 먹었어요.
N 때, A/V-(으)ㄹ 때At that time, or around when that happens.학생 때 자주 놀러 갔어요.
V-(으)면서Doing two things at the same time.음악을 들으면서 공부해요.
N 중, V-는 중Indicates that someone is in the middle of doing an action right now.지금 회의 중이에요.
V-자마자The next thing happens the instant the first one ends.집에 오자마자 잤어요.
N 동안, V-는 동안Throughout that stretch of time.방학 동안 아르바이트를 했어요.
V-(으)ㄴ 지Used to state how much time has passed since doing an action.한국에 온 지 일 년 됐어요.
V-(으)ㄹ 수 있다/없다Says whether you can or cannot do something. Covers both ability and possibility.저는 한국어를 조금 할 수 있어요.
V-(으)ㄹ 줄 알다/모르다Expresses knowing or not knowing how to do something as a learned skill.저는 수영할 줄 몰라요.
V-(으)세요Asks or tells someone to do something, politely. It also raises the listener.여기 앉으세요.
V-지 마세요A polite way to tell or ask someone not to do something.여기에서는 사진을 찍지 마세요.
A/V-아/어야 되다/하다Expresses necessity or obligation, meaning one must do something.내일까지 끝내야 돼요.
A/V-아/어도 되다Used to give permission, meaning it is okay to do something.여기 앉아도 돼요?
A/V-(으)면 안 되다Used to prohibit an action, stating that one must not do it.여기에서 담배를 피우면 안 돼요.
A/V-지 않아도 되다Means that it is completely fine not to do something.내일은 일찍 오지 않아도 돼요.
V-고 싶다Expresses that you want to do something.한국에 가고 싶어요.
A/V-았/었으면 좋겠다Expresses a wish for something to happen when it has not happened yet.내일 날씨가 좋았으면 좋겠어요.
A/V-아/어서 (이유)States that something happened because of this reason. It is the most basic way to express cause.배가 아파서 못 갔어요.
A/V-(으)니까 ①Gives a reason. Unlike 「-아/어서」, a request or a suggestion can follow.추우니까 창문을 닫아 주세요.
N 때문에, A/V-기 때문에Names the cause plainly. Common in writing.비 때문에 경기가 취소됐어요.
V-아/어 주세요, V-아/어 주시겠어요?Asks someone to do something for you. The second form is more polite.사진 좀 찍어 주세요.
V-아/어 줄게요, V-아/어 줄까요?Offers to do something for someone, or asks if they want you to do it.제가 도와 줄게요.
V-아/어 보다Means trying something out. It is also often used when making suggestions.이 떡볶이 한번 먹어 보세요.
V-(으)ㄴ 적이 있다/없다Expresses past experience by stating whether something has happened or not.한복을 입은 적이 있어요.
V-(으)ㄹ까요? ① (제안)Suggests doing something together by asking the listener's thought.같이 점심 먹을까요?
V-(으)ㄹ까요? ② (의향)Asks if the listener would like you to do something.창문을 열까요?
V-(으)ㅂ시다Suggests doing something together. It suits formal situations.이제 시작합시다.
V-(으)시겠어요?Asks someone's preference very politely. It is frequently used when serving guests.무엇을 드시겠어요?
V-(으)ㄹ래요? ①Casually asks what the listener wants to do among close acquaintances.같이 갈래요?
A/V-겠어요 ① (의지)Declares your clear intention to do something.제가 하겠습니다.
V-(으)ㄹ게요Tells the listener you will do something as if making a promise.제가 이따가 전화할게요.
V-(으)ㄹ래요 ② (의지)Casually states your own choice or intention to do something.저는 냉면 먹을래요.
A/V-(으)ㄴ/는데 ② (배경)Provides relevant background context before bringing up the main point. Used very often in Korean conversation.지금 밖인데 이따가 전화할게요.
V-(으)니까 ② (발견)Means discovering a fact after trying or experiencing something.먹어 보니까 생각보다 맛있네요.
V-(으)러 가다/오다States that you go or come in order to do something.밥을 먹으러 식당에 갔어요.
V-(으)려고You do something with that intention.한국에서 일하려고 한국어를 배워요.
V-(으)려고 하다Means you intend to do something, or that something is about to happen.내년에 유학을 가려고 해요.
N을/를 위해서, V-기 위해서Says what the purpose is. It suits writing.건강을 위해서 매일 걸어요.
V-기로 하다Means deciding to do something.내일 아침에 만나기로 했어요.
A/V-(으)면Means 'if' or 'when' something happens. Widely used for conditions and suppositions.시간이 있으면 같이 가요.
V-(으)려면If you intend to do that — and what follows is what it takes.표를 사려면 줄을 서야 해요.
A/V-아/어도Means that even in such a situation, the outcome remains unchanged.비가 와도 갈 거예요.
A/V-겠어요 ② (추측)Makes an immediate guess based on what you see, hear, or feel right now.와, 맛있겠어요!
A/V-(으)ㄹ 거예요 ② (추측)Expresses a guess that something is likely the case.지금쯤 도착했을 거예요.
A/V-(으)ㄹ까요? ③ (추측)Wonders together whether something might happen.내일 비가 올까요?
A/V-(으)ㄴ/는/(으)ㄹ 것 같다Softly expresses that something seems to be the case. Frequently used to avoid sounding overly direct.오늘은 좀 추운 것 같아요.
관형형 -(으)ㄴ/-는/-(으)ㄹ NChanges verbs and adjectives into forms that attach in front of nouns to modify them.어제 만난 사람이 누구예요?
A/V-기Turns verbs and adjectives into nouns.저는 노래하기를 좋아해요.
A-게Turns adjectives into adverbs to describe how an action is performed.머리를 짧게 잘랐어요.
A-아/어하다Used to describe another person's emotions observed from the outside.동생이 그 선물을 좋아해요.
V-고 있다 ② (착용)Indicates the ongoing state of wearing clothing, accessories, or footwear.안경을 쓰고 있어요.
V-아/어 있다Indicates that a state continues after an action has finished.문이 열려 있어요.
A-아/어지다Indicates a gradual change of state.날씨가 따뜻해졌어요.
V-게 되다Means that something ended up happening regardless of your own intention.회사 일로 부산에 살게 됐어요.
A/V-(으)ㄴ/는지Connects an uncertain clause within a sentence.그 사람이 오는지 모르겠어요.
V-는 데 걸리다/들다Expresses how much time or money it takes to do something.여기까지 오는 데 한 시간 걸렸어요.
A/V-지요?Used to ask for confirmation about something you already know. Shortened to 「-죠?」 in speech.오늘 좀 춥지요?
A/V-군요/는군요Expresses a realization or exclamation upon learning something you did not know before.여기가 그 유명한 곳이군요.
A/V-네요Expresses spontaneous admiration or reaction to what you are currently seeing or feeling directly.오늘 날씨가 정말 좋네요.
A-(으)ㄴ가요?, V-나요?Used to ask questions softly. It sounds more cautious and gentle than 「-아/어요?」.혹시 시간이 있으신가요?
A/V-(으)ㄴ/는데요Leaves the sentence open to create a lingering nuance. Used to decline softly or express mild surprise.저는 잘 모르겠는데요.
직접 인용Quotes someone's exact words directly as they were spoken.친구가 "내일 만나자"라고 했어요.
간접 인용Paraphrases and reports what you heard in your own words.친구가 내일 만나자고 했어요.
간접 인용 축약형Shortened forms of indirect quotations used in spoken Korean.내일 비가 온대요.
'ㅡ' 불규칙When stems ending in 「ㅡ」 meet 「-아/어」, the vowel 「ㅡ」 drops.머리가 너무 아파요.
'ㄹ' 불규칙The final consonant 「ㄹ」 drops before 「ㄴ, ㅂ, ㅅ」.저 사람을 아세요?
'ㅂ' 불규칙When the final consonant 「ㅂ」 meets a vowel, it changes to 「우」.오늘은 정말 추워요.
'ㄷ' 불규칙When the final consonant 「ㄷ」 meets a vowel, it changes to 「ㄹ」.음악을 들으면서 걸었어요.
'르' 불규칙When stems ending in 「르」 meet 「-아/어」, it changes into a double 「ㄹ」 (「ㄹㄹ」).저는 노래를 잘 몰라요.
'ㅅ' 불규칙When the final consonant 「ㅅ」 meets a vowel, it drops.감기가 다 나았어요.
'ㅎ' 불규칙For adjectives with a final consonant 「ㅎ」, the 「ㅎ」 drops and the vowel changes.이렇게 하면 돼요?
