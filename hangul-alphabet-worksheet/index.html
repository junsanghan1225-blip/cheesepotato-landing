Initial ㅇ has no sound and acts as a placeholder for vowels (아이 = a-i). At the end of a syllable, ㅇ makes the 'ng' sound (강 = gang).

2 · The 10 Basic Vowels · 기본 모음 10자
VowelSoundVowelSound
ㅏa (father)ㅓeo (like “u” in cup)
ㅗo (go)ㅜu (moon)
ㅡeu (like “oo” in book, lips flat)ㅣi (see)
ㅑya (yard)ㅕyeo (young)
ㅛyo (yo-yo)ㅠyu (youth)

Vertical vowels (ㅏ, ㅓ, ㅣ, ㅑ, ㅕ) go to the right of the consonant (가, 너). Horizontal vowels (ㅗ, ㅜ, ㅡ, ㅛ, ㅠ) go under the consonant (고, 누).

3 · How Hangul Syllables Work · 한글 음절 조합 원리

Every Korean syllable block has at least one consonant and one vowel: Initial consonant + Vowel (e.g., ㄱ + ㅏ = 가, ㅇ + ㅗ = 오). Many blocks add a final consonant (받침) at the bottom: Initial + Vowel + Final (e.g., ㄱ + ㅏ + ㄴ = 간, ㅂ + ㅏ + ㅂ = 밥).

How to use it

  • Memorize the 14 basic consonants and their traditional names (기역, 니은, 디귿...).
  • Practice combining vertical vowels (placed to the right) and horizontal vowels (placed underneath).
  • Complete Practice A to D and verify your answers with the answer key.
