격식 -ㅂ/습니다 · 5분

When to Use Formal (-ㅂ/습니다) vs Polite (-아/어요)

In Korean, both -ㅂ/습니다 (격식체) and -아/어요 (해요체) are polite and respectful. But they belong in completely different settings!

1. Formal Style (-ㅂ/습니다 / -(으)십시오):
- Public & Professional: Job interviews, business presentations, news broadcasts, subway announcements, and military.
- Creates professional distance and serious authority.
- *처음 뵙겠습니다. 잘 부탁드립니다.*

2. Polite Informal Style (-아/어요 / -(으)세요):
- Warm & Everyday: Talking with coworkers, neighbors, teachers, café staff, and friends.
- Creates friendly, approachable warmth.
- *안녕하세요? 오늘 날씨가 참 좋아요.*

Job interview / PresentationFormal (-ㅂ/습니다) · 열심히 하겠습니다 (I will work hard)
Subway / Public announcementFormal (-(으)십시오) · 안전선 뒤로 물러서십시오 (Please step back)
Ordering at a café / RestaurantPolite (-아/어요) · 따뜻한 아메리카노 한 잔 주세요
Daily chat with friendly coworkerPolite (-아/어요) · 주말에 뭐 했어요? (What did you do?)
이번 프로젝트 발표를 시작하겠습니다.I will now begin the project presentation. (Formal presentation)
문의 사항이 있으시면 언제든 연락 주십시오.If you have questions, please contact us anytime. (Formal notice)
오늘 점심 같이 먹으러 갈래요?Shall we go eat lunch together today? (Friendly polite chat)
