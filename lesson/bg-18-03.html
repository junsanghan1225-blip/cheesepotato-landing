3강. 실전 대조: 허락 vs 의무

되나요? 해야 하나요? · 7분

Notice the single-letter difference that completely changes the meaning:

- -아/어도 돼요? *(May I? — Asking permission)*
- -아/어야 돼요? *(Must I? — Asking about obligation)*

Mixing up 도 and 야 changes “May I leave?” into “Do I have to leave?”

여기 앉아도 돼요?-아/어도 되다 · May I sit here? (Permission)
지금 가야 돼요.-아/어야 되다 · I must go now. (Obligation)
신발을 벗어야 돼요?-아/어야 되다 · Do I have to take off my shoes?
먼저 먹어도 돼요.-아/어도 되다 · You may eat first. (Granting permission)
