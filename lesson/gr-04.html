Saying no: 안 and 못

Building Sentences · 6분

Korean has two different “not”, and picking the wrong one changes what you are saying about yourself.

안I choose not to · 안 가요 — I am not going
못I cannot · 못 가요 — I cannot go
This distinction matters socially. “안 갔어요” about a friend’s wedding sounds like you could not be bothered. “못 갔어요” says something prevented you. Same event, very different message.

The 하다 trap

With [noun + 하다] verbs, 안 goes inside, between the noun and 하다. 공부 안 해요 — not 안 공부해요.

The long forms

-지 않다 and -지 못하다 mean the same as 안 and 못, but sound a little more written. 가지 않아요 = 안 가요.
