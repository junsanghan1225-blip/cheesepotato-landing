2강. 정해진 일정 -(으)ㄹ 거예요

초급 세밀: -고 싶다 / -(으)ㄹ 거예요 뉘앙스 · 5분

What does this express?

-(으)ㄹ 거예요 expresses a scheduled or confirmed plan.

Not just a wish, but a locked plan: you bought the ticket, made the appointment, or firmly resolved to do it. It pairs with specific time words like “tomorrow” (내일), “next week” (다음 주), or “at 3:00” (세 시에).

Step 1 — Check for a final consonant (받침)

만나다 → 만나 … no 받침
읽다 → 읽 … has 받침 (ㄱ)

This single check determines everything.

Step 2 — No 받침 → -ㄹ 거예요 / Has 받침 → -을 거예요

No 받침 → stem + ㄹ 거예요
만나 + ㄹ 거예요 → 만날 거예요

Has 받침 → stem + 을 거예요
읽 + 을 거예요 → 읽을 거예요

만나다 — to meet만나라 · none · 만나ㄹ → 만날 거예요
보다 — to watch보 · none · 보ㄹ → 볼 거예요
읽다 — to read읽 · has ㄱ · 읽을 거예요
먹다 — to eat먹 · has ㄱ · 먹을 거예요
살다 — to live살 · ends in ㄹ · 살 거예요 (ㄹ stays as is)
내일 오전 열 시에 친구를 만날 거예요.I am meeting a friend at 10 tomorrow. — appointment made
이번 주말에는 집에서 영화를 볼 거예요.I am going to watch films at home this weekend.
올해는 책을 오십 권 읽을 거예요.I am going to read fifty books this year.
점심은 학교 앞에서 먹을 거예요.I will eat lunch in front of the school.
다음 달부터 서울에서 살 거예요.I am going to live in Seoul from next month.
If the stem already ends in ㄹ, do not add 을.

살다 → 살 거예요 (○) / 살을 거예요 (✕)
만들다 → 만들 거예요 (○)

Because ㄹ is already there, leave it as is.

When to use and when not to use

Use it — when the time is set, tickets are booked, or the plan is firm:
다음 주에 이사할 거예요.

Don’t use it — when nothing is decided yet. Use -고 싶어요 instead.

Related patterns — -(으)려고 해요 means “planning/thinking of”, and -기로 했어요 means “decided on”. All three look ahead, but with different levels of firmness.
