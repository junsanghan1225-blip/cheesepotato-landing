이유의 두 갈래: -아/어서 vs -(으)니까 · 5분

Why Social Greetings and Apologies Only Use -아/어서

In Korean, social conventions strictly require -아/어서 for greetings, apologies, and thanks:

- Apologies (죄송해요 / 미안해요): 늦어서 죄송합니다 (Sorry for being late). *Never* 늦으니까 죄송합니다!
- Thanks (고마워요 / 감사합니다): 도와주셔서 감사합니다 (Thank you for helping). *Never* 도와주시니까!
- Greetings (반가워요): 만나서 반가워요 (Nice to meet you). *Never* 만나니까 반가워요!

Use -(으)니까 when you give a reason to suggest an action, make a request, or give a command (e.g., "Since it's raining, let's take an umbrella").

Apology늦어서 죄송해요 · 늦으니까 죄송해요 (X) · Sorry for being late
Thanks도와줘서 고마워요 · 도와주니까 고마워요 (X) · Thanks for helping me
Greeting만나서 반가워요 · 만나니까 반가워요 (X) · Nice to meet you
Suggestion비 오니까 우산 써요 (O) · 비 와서 우산 써요 (X) · It’s raining, so use an umbrella
Rule of thumb: If you are saying thank you, sorry, or pleased to meet you, always reach for -아/어서 without hesitation!
약속 시간에 늦어서 정말 죄송합니다.I am truly sorry for being late to the appointment. (-어서 for apology)
도와주셔서 진심으로 감사드려요.Thank you sincerely for helping me. (-어서 for thanks)
지금 길이 막히니까 지하철을 탑시다.Since the roads are blocked right now, let’s take the subway. (-(으)니까 before suggestion)
