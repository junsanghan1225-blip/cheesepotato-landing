2강. (이)나: 선택과 수량 강조, 두 가지 다른 쓰임

남은 조사 ③ 나열과 선택 · 7분

The particle (이)나 has two very distinct lives depending on where it appears in a sentence:

1. Choice (“Or / Either... or”):
- Positioned between two nouns to suggest options (*“either A or B is fine”*):
- *“커피나 차 드세요.”* *(Please have coffee or tea.)*
- *“주말에는 영화나 독서를 해요.”* *(On weekends I watch movies or read.)*
- *“밥이나 빵을 먹을까요?”* *(Shall we eat rice or bread?)*

2. Quantity Emphasis (“As many as... / A whopping...!”):
- Attached to a counter or quantity number (*3 cups, 10 hours, 5 people*).
- Conveys surprise or amazement that the amount is greater than expected:
- *“커피를 세 잔이나 마셨어요!”* *(I drank as many as three whole cups of coffee!)*
- *“어제 10시간이나 잤어요.”* *(I slept a whopping 10 hours!)*

How to Distinguish at a Glance:

- Between two nouns? → Choice (“or”)
- Vowel ending: -나 (*커피나 차, 영화나 운동*)
- Batchim ending: -이나 (*밥이나 빵, 책이나 잡지*)

- Attached to a number / counter? → Quantity Emphasis (“as many as”)
- *3잔이나 (3 whole cups!), 5개나 (as many as 5!), 10명이나 (10 whole people!)*
Choice (선택)*or* · Between nouns (either option) · 물이나 주스 주세요. (Water or juice)
Quantity (수량 강조)*as many as* · After number / counter · 세 잔이나 마셨어요. (3 whole cups!)
Choice (선택)*or* · Between nouns (free choice) · 토요일이나 일요일 (Saturday or Sunday)
Quantity (수량 강조)*as long as* · After duration / time · 10시간이나 잤어요. (A whole 10 hours!)

어제 10시간이나 잤어요.I slept a whopping 10 hours! (Quantity: 이나)
