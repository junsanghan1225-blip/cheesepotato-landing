1강. -네요: 지금 막 느낀 것에 대한 감탄

반응 -네요 · -군요 · -지요 · 7분

Have you noticed Koreans saying 맛있네요!, 춥네요!, or 예쁘네요! in the moment?

-네요 is used when you directly experience or perceive something right now, and make an immediate, spontaneous exclamation!

- Taking a sip of hot coffee: *“이 커피 정말 맛있네요!”* *(Wow, this coffee is really delicious!)*
- Stepping outside into the winter chill: *“오늘 진짜 춥네요.”* *(Brr, it’s really cold today!)*
- Seeing a friend’s new outfit: *“옷이 정말 예쁘네요!”* *(Your outfit looks so lovely!)*

It shares your genuine, live feeling with the listener in a warm, polite tone.

Ultra-Simple Conjugation!

Unlike many endings you’ve learned, -네요 never changes based on batchim or vowel harmony! Simply take the stem and attach -네요:

- 가다 → 가네요 *(They are going!)*
- 먹다 → 먹네요 *(You eat so well!)*
- 크다 → 크네요 *(It’s so big!)*
- 작다 → 작네요 *(It’s quite small!)*
- Past tense: stem + -았/었네요 *(어제 비가 많이 왔네요 = It really rained a lot yesterday!)*
- Noun: Noun + -(이)네요 *(벌써 금요일이네요 = It’s already Friday!)*
Adjective맛있다 (delicious) · 정말 맛있네요! · Wow, it’s really delicious!
Adjective춥다 (cold, ㅂ irreg) · 오늘 진짜 춥네요. · Brr, it’s really cold today.
Verb잘하다 (to do well) · 한국어 정말 잘하시네요! · Wow, you speak Korean so well!
Past오다 (to come / rain) · 비가 많이 왔네요. · Oh, it rained a lot!
Noun금요일 (Friday) · 벌써 금요일이네요! · Oh, it’s already Friday!
이 커피 정말 맛있네요!Wow, this coffee is really delicious! (Direct taste)
오늘 바람이 많이 부네요.It’s so windy today. (Feeling the breeze outside)
벌써 주말이네요!It’s already the weekend! (Looking at calendar)
