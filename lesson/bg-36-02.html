2강. 언제·왜·어떻게: 때, 까닭, 방법 묻기

의문사 10개 · 7분

Now let’s explore the three words that ask about circumstances, reasons, and procedures:

1. 언제 (When):
- Inquires about time, dates, or days:
- *“생일이 언제예요?”* *(When is your birthday?)*
- *“언제 한국에 왔어요?”* *(When did you come to Korea?)*
- *(Notice: 언제 already holds temporal meaning, so you do NOT add the particle 에 to it — just say 언제, never 언제에!)*

2. 왜 (Why):
- Asks for reasons and motives:
- *“왜 한국어를 배워요?”* *(Why do you study Korean?)*
- Natural responses use reason connectors you already mastered: -아/어서, -(으)니까, or -기 때문에! (*“한국 노래가 좋아서 배워요.”*)

3. 어떻게 (How / In what way):
- Inquires about methods, means of transportation, or procedures:
- *“회사에 어떻게 가요?”* *(How do you go to work?)*
- Natural responses pair with the means particle (으)로 (learned in bg-31!): *“지하철로 가요.”* *(I go by subway.)*

Two Common Friendly Social Idioms with 어떻게

Beyond asking for physical directions or transportation, 어떻게 is constantly used in daily conversational greetings:
- *“요즘 어떻게 지내요?”* *(How have you been doing lately?)*
- *“이 문제에 대해 어떻게 생각해요?”* *(How/what do you think about this issue?)*
언제When · Time / Date (금요일에, 내일) · 생일이 언제예요? — 내일이에요.
왜Why · Reason (-아/어서, -(으)니까) · 왜 늦었어요? — 차가 막혀서 늦었어요.
어떻게How (Method) · Means ((으)로, 걸어서) · 학교에 어떻게 가요? — 지하철로 가요.

왜 한국어를 배워요?Why do you learn Korean? (왜 = why)
학교에 어떻게 가요?How do you go to school? (어떻게 = how)
