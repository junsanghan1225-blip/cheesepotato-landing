3강. 음료·책·나이: 잔, 병, 권, 살과 실전 종합

단위명사와 몇 · 8분

Let’s master the final set of high-frequency counters used constantly in cafes, bookstores, and self-introductions:

1. 잔 (Cups / Glasses):
- For coffee, tea, or water served in cups or glasses:
- *“아메리카노 두 잔 주세요.”* *(Two cups of Americano, please.)*
- *“물 한 잔 마셨어요.”* *(I drank a glass of water.)*

2. 병 (Bottles):
- For liquids in glass or plastic bottles:
- *“생수 한 병”* *(one bottle of spring water)*
- *“콜라 두 병”* *(two bottles of cola)*

3. 권 (Volumes / Books):
- For bound reading materials like books and notebooks:
- *“한국어 책 세 권”* *(three Korean books)*

4. 살 (Age in Years):
- Used with Native Korean numbers to state age (*스물다섯 살이에요*).

⚡ CRITICAL AGE EXCEPTION: 스물 → 스무 살!

When stating the age 20, the number 스물 drops its final ㄹ to become 스무:
- 스무 살 *(20 years old — NOT 스물 살!)*
- *“저는 올해 스무 살이에요.”* *(I am 20 years old this year.)*
- Question: *“몇 살이에요?”* *(How old are you?)*
