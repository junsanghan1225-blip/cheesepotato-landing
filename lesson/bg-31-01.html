1강. 에게 와 한테: 사람에게

남은 조사 ① 사람과 방향 · 7분

In course bg-29, you learned that when doing something for an honored elder (like parents or teachers), you use the honorific particle 께 (*선생님께 편지를 드렸어요*).

Now, what about friends, coworkers, siblings, and ordinary people?

You use 에게 or 한테 (*“to / towards someone”*)!

- Giving a gift: *“친구한테 선물을 줬어요.”* *(I gave a gift to my friend.)*
- Sending a message: *“동생에게 문자를 보냈어요.”* *(I sent a text to my younger sibling.)*

Both mean exactly the same thing grammatically. The only difference is style and register!

Written vs. Spoken Register:

- 에게: Written / Formal register (문어체). Common in literature, news articles, formal business emails, and official notices.
- 한테: Everyday Spoken register (구어체). What native speakers use 99% of the time in daily conversation with friends, family, and colleagues!

Both attach directly to the person noun without any batchim changes:
- 친구 + 한테 = 친구한테
- 동생 + 에게 = 동생에게
- 민수 + 한테 = 민수한테
Honored Elder (선생님)께 · Honorific (높임) · 선생님께 여쭤보세요.
Friend / Peer (문어체)에게 · Formal / Written · 친구에게 편지를 씁니다.
Friend / Peer (구어체)한테 · Spoken / Everyday · 친구한테 카톡 보냈어.
Younger (동생)한테 / 에게 · Neutral / Casual · 동생한테 용돈을 줬어요.
친구한테 선물을 줬어요.I gave a gift to a friend. (Spoken, natural)
동생에게 문자를 보냈어요.I sent a text to my sibling. (Written/formal)
