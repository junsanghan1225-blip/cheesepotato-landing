2강. 사람과 동물: 명/분, 마리

단위명사와 몇 · 7분

When counting living beings, Korean uses specialized counters instead of 개:

1. 명 (General People Counter):
- Used for friends, students, coworkers, family, and people in general:
- *“학생 세 명”* *(three students)*
- *“친구 네 명”* *(four friends)*
- *“몇 명이에요?”* *(How many people are there?)*

2. 분 (Honorific People Counter):
- The polite/honorific version of 명! Used for honored elders, teachers, and guests/customers:
- *“선생님 한 분”* *(one teacher)*
- *“손님 두 분”* *(two valued guests)*
- *“몇 분 오세요?”* *(How many esteemed guests are coming?)*

3. 마리 (Animal Counter):
- Used for all animals, pets, birds, and fish:
- *“강아지 한 마리”* *(one puppy)*
- *“고양이 두 마리”* *(two cats)*

Restaurant Manners: 몇 분 vs. 몇 명

When you enter a restaurant in Korea, the host will greet you respectfully:
- Host: *“어서 오세요! 몇 분이세요?”* *(Welcome! How many in your party?)*

When answering for yourself and your group, use the humble/neutral 명 (you never honor yourself with 분!):
- You: *“두 명이에요.”* *(Two people, please.)*
- Host to coworkers: *“손님 두 분 들어오십니다!”* *(Two guests entering!)*

Honored People분 · Elders, teachers, customers · 손님 두 분 (two guests)
Animals / Pets마리 · Dogs, cats, birds, fish · 강아지 한 마리 (one puppy)
