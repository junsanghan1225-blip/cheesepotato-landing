Lesson 1. 있어요 / 없어요

There Is, There Isn’t — 있어요 / 없어요 · 6분

있어요 = it exists / there is / I have.
없어요 = it does not exist / there is none / I don’t have.

One pair of words, three English translations. Korean does not separate *having* from *existing*.

시간 있어요?Do you have time? — literally “time exists?”
돈이 없어요.I have no money.
화장실이 어디에 있어요?Where is the bathroom?
없어요 is not 안 + 있어요. Korean has a separate word for the negative here, and 안 있어요 is simply not said. Learn the pair together as one item.

The thing that exists takes 이/가

돈이 없어요. · 시간이 있어요.

Same consonant/vowel split you already know.
