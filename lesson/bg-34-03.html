3강. 꼴 만들기: -기 와 -(으)ㅁ 붙이기

명사로 만들기 -기 · -음/ㅁ · 5분

How to Build Nominal Forms: -기 vs -(으)ㅁ

Korean turns verbs and adjectives into nouns using two main endings:

1. -기 (Simple & Direct): Attach -기 directly to the verb stem without changing anything, whether there is a final consonant or not!
- 읽다 → 읽기 *(reading)*
- 쓰다 → 쓰기 *(writing)*
- 듣다 → 듣기 *(listening)*

2. -(으)ㅁ (Creates Concrete & Established Nouns):
- Vowel ending: attach -ㅁ (자다 → 잠 *sleep*, 꿈을 꾸다 → 꿈 *dream*)
- Consonant ending: attach -음 (믿다 → 믿음 *faith/trust*, 웃다 → 웃음 *laughter*)
- ㄹ batchim: attach -ㅁ under ㄹ (살다 → 삶 *life*)
- ㅂ irregular: 돕다 → 도움 *help*

자다 (to sleep)Vowel ending · 자기 · 잠 (sleep)
믿다 (to trust)Consonant ending · 믿기 · 믿음 (trust/faith)
살다 (to live)ㄹ batchim · 살기 · 삶 (life)
돕다 (to help)ㅂ irregular · 돕기 · 도움 (help)
한국어 시험에서 듣기와 읽기를 연습해요.I practice listening and reading for the Korean exam. (듣기, 읽기 with -기)
어려울 때 친구의 도움으로 힘을 냈어요.In tough times, I gained strength from a friend's help. (도움 from 돕다)
아이의 밝은 웃음이 온 집안을 채웠어요.The child's bright laughter filled the whole house. (웃음 from 웃다)
