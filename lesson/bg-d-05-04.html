4강. 존댓말 종합 실전

초급 세밀: 존댓말 마무리 — 명령·청유·겸양 · 5분

Everyday conversation, right now-(으)세요 · 가세요
Everyday conversation, already happened-(으)셨어요 · 가셨어요
A formal setting, right now-(으)십니다 · 가십니다
A formal command or instruction-(으)십시오 · 가십시오
A formal proposal to a group-(으)십시다 · 가십시다
Offering casually-(으)실래요? · 가실래요?
Offering politely-(으)시겠어요? · 가시겠어요?
Lowering yourself to honor someone elsehumble expressions · 드리다·여쭙다·뵙다·모시다

Four questions are all you need

① Who is this about? — If the subject is someone senior, -(으)시- goes in.

② When? — Right now, keep it as is; already happened, add -셨-.

③ How formal is the setting? — Everyday conversation: -어요. An announcement or service setting: -ㅂ니다/-십시오.

④ What am I doing for someone senior? — Giving, asking, or meeting them calls for a humble expression (드리다, 여쭙다, 뵙다).

할머니, 제가 짐을 들어 드릴게요.humble (드리다) — what I’m doing is for Grandma
손님 여러분, 곧 문이 닫힙니다. 안전선 밖으로 나와 주십시오.a formal instruction (-십시오)
자, 다 같이 시작해 보십시다.a proposal (-십시다)
Subject honorification and humble expressions can appear in the same sentence. If the subject is someone senior, use -시-. If I’m doing something for someone senior, use a humble word.

제가 할머니를 모시고 병원에 가요. (the humble word 모시다 — I don’t also add -시-)

할머니께서 병원에 가세요. (the subject is 할머니 — here it does take -시-)
