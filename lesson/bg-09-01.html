Lesson 1. 안 — I don’t

Saying No — 안 and 못 · 5분

Put 안 directly in front of the verb.

저는 커피를 안 마셔요. — I don’t drink coffee.

하다 verbs split. 공부하다 does not become 안 공부해요 — the 안 goes inside, right before 하다:

공부를 안 해요 or 공부 안 해요 (○)
안 공부해요 (✗)

This catches everyone once. After that it feels natural, because 공부 is really a noun.
저는 술을 안 마셔요.I don’t drink alcohol.
오늘은 학교에 안 가요.I’m not going to school today.
주말에는 일 안 해요.I don’t work on weekends. Note the split in 일하다.

There is a longer form too

-지 않아요 means the same thing and sounds a little more formal or written.

안 마셔요 = 마시지 않아요

Learn to recognise it now; 안 is what you will actually say.
