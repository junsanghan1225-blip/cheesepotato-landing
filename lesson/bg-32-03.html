3강. 만 과 밖에: "오직" 두 가지 방법

남은 조사 ② 견주기와 범위 · 8분

Both 만 and 밖에 translate to *“only / just / nothing but”* in English, but they are the single biggest grammatical trap for Korean learners!

Master this one core rule, and you will never make this mistake again:

1. 만 (Neutral “Only / Just”) — AFFIRMATIVE & NEGATIVE:
- Works freely anywhere! You can use it with both positive and negative verbs:
- *“물만 마셔요.”* *(I only drink water. — Affirmative)*
- *“사과만 안 먹었어요.”* *(Only the apple was not eaten. — Negative)*
- *“지갑에 천 원만 있어요.”* *(I have only 1,000 won.)*

2. 밖에 (Restricted “Nothing but / Only”) — STRICTLY NEGATIVE ONLY!:
- 밖에 literally means “outside of / other than”.
- Therefore, it MUST ALWAYS be followed by a negative predicate (없다, 안, 못, 모르다)!
- *“지갑에 천 원밖에 없어요.”* *(Outside of 1,000 won, I have nothing = I only have 1,000 won.)*
- *“사과밖에 안 먹었어요.”* *(I ate nothing except apples.)*
- *“한국어는 인사말밖에 몰라요.”* *(I know nothing of Korean other than greetings.)*

🚨 FATAL GRAMMAR ERROR: Never use 밖에 with an affirmative verb!

- ❌ *천 원밖에 있어요.* (IMPOSSIBLE IN KOREAN!)
- ❌ *사과밖에 먹었어요.* (IMPOSSIBLE IN KOREAN!)

If the ending verb is affirmative (*있어요, 먹었어요, 알아요*), you MUST USE 만:
- *천 원만 있어요.* (O)
- *사과만 먹었어요.* (O)

If the ending verb is negative (*없어요, 안 먹었어요, 몰라요*), you can use 밖에 to emphasize scarcity:
- *천 원밖에 없어요.* (O)
Noun + 만Affirmative (있어요) · 사과만 있어요. · ✅ Natural & Correct
Noun + 만Negative (없어요) · 사과만 없어요. · ✅ Natural & Correct
Noun + 밖에Negative (없어요) · 사과밖에 없어요. · ✅ Natural & Correct *(Only apples left)*
Noun + 밖에Affirmative (있어요) · 사과밖에 있어요. · ❌ FORBIDDEN GRAMMAR ERROR
지갑에 천 원밖에 없어요.I have only 1,000 won left. (밖에 + 없어요: Negative)
시간이 5분만 있어요.I have only 5 minutes. (만 + 있어요: Affirmative)
물밖에 안 마셨어요.I drank nothing but water. (밖에 + 안 마셨어요: Negative)
