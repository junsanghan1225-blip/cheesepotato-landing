1. 개요

everykoreans(이하 "개발자")은 치즈감자 앱(이하 "앱")과 웹사이트 everykoreans.com(이하 "웹")을 이용하는 분의 개인정보를 소중히 다루며, 아래와 같이 처리합니다.

앱과 웹은 같은 계정을 씁니다. 앱에서 만든 계정으로 웹에 로그인할 수 있고, 그 반대도 됩니다. 단어장도 같은 것을 봅니다.

앱과 웹 모두 광고 및 분석 도구를 일절 사용하지 않습니다. 이용자를 추적하거나 행동 데이터를 수집하지 않습니다.

English

1. Overview

everykoreans ("we") operates the CheesePotato app ("the app") and the website everykoreans.com ("the site"). This policy explains what we collect and how we handle it.

The app and the site share one account. You can sign in to the site with the account you made in the app and vice versa, and both show the same wordbook.

Neither contains advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not track users or collect behavioral data.

2. What we collect

Account — email address and password (stored encrypted; we cannot read it). If you sign in with Google, we receive your email, name, and profile picture from Google. We never receive your Google password.

Your content — words and meanings, photos you take or choose, difficulty and memorization status, view counts, and study settings (wordbook name, languages, daily goal, study period, start date), profile name and picture.

Word lists you share on the site (⚠️ these become public) — if you upload a word list to the site's Library, the following becomes visible to anyone, including people who are not signed in:

ItemDetail
Words and meaningsExtracted from the file you uploaded
Title, description, categoryEntered by you
Display nameOnly the part of your email before @ (e.g. hong@gmail.com → hong)
Upload date, download countRecorded automatically

We do not keep the file you upload. Your browser reads out the words and meanings, and the original file is discarded on the spot. Your full email address is never shown.

If you would rather not publish, simply do not upload. You can delete anything you shared at any time — though files other people already downloaded cannot be recalled. We remove inappropriate submissions.

Stored only on your device — theme preference, app language, profile name and photo path. These are never sent to our servers.

We do not collect location, contacts, call logs, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics.

3. How we use it

To sign you in, store and display your wordbook, track your study progress, and — for photos only — to recognize objects and generate vocabulary.

4. Third-party processors

ProcessorData sharedPurpose
Supabase Inc.All app dataHosting, database, file storage
Google LLC (Gemini API)Photos you takeObject recognition for word generation

About photos: photos taken with the camera feature are sent to Google's Gemini API for object recognition. If you do not use the camera feature, no photo is ever sent. You can use the app fully by typing words yourself.

  • Supabase: https://supabase.com/privacy
  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your data until you delete your account. Settings → Delete account permanently removes your account, words, and photos immediately — including anything you shared in the site's Library. Deleted data cannot be recovered, and files other people already downloaded cannot be recalled.

6. Your rights

You can view and edit your data in the app, delete individual words, delete all words, or delete your entire account — all directly in the app. The site shows the same wordbook read-only, and lets you delete word lists you shared.

Found something inappropriate in the Library? Use the report button on that entry and we will review and remove it.

For anything else, contact junsanghan1225@gmail.com.

7. Permissions

Camera and Notifications are both optional. Denying them still lets you use every study feature by entering words manually.

We do not request photo library access. Picking an image uses the Android system photo picker, which hands the app only the single photo you chose. The app cannot read your photo library.

You can change permissions in your device settings at any time.

8. Security

All traffic is encrypted over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed one-way. The database enforces row-level security so only you can reach your own rows. Photos live in a private bucket and are served through short-lived signed URLs.

The Library never stores uploaded files. The browser reads out only the words and meanings and discards the original, so nothing else that a spreadsheet might carry ever reaches our servers.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 14. If we learn that a child under 14 has registered without parental consent, we will delete the account.

10. Changes

We will announce changes through the app store release notes and this page.
