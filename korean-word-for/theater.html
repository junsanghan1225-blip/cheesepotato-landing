How to say “theater” in Korean

The most common Korean word for “theater” is 극장 (geukjang). 「theater」는 한국어로 극장.

1극장 geukjang

theater; cinema

TOPIK 1 · noun

친구와 영화를 보러 극장에 가요.I go to the cinema with a friend to watch a movie.
