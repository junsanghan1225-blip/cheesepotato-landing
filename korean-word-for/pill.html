How to say “pill” in Korean

The most common Korean word for “pill” is 알약 (aryak). 「pill」는 한국어로 알약.

1알약 aryak

pill; tablet

TOPIK 2 · noun

물과 함께 알약을 삼켜요.I swallow the pill with water.
