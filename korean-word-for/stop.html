How to say “stop” in Korean

The most common Korean word for “stop” is 말다 (malda). There are 4 more words with a similar meaning — compare them below. 「stop」는 한국어로 말다.

1말다 malda

to stop; do not

TOPIK 1 · verb

너무 걱정하지 말고 편안한 마음으로 쉬어요.Do not worry too much and rest with a comfortable mind.
2정류장 jeongryujang

(bus) stop

TOPIK 1 · noun

집 앞 버스 정류장에서 친구를 기다려요.I wait for my friend at the bus stop in front of my house.
3그만 geuman

stop; no more

TOPIK 2 · adverb

시간이 늦었으니 그만 자요.It is late, so stop and go to sleep.
4그치다 geuchida

to stop; to cease

TOPIK 2 · verb

오후가 되자 비가 그쳤어요.As afternoon came, the rain stopped.
5멈추다 meomchuda

stop doing; quit

TOPIK 2 · verb

빨간 불에는 차를 멈추어야 해요.You must stop the car on the red light.
