How to use

  • CSV opens in Excel, Google Sheets and Numbers (UTF-8, so Korean letters display correctly). Columns: Korean, part of speech, English, Vietnamese, Japanese, example (Korean and English), level.
  • Anki: File → Import → choose the .txt file. Front is the Korean word; back is the meanings and the example. Column 3 becomes a tag (the level). Make sure “Allow HTML in fields” is on if Anki asks.
  • Prefer to study online? Browse the same words by topic: TOPIK I words · TOPIK II words · dictionary.

About this list

Levels follow the National Institute of Korean Language’s International Standard Curriculum for Korean Language Education (국립국어원 「국제 통용 한국어 표준 교육과정」, Korea Open Government License Type 1). Korean headwords and parts of speech draw on the National Institute of Korean Language’s Korean Learners’ Dictionary (한국어기초사전, CC BY-SA 2.0 KR). English, Vietnamese and Japanese meanings and example sentences in the study lists were written by CheesePotato. The list is a study aid and is not an official TOPIK word list. Free for personal and classroom use — please keep this credit if you share it.
