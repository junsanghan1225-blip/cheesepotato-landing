Which age do Koreans use?

Since 28 June 2023, Korean law and government documents use the international age (만 나이) — the same age as in most countries. You will still hear the other two:

  • 연 나이 (year age) — this year minus your birth year. A few laws still use it, such as the rules on buying alcohol and tobacco and on military service.
  • 세는 나이 (counting age) — the traditional way: everyone is 1 at birth and gets a year older on New Year's Day. Older people may still ask it this way.

How to say your age

In conversation Koreans count age with native Korean numbers and 살: 스물다섯 살 (25). In formal writing they use Sino-Korean numbers and 세: 25세 (이십오 세).

한국어

2023년 6월 28일부터 법과 행정에서는 만 나이를 씁니다. 말할 때는 「스물다섯 살」처럼 고유어 수와 「살」을, 글에서는 「25세」를 씁니다. 띠는 태어난 해로 정하지만, 음력 설 전에 태어났으면 앞 해의 띠일 수 있어요.
