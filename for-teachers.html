What you get

  • Homework in two taps — a course lesson, a word session (how many times), a grammar drill. Weekly repeats and homework bundles.
  • Set each student's level — no level test needed; the student's lessons start where you put them.
  • Progress report — students × homework table, CSV export, a printable report for each student.
  • "Students to check today" — overdue, inactive for 3 days, or just finished.
  • Comments on finished homework, and a private note for each student.
  • Join by link or QR — students sign in and they are in your class.

What stays yours

Your lessons, your schedule and your payments stay on your own platform. The class tool is only for homework and review — inside your class, students do not see our own lesson booking links.

How to start

  1. Email junsanghan1225@gmail.com with the email you use to sign in.
  2. We open a teacher account for you and reply by email.
  3. Create a class, share the link or QR, and give the first homework.
