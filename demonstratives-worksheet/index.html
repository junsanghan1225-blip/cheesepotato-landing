Korean Demonstratives: 이 · 그 · 저 · 지시어: 이 · 그 · 저

This, that and that over there: modifiers, places (여기, 거기, 저기) and things (이것, 그것, 저것). 이·그·저의 거리 기준, 장소 지시(여기·거기·저기), 사물 지시(이것·그것·저것)

1 · The Three Distances of 이, 그, 저 · 거리 기준에 따른 지시어
CategoryNear Speaker (This)Near Listener (That)Far from Both (That over there)
Modifier (before noun)이 (이 사람 = this person)그 (그 사람 = that person)저 (저 사람 = that person over there)
Thing / Object이것 (this thing)그것 (that thing)저것 (that thing over there)
Place / Location여기 (here)거기 (there)저기 (over there)
Direction이쪽 (this way)그쪽 (that way)저쪽 (that way over there)

이 refers to what is close to the speaker. 그 refers to what is close to the listener (or already mentioned in conversation). 저 refers to what is visible but distant from both speaker and listener.

2 · Spoken Contractions of 이것, 그것, 저것 · 사물 지시 대명사의 줄임말
Full formSubject (+ 이)Topic (+ 은)Object (+ 을)
이것 (this thing)이게 (이것이)이건 (이것은)이걸 (이것을)
그것 (that thing)그게 (그것이)그건 (그것은)그걸 (그것을)
저것 (that thing over there)저게 (저것이)저건 (저것은)저걸 (저것을)

In natural spoken Korean, native speakers almost always use the short forms: 이게 뭐예요? (What is this?), 이건 제 거예요 (This is mine), 이걸로 주세요 (Give me this one).

3 · Using 이, 그, 저 with Nouns · 명사를 꾸미는 이, 그, 저

Place 이, 그, or 저 directly before any noun without a particle: 이 책 (this book), 그 영화 (that movie you mentioned), 저 건물 (that building over there). To ask 'which', use 어느: 어느 나라 사람이에요? (Which country are you from?).

4 · Practice A — Choose 이, 그, or 저 based on distance · 거리 기준에 맞게 이, 그, 저 중 하나를 골라요
  1. 1. Object in the speaker's hand: 책 (this book)
  2. 2. Object right next to the listener: 가방 (that bag near you)
  3. 3. Distant mountain visible to both: 산 (that mountain over there)
  4. 4. Building across the street: 건물 (that building over there)
  5. 5. Food the speaker is currently eating: 음식 (this food)
5 · Practice B — Choose place words: 여기, 거기, 저기 · 여기, 거기, 저기 중 알맞은 장소를 골라요
  1. 1. Right where the speaker stands: 에 앉으세요 (Sit here)
  2. 2. Where the listener is: 는 날씨가 어때요? (How is the weather there?)
  3. 3. Far spot across the road: 에 편의점이 있어요 (Over there)
  4. 4. Getting the attention of someone far away: 요! (Excuse me!)
  5. 5. Come toward the speaker: 로 오세요 (Come this way)
6 · Practice C — Choose the spoken contraction · 알맞은 줄임말을 골라요
  1. 1. What is this? (subject): 뭐예요? (이게 / 이건)
  2. 2. As for this, it is my notebook (topic): 제 공책이에요 (이게 / 이건)
  3. 3. Please give me this (object): 주세요 (이걸 / 이게)
  4. 4. That is a great idea! (subject): 좋은 생각이에요! (그게 / 그걸)
  5. 5. Look at that over there! (object): 보세요 (저걸 / 저게)
7 · Practice D — Complete the demonstrative sentence · 문맥에 맞는 지시어를 넣어요
  1. 1. Who is that person over there?: 사람은 누구예요? (이 / 그 / 저)
  2. 2. How much is this pencil in my hand?: 연필 얼마예요? (이 / 그 / 저)
  3. 3. Did you like the movie you watched?: 영화 재미있었어요? (이 / 그 / 저)
  4. 4. The restroom is over there: 화장실은 에 있어요 (여기 / 거기 / 저기)
  5. 5. Please show me that one near you: 좀 보여 주세요 (이것 / 그것)

How to use it

  • Master the 3 distance zones: 이 (close to speaker), 그 (close to listener / previously mentioned), and 저 (distant from both).
  • Familiarize yourself with the spoken short forms: 이게 (이것이), 이건 (이것은), and 이걸 (이것을).
  • Complete Practice A to D and check your answers with the answer key.
