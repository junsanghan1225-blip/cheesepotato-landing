Korean words: Directions 🇰🇷
길 · 방향 — 한국어 단어 5개

1. 걸어가다 (georeogada) — to walk
2. 뛰어가다 (ttwieogada) — to run (there) / dash
3. 들르다 (deulleuda) — to drop by
4. 올라가다 (ollagada) — to go up / ascend
5. 돌아가다 (doragada) — to return / go back
