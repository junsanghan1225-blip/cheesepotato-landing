Korean words: Appearance 🇰🇷
외모 — 한국어 단어 5개

1. 멋있다 (meositda) — cool / stylish
2. 귀엽다 (gwiyeopda) — cute / lovely
3. 닮다 (damda) — to resemble / look like
4. 모습 (moseup) — appearance / figure
5. 멋 (meot) — stylishness
