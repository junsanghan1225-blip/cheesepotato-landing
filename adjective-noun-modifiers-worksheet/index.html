Noun-Modifying Adjectives: -(으)ㄴ · 형용사로 명사 꾸미기

How to turn Korean descriptive verbs into adjectives that modify nouns (e.g. 예쁜 꽃, 맛있는 음식). 형용사 어간에 -(으)ㄴ을 붙여 명사를 꾸미는 방법

1 · Regular Adjective Modifying Pattern: -(으)ㄴ · 기본 형용사 명사 수식형
Dictionary formStem ends inModifying formExample phrase with noun
예쁘다 (pretty)Vowel (no 받침)예쁜예쁜 꽃 (a pretty flower)
크다 (big)Vowel (no 받침)큰큰 가방 (a big bag)
비싸다 (expensive)Vowel (no 받침)비싼비싼 시계 (an expensive watch)
작다 (small)Consonant (받침 O)작은작은 방 (a small room)
좋다 (good)Consonant (받침 O)좋은좋은 친구 (a good friend)
많다 (many)Consonant (받침 O)많은많은 사람 (many people)
높다 (high)Consonant (받침 O)높은높은 산 (a high mountain)
넓다 (spacious)Consonant (받침 O)넓은넓은 집 (a spacious house)

Vowel stems take ㄴ at the bottom (크다 → 큰). Consonant stems attach 은 (작다 → 작은). Sound changes: 좋은 is pronounced [조은] (silent ㅎ); 넓은 is pronounced [널븐] (liaison).

2 · Special & Irregular Adjective Modifiers · 특수 및 불규칙 형용사 수식형
Base adjectivePattern / CategoryModifying formExample phrase
맛있다 (delicious)-있다 family (always -는!)맛있는맛있는 음식 (delicious food)
재미있다 (fun / exciting)-있다 family (always -는!)재미있는재미있는 영화 (a fun movie)
맛없다 (unpalatable)-없다 family (always -는!)맛없는맛없는 음식 (bad-tasting food)
더운 (hot weather)ㅂ irregular (ㅂ → 우 + ㄴ)더운더운 날씨 (hot weather)
추운 (cold weather)ㅂ irregular (ㅂ → 우 + ㄴ)추운추운 겨울 (cold winter)
어렵다 (difficult)ㅂ irregular (ㅂ → 우 + ㄴ)어려운어려운 시험 (a difficult exam)
쉽다 (easy)ㅂ irregular (ㅂ → 우 + ㄴ)쉬운쉬운 문제 (an easy problem)
길다 (long)ㄹ irregular (ㄹ drops + ㄴ)긴긴 머리 (long hair)
빨갛다 (red)ㅎ irregular (ㅎ drops + ㄴ)빨간빨간 사과 (a red apple)

Key rules: 1. Adjectives ending in -있다 / -없다 always take -는 (맛있는, 재미있는). 2. ㅂ irregular stems turn ㅂ into 우, yielding -운 (덥다 → 더운, 춥다 → 추운).

3 · Sentence Predicate vs Noun Modifier · 서술형 문장과 명사 수식형 비교
ConceptPredicate at end (-아요/어요)Modifier before noun (-(으)ㄴ)English comparison
Pretty꽃이 예뻐요.예쁜 꽃을 샀어요."The flower is pretty." vs "I bought a pretty flower."
Cold날씨가 추워요.추운 날씨를 싫어해요."The weather is cold." vs "I dislike cold weather."
Delicious음식이 맛있어요.맛있는 음식을 먹어요."The food is delicious." vs "I eat delicious food."
Big가방이 커요.큰 가방이 필요해요."The bag is big." vs "I need a big bag."

Modifiers sit directly in front of the noun they describe without any intervening particles.

How to use it

  • Review the basic rule: add ㄴ to vowel stems (예쁜, 큰) and 은 to consonant stems (작은, 좋은).
  • Remember that adjectives ending in -있다 and -없다 take -는 (맛있는, 재미있는), and ㅂ irregular stems become -운 (더운, 추운).
  • Complete Practice A to C and check your answers with the answer key.
