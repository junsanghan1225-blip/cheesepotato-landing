1 · Essential Greetings · 기본 인사말
KoreanEnglishSituation / UsageReading tip
안녕하세요Hello / Good morningEveryday polite greeting—
반갑습니다Nice to meet youFirst meeting or introduction반갑씀니다
안녕히 계세요Goodbye (stay well)Said by the person leaving—
안녕히 가세요Goodbye (go well)Said to the person leaving—
감사합니다Thank youFormal / polite appreciation감사함니다
죄송합니다I am sorryPolite apology죄송함니다
괜찮아요It is okay / No worriesPolite response to apology괜차나요

Remember: 안녕히 계세요 is said to someone staying. 안녕히 가세요 is said to someone leaving.

How to use it

  • Learn the difference between 안녕히 계세요 (to someone staying) and 안녕히 가세요 (to someone leaving).
  • Practice introducing yourself using '저는 [이름]이에요/예요' and '저는 [국적] 사람이에요'.
  • Solve Practice A to D and check your answers with the answer key.
