Korean words: Directions 🇰🇷
길 · 방향 — 한국어 단어 5개

1. 멈추다 (meomchuda) — to stop
2. 나타나다 (natanada) — to appear / show up
3. 걸어오다 (georeooda) — to walk here / come on foot
4. 세우다 (seuda) — to stand / establish / stop
5. 길 (gil) — road / way
