FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know

What is Every Koreans?

Every Koreans is a 100% free Korean learning website covering everything from the Hangul alphabet to TOPIK level 6. It features 21 courses, 82 lessons, 290 grammar points, 480 TOPIK questions, 54 reading passages, and a 5,346-word dictionary. It runs directly in your browser with zero installation.

Is it really free? Do I need to register?

Yes, it is completely free and you can start immediately without signing up. Courses, TOPIK drills, reading exercises, and the dictionary are accessible right away. Creating an account is only needed if you want to sync your study progress across devices or record full mock exams.

I am a complete beginner. Where should I start?

Start with 'Learn by Course' in the Beginner section. The first course teaches the Hangul alphabet step-by-step with authentic audio for every character. You do not need to know any Korean beforehand.

What is the difference between TOPIK I and TOPIK II?

TOPIK I tests levels 1-2 and consists of 30 Listening and 40 Reading questions. TOPIK II tests levels 3-6 and consists of 50 Listening, 50 Reading, and 4 Writing tasks. TOPIK I has no writing section. On Every Koreans, questions are separated accordingly.

How does TOPIK listening practice work?

You listen to the audio track and choose from four options. The transcript only opens after you submit your answer, ensuring realistic listening training. Once submitted, a detailed speaker-tagged transcript appears explaining what you heard.

Are these official past exam questions?

No. All questions on Every Koreans are original questions created in accordance with the official TOPIK format, length, and difficulty. They are not past papers and are not affiliated with NIIED.

Where do the word definitions come from?

Our 5,346 vocabulary definitions are sourced from the National Institute of Korean Language Basic Korean Dictionary under CC BY-SA 2.0 KR. The 197 grammar entries are written by Every Koreans.

Does it work on smartphones?

Yes! Because most learners study on mobile devices, the platform is designed mobile-first. A companion Android wordbook app is also available on Google Play.
