뻔히

ppeonhi

clearly; obviously; visibly

예문 Examples

속셈이 뻔히 보여서 부탁을 거절했어요.The ulterior motive was obvious, so I refused the request.
위험한 줄 뻔히 알면서도 그 길을 선택했다.Even though knowing clearly that it was dangerous, he chose that path.
