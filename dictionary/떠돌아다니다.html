떠돌아다니다

tteodoradanida · to wander around

뜻 · Meaning

Englishto wander around; to roam; to driftwander around / roam
로마자 · Romanizationtteodoradanida

예문 · Examples

정처 없이 낯선 거리를 혼자 떠돌아다녀요.I wander around unfamiliar streets alone without an aim.
그는 젊은 시절 일자리를 찾아 전국 각지를 떠돌아다녔다.In his youth, he roamed around all parts of the country in search of jobs.
