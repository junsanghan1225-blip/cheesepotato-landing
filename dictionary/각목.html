gangmok

Pronounced [강목] — Nasalization · 비음화

Why does it sound different?
  • Nasalization (비음화) — ㄱ ㄷ ㅂ before ㄴ ㅁ turn into ㅇ ㄴ ㅁ (and ㄹ after ㅁ ㅇ ㄱ ㅂ becomes ㄴ).

Standard pronunciation of the word on its own. Sounds can shift a little inside a sentence.
