1 · Restaurant Ordering Phrases · 식당 필수 주문 표현
KoreanEnglishUsage / ContextReading tip
여기요 / 저기요Excuse me!Calling the server to your table—
메뉴판 좀 주세요Please give me the menuAsking for the menu—
물 좀 주세요Please give me waterAsking for drinking water—
비빔밥 하나 주세요One bibimbap, pleaseOrdering a single dish비빔밥 [비빔빱]
삼겹살 2인분 주세요Two servings of pork bellyOrdering meat portions삼겹살 [삼겹쌀] · 이 인분
얼마예요?How much is it?Asking for the price—
계산해 주세요Check, pleaseAsking to pay the bill—

Standard pattern: [Food name] + [Quantity] + 주세요. For example: 비빔밥 하나 주세요 (One bibimbap, please).

BBQ and meat portions always use Sino-Korean numbers + 인분: 1인분 (일 인분), 2인분 (이 인분), 3인분 (삼 인분).

3 · Taste Adjectives · 맛을 나타내는 형용사
TasteBasic formPolite present (해요체)Example / Note
Delicious맛있다맛있어요Opposite: 맛없다 (맛없어요)
Spicy맵다매워요ㅂ irregular: 떡볶이가 매워요
Sweet달다달아요사탕이 달아요
Salty짜다짜요Opposite: 싱겁다 (싱거워요)
Bland / Not salty싱겁다싱거워요ㅂ irregular: 국이 싱거워요
Sour시다셔요레몬이 셔요
Bitter쓰다써요약이 써요

Notice the ㅂ irregular conjugations: 맵다 becomes 매워요, and 싱겁다 becomes 싱거워요.

6 · Practice C — Choose the taste matching the food · 음식에 어울리는 맛을 골라요
  1. 1. 사탕 (candy)은 (달아요 / 매워요)

  3. 3. 소금 (salt)은 (짜요 / 달아요)
  4. 4. 레몬 (lemon)은 (셔요 / 써요)
  5. 5. 커피 (black coffee)는 (써요 / 달아요)

How to use it

  • Learn key restaurant phrases: 여기요 (excuse me), 메뉴판 좀 주세요 (menu please), 계산해 주세요 (check please).
  • Remember that barbecue portions take 인분 (1인분, 2인분) and single dishes use 하나, 둘.
  • Practice taste adjectives in polite present forms (매워요, 달아요, 짜요, 싱거워요) in Practice A–D.
