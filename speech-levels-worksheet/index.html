Speech Levels: 해요체, 합쇼체 & 반말 · 해요체 · 합쇼체 · 반말 — 말투 바꾸기

Polite informal (해요체), formal polite (합쇼체), and intimate casual (반말). 상황에 따른 세 가지 말투 비교, 어미 변화 규칙과 전환 연습

1 · The Three Core Speech Levels · 세 가지 말투와 사용 상황
Speech levelKorean nameTypical situation / AudienceEnding form
Polite Informal해요체Daily conversations with coworkers, acquaintances, elders-아/어요
Formal Polite합쇼체Presentations, news broadcasts, business, formal service-ㅂ/습니다
Casual / Intimate반말 (해체)Close friends of same age, younger siblings, children-아/어

Foreign learners should primarily use 해요체. Switching to casual 반말 requires mutual agreement or same-age close friendship.

2 · Conjugation Comparison Across Speech Levels · 말투별 어미 비교
Base wordMeaning해요체 (Polite informal)합쇼체 (Formal polite)반말 (Casual intimate)
가다To go가요갑니다가
먹다To eat먹어요먹습니다먹어
보다To see / watch봐요봅니다봐
하다To do해요합니다해
있다To exist / have있어요있습니다있어
크다To be big커요큽니다커
좋다To be good좋아요좋습니다좋아

Quick conversion rule: remove the polite ending marker 요 from 해요체 to make casual 반말 (가요 → 가, 먹어요 → 먹어, 좋아요 → 좋아).

3 · Pronouns Change With Speech Level · 말투에 따른 인칭 대명사

In polite speech (해요체 and 합쇼체), use humble pronouns for yourself: 저 (I) and 저희 (we). In casual speech (반말), switch to informal pronouns: 나 (I), 우리 (we), and 너 (you). Never call an adult or elder 너.

How to use it

  • Learn the distinctive endings of the 3 speech levels: -아/어요 (해요체), -ㅂ/습니다 (합쇼체), and -아/어 (반말).
  • Notice how dropping the final 요 from 해요체 produces natural casual 반말.
  • Solve Practice A to D and check your answers with the answer key.
