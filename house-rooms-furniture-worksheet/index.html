1 · Types of Housing & Rooms · 집 종류와 방 이름
Area / RoomKoreanSpoken readingMeaning & Note
House / Home집 / Home or house in general
Apartment아파트 / Apartment flat in a building

Pronunciation highlights: 부엌 is pronounced [부억] with representative consonant ㄱ; 원룸 is pronounced [월룸] through liquidization.

Sound changes: 책상 [책쌍], 옷장 [옫짱], 세탁기 [세탁끼] undergo tensification (경음화) after stop consonants.

3 · Describing Where Things Are · 집안 위치 표현
PatternKorean exampleSpoken readingMeaning
In the living room거실에 소파가 있어요.거시레There is a sofa in the living room.
On the desk책상 위에 책이 있어요.책쌍 위에 채기There is a book on the desk.
Inside the fridge냉장고 안에 물이 있어요.아네 무리There is water inside the fridge.
Next to the bed침대 옆에 옷장이 있어요.여페 옫짱이There is a closet next to the bed.

Position words follow the noun: 책상 위 (on top of desk), 냉장고 안 (inside fridge), 침대 옆 (next to bed).
