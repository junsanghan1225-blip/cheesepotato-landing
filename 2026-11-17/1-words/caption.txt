Korean words: Working life 🇰🇷
직장생활 — 한국어 단어 5개

1. 일하다 (ilhada) — to work
2. 퇴근하다 (toegeunhada) — to leave work / get off
3. 능력 (neungryeok) — ability / capability
4. 실패 (silpae) — failure
5. 성공 (seonggong) — success
