About this book · 책 소개

Book 3 of the series: 940 level-3 words that are not in Books 1 and 2, 20 per unit in 47 units. Each unit has 20 words with part of speech, an English meaning, an example sentence with translation, a level mark (cheese) and a frequency mark (potato). Then you write the meaning, write the Korean word and write your own sentence, finish with a unit checkup, and mark your work with the answer key at the back. A QR code opens a free match game for the same 20 words. Look-alike words (synonyms and sound-alikes) are explained at the end of each unit.
