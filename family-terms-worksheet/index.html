Family Terms & Honorifics · 가족 호칭과 높임말

Family members, terms based on speaker gender, and basic honorific vocabulary. 가족 호칭, 화자 기준 부르는 말, 기초 높임말 단어

1 · Immediate Family Members · 가족 호칭
RelativeKoreanRespectful formNote
Grandfather할아버지—Father's or mother's father
Grandmother할머니—Father's or mother's mother
Father아버지아버님 (respectful; also spouse’s father)Casual: 아빠
Mother어머니어머님 (respectful; also spouse’s mother)Casual: 엄마

Older brother (male speaker)형형님 (respectful)Used by males
Older sister (male speaker)누나누님 (respectful)Used by males
Older brother (female speaker)오빠—Used by females
Older sister (female speaker)언니—Used by females
Younger sibling동생—Little brother (남동생) / little sister (여동생)

Korean words for older siblings depend on the speaker: a male says 형 and 누나, while a female says 오빠 and 언니.

Use honorific words when speaking to or talking about elders, grandparents, parents, and teachers.

3 · Honorific Particles: 께서 and 께 · 높임 조사

When the subject of the sentence is an elder, the subject particle 이/가 is replaced by 께서 (할머니께서 오셨어요). The topic particle 은/는 becomes 께서는 (아버지께서는 회사에 가셨어요). The giving particle 에게/한테 becomes 께 (선생님께 질문을 드려요).

How to use it

  • Note whether terms are used by a male speaker (형, 누나) or a female speaker (오빠, 언니).
  • Memorize key honorific vocabulary pairs: 밥-진지, 이름-성함, 나이-연세, 자다-주무시다.
  • Complete Practice A to D and verify your answers using the answer key.
