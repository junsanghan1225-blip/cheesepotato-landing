퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 나란히 mean?

  • ① trace
  • ② side by side
  • ③ to wrap
  • ④ profit

나란히 means side by side; in a row. 교실 앞줄에 의자들이 가지런히 나란히 놓여 있었다. (Chairs were neatly placed side by side in the classroom front row.)

Q2Which Korean word means "to be rough"?

  • ① 닳다
  • ② 마련
  • ③ 거칠다
  • ④ 나란히

거칠다 means to be rough; to be coarse. 파도가 거칠고 바람이 심하여 배 운항이 통제되었다. (Waves were rough and wind was severe, so ship operations were restricted.)

Q3What does 흔적 mean?

  • ① bosom
  • ② transaction
  • ③ small size
  • ④ trace

흔적 means trace; track. 시간이 흘러 비바람에 깎여도 바위 위의 글씨 흔적은 뚜렷했다. (Though weathered by wind and rain over time, the writing traces on the rock were clear.)

Q4Which Korean word means "modern"?

  • ① 현대적
  • ② 낭비
  • ③ 거칠다
  • ④ 창작

현대적 means modern; contemporary. 도시는 현대적 건축물과 녹지 공간이 조화를 이룬다. (The city harmonizes modern architecture and green spaces.)

Q5What does 두르다 mean?

  • ① surface
  • ② to wrap around
  • ③ unique
  • ④ permeate

두르다 means to wrap around; to encircle. 프라이팬에 기름을 살짝 두르고 달걀을 부친다. (Lightly coat oil in the frying pan and fry an egg.)

Q6Which Korean word means "preparation"?

  • ① 흔적
  • ② 감싸다
  • ③ 마련
  • ④ 소비

마련 means preparation; provision. 학생은 방학 동안 아르바이트를 하며 학비 마련에 힘썼다. (The student worked hard to prepare tuition by doing part-time jobs during vacation.)
