퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 고급 mean?

  • ① to have ahead
  • ② high quality
  • ③ day and night
  • ④ only then

고급 means high quality; high grade. 이 호텔은 고급 인테리어로 투숙객의 호평을 받는다. (This hotel receives praise from guests for its luxury interior.)

Q2Which Korean word means "early stage"?

  • ① 무렵
  • ② 다가오다
  • ③ 초기
  • ④ 고급

초기 means early stage; beginning. 질병은 초기 단계에 발견하여 치료하는 것이 가장 효과적이다. (It is most effective to discover and treat diseases in early stages.)

Q3What does 앞두다 mean?

  • ① straight
  • ② winter season
  • ③ darkness
  • ④ to have ahead

앞두다 means to have ahead; to be face to face with. 졸업을 앞둔 대학생들은 취업 준비에 온 힘을 쏟고 있다. (College students facing graduation are pouring all energy into job prep.)

Q4Which Korean word means "to delay"?

  • ① 늦추다
  • ② 가뭄
  • ③ 초기
  • ④ 당시

늦추다 means to delay; to slow down. 커브 길에서는 차량의 속도를 충분히 늦추어야 한다. (On a curved road, vehicle speed must be sufficiently slowed down.)

Q5What does 순식간 mean?

  • ① date and time
  • ② instant
  • ③ interval
  • ④ being periodic

순식간 means instant; moment. 사고는 운전자가 방심한 순식간에 일어났다. (The accident happened in an instant when the driver was inattentive.)

Q6Which Korean word means "to approach"?

  • ① 앞두다
  • ② 밤낮
  • ③ 다가오다
  • ④ 빗물

다가오다 means to approach; to come near. 친절한 미소를 지으며 낯선 여행자에게 다가온 주민이 있었다. (There was a resident who approached the unfamiliar traveler with a kind smile.)
