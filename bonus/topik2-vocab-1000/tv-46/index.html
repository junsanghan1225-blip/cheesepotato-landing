Q1What does 전면 mean?

  • ① mural
  • ② all-out
  • ③ merchant
  • ④ voluntary

전면 means all-out; full-scale. 정부는 해당 정책의 전면 재검토를 발표했다. (The government announced an all-out re-examination of the policy.)

Q2Which Korean word means "to be small"?

  • ① 손수
  • ② 궂다
  • ③ 잘다
  • ④ 전면

잘다 means to be small; to be fine. 마늘을 잘게 다져서 양념장에 넣으면 음식의 풍미가 한층 깊어진다. (If garlic is finely minced and added to the sauce, food flavor deepens further.)

Q3What does 벽화 mean?

  • ① to be covered over
  • ② extreme
  • ③ norm
  • ④ mural

벽화 means mural; wall painting. 오래된 마을 담장에 아름다운 벽화를 그렸다. (They painted beautiful murals on the old village fence walls.)

Q4Which Korean word means "subsidy"?

  • ① 지원금
  • ② 고스란히
  • ③ 잘다
  • ④ 두툼하다

지원금 means subsidy; financial grant. 지자체는 소상공인을 돕기 위해 긴급 경영 지원금을 지급했다. (The municipality disbursed emergency management grants to help small merchants.)

Q5What does 시간대 mean?

  • ① to be heavy
  • ② time slot
  • ③ fiber
  • ④ to spout out

시간대 means time slot; time zone. 야간 시간대에는 승객 안전을 위해 순찰을 한층 강화한다. (During nighttime slots, patrols are strengthened further for passenger safety.)

Q6Which Korean word means "to be foul"?

  • ① 벽화
  • ② 상인
  • ③ 궂다
  • ④ 자율적

궂다 means to be foul; to be nasty (weather). 비바람이 몰아치며 날씨가 몹시 궂다. (The weather is very foul with sweeping rain and wind.)
