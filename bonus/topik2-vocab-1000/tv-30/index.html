Q1What does 현대인 mean?

  • ① to turn (pages)
  • ② modern people
  • ③ level
  • ④ supply

Q2Which Korean word means "look in eyes"?

  • ① 과제
  • ② 상승
  • ③ 눈빛
  • ④ 현대인

눈빛 means look in eyes; gaze. 선수의 결연한 눈빛에서 반드시 승리하겠다는 의지가 엿보였다. (From the athlete’s resolute gaze, determination to surely win was glimpsed.)

Q3What does 넘기다 mean?

  • ① company
  • ② distribution
  • ③ to unfold
  • ④ to turn (pages)

넘기다 means to turn (pages); to pass. 어려운 고비를 무사히 넘긴 환자는 건강을 점차 회복하기 시작했다. (The patient safely passing the difficult crisis began to gradually recover health.)

Q4Which Korean word means "knowledge"?

  • ① 지식
  • ② 경쟁
  • ③ 눈빛
  • ④ 실습

지식 means knowledge. 정보화 사회에서는 새로운 지식 습득이 경쟁력이다. (In the information society, acquiring new knowledge is competitiveness.)

Q5What does 교재 mean?

  • ① be seen
  • ② teaching material
  • ③ basis
  • ④ consumer

교재 means teaching material; textbook. 어학당은 학습자의 수준에 맞춘 맞춤형 교재를 자체 개발했다. (The language institute developed tailored teaching materials suited to learners’ levels.)

Q6Which Korean word means "rise"?

  • ① 넘기다
  • ② 수준
  • ③ 상승
  • ④ 상업

상승 means rise; increase. 원자재 가격 상승은 완제품의 생산 단가를 높이는 주요 원인이다. (Raw material price rises are the primary cause increasing finished product costs.)
