퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 시선 mean?

  • ① to lean on
  • ② gaze
  • ③ worsening
  • ④ to be intact

시선 means gaze; line of sight. 발표자는 청중과 시선을 마주치며 발표를 이어갔다. (The presenter continued the speech while making eye contact with the audience.)

Q2Which Korean word means "throat"?

  • ① 체중
  • ② 고통
  • ③ 목구멍
  • ④ 시선

목구멍 means throat. 의사는 환자에게 입을 크게 벌리고 목구멍을 보여 달라고 했다. (The doctor asked the patient to open their mouth wide and show their throat.)

Q3What does 짚다 mean?

  • ① breathing
  • ② to be caused by
  • ③ inside the body
  • ④ to lean on

짚다 means to lean on; to point out. 선생님은 학생들의 오류를 명확하게 짚어 주셨다. (The teacher clearly pointed out students' errors.)

Q4Which Korean word means "disease"?

  • ① 질병
  • ② 의식
  • ③ 목구멍
  • ④ 시력

질병 means disease; illness. 만성 질병은 조기에 발견하여 치료하는 것이 중요하다. (It is important to detect and treat chronic diseases early.)

Q5What does 지치다 mean?

  • ① in one's head
  • ② to be exhausted
  • ③ body temperature
  • ④ pain

지치다 means to be exhausted; to be tired. 현대인들은 치열한 일상에 지쳐 휴식을 갈망한다. (Modern people, exhausted by fierce daily life, yearn for rest.)

Q6Which Korean word means "pain"?

  • ① 체온
  • ② 지치다
  • ③ 고통
  • ④ 인하다

고통 means pain; suffering. 전쟁은 무고한 민간인들에게 씻을 수 없는 큰 고통을 안겨 준다. (War brings indelible great suffering to innocent civilians.)
