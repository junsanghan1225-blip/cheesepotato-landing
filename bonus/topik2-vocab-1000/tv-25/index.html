퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 추위 mean?

  • ① to be refreshing
  • ② cold
  • ③ to dedicate
  • ④ still

추위 means cold; cold weather. 동물들은 긴 겨울 추위를 견디기 위해 깊은 겨울잠에 들어간다. (Animals enter deep hibernation to endure long winter cold.)

Q2Which Korean word means "shade"?

  • ① 부정적
  • ② 지켜보다
  • ③ 그늘
  • ④ 추위

그늘 means shade. 큰 건물에 가려 마당에 짙은 그늘이 드리워졌다. (Blocked by a large building, deep shade was cast over the yard.)

Q3What does 상쾌하다 mean?

  • ① to be childish
  • ② to be trivial
  • ③ rain and wind
  • ④ to be refreshing

상쾌하다 means to be refreshing; to be exhilarating. 찬물로 세수를 하고 나자 기분이 상쾌해졌다. (After washing my face with cold water, my mood became refreshing.)

Q4Which Korean word means "silently"?

  • ① 묵묵히
  • ② 무사히
  • ③ 그늘
  • ④ 정성

묵묵히 means silently; without a word. 그는 불평 한마디 없이 묵묵히 일하며 궂은일을 도맡아 처리했다. (Without a single complaint, he worked silently and handled rough tasks.)

Q5What does 손길 mean?

  • ① heavy rain
  • ② helping hand
  • ③ to be covered
  • ④ hide

손길 means helping hand; touch. 재해 지역 주민들에게 온정의 손길이 이어졌다. (Hands of warm affection followed for disaster region residents.)

Q6Which Korean word means "observe"?

  • ① 상쾌하다
  • ② 바치다
  • ③ 지켜보다
  • ④ 견디다

지켜보다 means observe. 정부는 시장 반응을 면밀히 지켜본 후 대책을 내놓았다. (The government closely observed market reactions and then released countermeasures.)
