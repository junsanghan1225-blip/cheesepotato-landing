퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 유난히 mean?

  • ① ratio
  • ② exceptionally
  • ③ to graze
  • ④ beyond

유난히 means exceptionally; particularly. 올해 여름은 작년에 비해 유난히 무덥고 길었다. (This summer was exceptionally hot and long compared to last year.)

Q2Which Korean word means "thousands"?

  • ① 빠져나가다
  • ② 나란히
  • ③ 수천
  • ④ 유난히

수천 means thousands. 박물관 수장고에는 수천 점의 소중한 고대 유물이 보관되어 있다. (Thousands of precious ancient relics are stored in the museum repository.)

Q3What does 비율 mean?

  • ① to become distant
  • ② surface
  • ③ hundreds
  • ④ ratio

비율 means ratio; proportion. 전문가들은 고령화에 따른 노인 인구 비율의 증가를 분석했다. (Experts analyzed the increase in the elderly population ratio due to aging.)

Q4Which Korean word means "to be trapped"?

  • ① 갇히다
  • ② 밑바닥
  • ③ 수천
  • ④ 내부

갇히다 means to be trapped; to be confined. 고정관념에 갇혀 있으면 새로운 창의적 발상을 하기 어렵다. (If trapped in fixed stereotypes, it is difficult to make new creative ideas.)

Q5What does 텅 mean?

  • ① to be numerous
  • ② completely empty
  • ③ group
  • ④ corner

텅 means completely empty; hollowly. 이사 가고 난 텅 빈 방을 바라보며 시원섭섭한 아쉬움이 밀려왔다. (Gazing at the completely empty room after moving, bittersweet longing rushed in.)

Q6Which Korean word means "side by side"?

  • ① 비율
  • ② 스치다
  • ③ 나란히
  • ④ 눈앞

나란히 means side by side; in a row. 교실 앞줄에 의자들이 가지런히 나란히 놓여 있었다. (Chairs were neatly placed side by side in the classroom front row.)
