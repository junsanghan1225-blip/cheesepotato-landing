퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 체험 mean?

  • ① Buddhist temple
  • ② experience
  • ③ vest
  • ④ cost

체험 means experience; hands-on activity. 다양한 직업 체험 활동은 청소년의 진로 선택에 큰 도움을 준다. (Various vocational experience activities help youths greatly in career choices.)

Q2Which Korean word means "art"?

  • ① 끈
  • ② 패션
  • ③ 미술
  • ④ 체험

미술 means art; fine arts. 어린이 미술 교육은 창의력과 감수성 발달에 큰 도움을 준다. (Children’s art education helps greatly with developing creativity and sensitivity.)

Q3What does 절 mean?

  • ① to be polished
  • ② payment
  • ③ relic
  • ④ Buddhist temple

절 means Buddhist temple. 새해 첫날 아침에 어르신들께 큰절을 올린다. (On the morning of the New Year's Day, we give deep bows to elders.)

Q4Which Korean word means "wristwatch"?

  • ① 손목시계
  • ② 경제적
  • ③ 미술
  • ④ 점퍼

손목시계 means wristwatch; watch. 그는 시간을 확인하기 위해 손목시계를 내려다보았다. (He looked down at his wristwatch to check the time.)

Q5What does 구멍 mean?

  • ① traditional
  • ② hole
  • ③ leather
  • ④ to tie

구멍 means hole; pit. 벽에 못을 박기 위해 드릴로 구멍을 뚫었다. (He drilled a hole in the wall to drive a nail.)

Q6Which Korean word means "fashion"?

  • ① 절
  • ② 조끼
  • ③ 패션
  • ④ 부담

패션 means fashion. 그녀는 자신만의 독특한 패션 감각을 자랑한다. (She boasts her own unique sense of fashion.)
