Q2Which Korean word means "art"?

  • ① 끈
  • ② 패션

Q3What does 절 mean?

  • ① to be polished
  • ② payment
  • ③ relic
  • ④ Buddhist temple

Q4Which Korean word means "wristwatch"?

  • ① 손목시계

Q5What does 구멍 mean?

구멍 means hole; pit. 벽에 못을 박기 위해 드릴로 구멍을 뚫었다. (He drilled a hole in the wall to drive a nail.)
