퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 머릿속 mean?

  • ① worsening
  • ② in one's head
  • ③ to be caused by
  • ④ go through

머릿속 means in one's head. 갑작스러운 충격에 그녀는 머릿속이 하얘지는 기분을 느꼈다. (At the sudden shock, she felt as if inside her head went blank.)

Q2Which Korean word means "sight"?

  • ① 고통
  • ② 갖추다
  • ③ 시력
  • ④ 머릿속

시력 means sight; vision. 정기적인 검사를 통해 시력을 보호해야 한다. (Vision must be protected through regular checkups.)

Q3What does 악화 mean?

  • ① to go through
  • ② flow
  • ③ disease
  • ④ worsening

악화 means worsening; deterioration. 건강 상태가 급격히 악화되자 긴급 입원을 결정했다. (As health status worsened sharply, emergency hospitalization was decided.)

Q4Which Korean word means "breathing"?

  • ① 호흡
  • ② 이어지다
  • ③ 시력
  • ④ 통증

호흡 means breathing; respiration. 가파른 언덕을 오르자 호흡이 점차 가빠지기 시작했다. (As I climbed the steep hill, breathing gradually began to quicken.)

Q5What does 낮추다 mean?

  • ① body temperature
  • ② to lower
  • ③ to be exhausted
  • ④ existing

낮추다 means to lower; to reduce. 여름철 실내 온도를 지나치게 낮추지 말아야 한다. (One should not excessively lower indoor temperatures in summer.)

Q6Which Korean word means "to possess"?

  • ① 악화
  • ② 인하다
  • ③ 갖추다
  • ④ 줄어들다

갖추다 means to possess; to be equipped with. 사회인으로서 타인을 배려하는 올바른 예의범절을 갖추어야 한다. (As a member of society, one must possess proper manners considering others.)
