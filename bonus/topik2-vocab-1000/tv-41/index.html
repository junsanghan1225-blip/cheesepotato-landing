퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 생물 mean?

  • ① soil
  • ② organism
  • ③ water quality
  • ④ weed

생물 means organism; living thing. 지구상에는 수많은 동식물과 미생물이 공존한다. (Countless animals, plants, and microorganisms coexist on Earth.)

Q2Which Korean word means "to fly in"?

  • ① 매연
  • ② 진흙
  • ③ 날아들다
  • ④ 생물

날아들다 means to fly in; to come flying. 봄이 오자 꽃밭으로 나비가 날아든다. (As spring arrives, butterflies fly into the flower garden.)

Q3What does 토양 mean?

  • ① ocean
  • ② share
  • ③ eco-friendly
  • ④ soil

토양 means soil; ground. 유기농 재배는 건강한 토양 환경을 유지하는 데 기여한다. (Organic farming contributes to maintaining a healthy soil environment.)

Q4Which Korean word means "natural"?

  • ① 천연
  • ② 홀로
  • ③ 날아들다
  • ④ 배출

천연 means natural; nature. 천연 원료로 만든 친환경 제품이 소비자들에게 큰 인기다. (Eco-friendly goods made of natural ingredients are very popular with consumers.)

Q5What does 석탄 mean?

  • ① wild
  • ② coal
  • ③ concentration
  • ④ wind power

석탄 means coal. 탄소 배출을 줄이기 위해 석탄 화력 발전소를 점진 폐쇄한다. (Coal-fired power plants are gradually closed to reduce carbon emissions.)

Q6Which Korean word means "mud"?

  • ① 토양
  • ② 수질
  • ③ 진흙
  • ④ 종

진흙 means mud; clay. 아이들은 갯벌에서 부드러운 진흙을 만지며 즐겁게 놀았다. (Children played delightfully touching soft mud on the tidal flat.)
