Q1What does 거 mean?

  • ① this
  • ② thing
  • ③ to fold
  • ④ residence

거 means thing. 이거 누구 가방인지 잘 알아요? (Do you know well whose bag this is?)

Q2Which Korean word means "rubbish"?

  • ① 닦다
  • ② 엘리베이터
  • ③ 쓰레기
  • ④ 달리다

쓰레기 means rubbish; trash. 쓰레기를 쓰레기통에 버려요. (I throw away trash in the trash can.)

Q3What does 접다 mean?

  • ① the house next door
  • ② radio
  • ③ puppy
  • ④ to fold

접다 means to fold; to collapse. 편지를 쓰고 종이를 반으로 접어요. (I write a letter and fold the paper in half.)

Q4Which Korean word means "the house next door"?

  • ① 옆집
  • ② 바닥
  • ③ 새
  • ④ 쓰레기

옆집 means the house next door. 옆집 아저씨께서 마당의 꽃을 정성껏 가꾸세요. (The uncle next door takes care of flowers in the yard with devotion.)

Q5What does 유리 mean?

  • ① to raise
  • ② glass
  • ③ to throw away
  • ④ to repair

유리 means glass. 깨끗하게 닦은 창문 유리가 거울처럼 반짝여요. (The cleanly wiped window glass sparkles like a mirror.)

Q6Which Korean word means "to run"?

  • ① 이거
  • ② 접다
  • ③ 달리다
  • ④ 댁

달리다 means to run; to race. 아침마다 건강을 위해 운동장을 열심히 달려요. (Every morning, I run diligently around the playground for my health.)
