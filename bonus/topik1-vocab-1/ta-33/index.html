Q1What does 영화배우 mean?

  • ① employee
  • ② movie actor / actress
  • ③ to be pretty
  • ④ class

영화배우 means movie actor / actress. 극장에서 유명한 영화배우를 보았어요. (I saw a famous film actor at the theater.)

Q2Which Korean word means "employee"?

  • ① 예쁘다
  • ② 수업
  • ③ 직원
  • ④ 지각하다

직원 means employee; staff. 회사 직원들과 회식을 해요. (I have a company dinner with office colleagues.)

Q3What does 예쁘다 mean?

  • ① student
  • ② computer lab
  • ③ book
  • ④ to be pretty

예쁘다 means to be pretty; to be beautiful. 봄이 되니 길가에 예쁜 꽃들이 피었어요. (As spring arrives, pretty flowers bloom on the roadside.)

Q4Which Korean word means "student"?

  • ① 학생
  • ② 방학
  • ③ 읽다
  • ④ 직원

학생 means student. 교실에서 학생들이 한국어 수업을 들어요. (Students take a Korean class in the classroom.)

Q5What does 교실 mean?

  • ① blackboard
  • ② classroom
  • ③ homemaker
  • ④ cool

교실 means classroom. 아침에 교실에 들어가서 창문을 열고 환기해요. (I enter the classroom in the morning, open the window, and air it out.)

Q6Which Korean word means "playground"?

  • ① 종업원
  • ② 예쁘다
  • ③ 운동장
  • ④ 수업

운동장 means playground; sports field. 학생들이 운동장에서 축구해요. (Students play soccer on the playground.)
