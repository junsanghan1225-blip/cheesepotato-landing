퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 입다 mean?

  • ① to put on
  • ② to wear
  • ③ indoor shoes
  • ④ sneakers

입다 means to wear; to put on. 날씨가 쌀쌀하니까 따뜻한 외투를 입어요. (Because the weather is chilly, I wear a warm coat.)

Q2Which Korean word means "bag"?

  • ① 안경
  • ② 구두
  • ③ 가방
  • ④ 얼마

가방 means bag. 학교에 갈 때 교과서와 필통을 가방에 넣어요. (When going to school, I put textbooks and pencil cases in my bag.)

Q3What does 실내화 mean?

  • ① hiking boots
  • ② won (Korean currency)
  • ③ to exchange currency
  • ④ indoor shoes

실내화 means indoor shoes; slippers. 하얀 실내화를 깨끗이 빨았어요. (I washed the white slippers cleanly.)

Q4Which Korean word means "hiking boots"?

  • ① 등산화
  • ② 티셔츠
  • ③ 은행
  • ④ 가방

등산화 means hiking boots. 새로 산 등산화 끈을 단단히 매요. (I firmly tie the laces of my newly bought hiking boots.)

Q5What does 치마 mean?

  • ① card
  • ② skirt
  • ③ shoes
  • ④ pants

치마 means skirt. 백화점에서 긴 치마를 샀어요. (I bought a long skirt at the department store.)

Q6Which Korean word means "how much"?

  • ① 신다
  • ② 실내화
  • ③ 얼마
  • ④ 운동화

얼마 means how much; some amount. 이 사과 한 상자에 얼마인지 여쭤봐요. (I ask how much this box of apples is.)
