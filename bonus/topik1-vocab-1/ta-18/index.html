퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 맑다 mean?

  • ① hard
  • ② to be clear
  • ③ to be bad
  • ④ to like

맑다 means to be clear; to be clean. 시골의 공기가 아주 맑고 좋아요. (The countryside air is very clear and nice.)

Q2Which Korean word means "to be important"?

  • ① 포기하다
  • ② 싶다
  • ③ 중요하다
  • ④ 기분

중요하다 means to be important. 이 약속은 아주 중요해요. (This appointment is very important.)

Q3What does 나쁘다 mean?

  • ① to be good
  • ② to be hard
  • ③ worry
  • ④ to be bad

나쁘다 means to be bad. 건강에 나쁜 습관을 고쳐요. (I fix habits that are bad for health.)

Q4Which Korean word means "to be good"?

  • ① 좋다
  • ② 재미있다
  • ③ 반갑다
  • ④ 중요하다

좋다 means to be good; to be nice. 이 식당은 음식 맛과 품질이 좋아요. (This restaurant has good food taste and quality.)

Q5What does 마음 mean?

  • ① to be okay
  • ② mind
  • ③ firmly
  • ④ pardon

마음 means mind; heart. 친구의 따뜻한 위로에 마음이 편안해졌어요. (My heart felt at ease with my friend's warm comfort.)

Q6Which Korean word means "mood"?

  • ① 열심히
  • ② 나쁘다
  • ③ 기분
  • ④ 좋아하다

기분 means mood; feeling. 시험에 합격했다는 소식을 듣고 기분이 무척 좋았어요. (Hearing the news of passing the exam, I felt very happy.)
