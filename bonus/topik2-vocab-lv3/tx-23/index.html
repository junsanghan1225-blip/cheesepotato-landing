퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 들여놓다 mean?

  • ① residence
  • ② to bring in
  • ③ ventilation
  • ④ gag

들여놓다 means to bring in; to introduce; to step into. 그는 위험한 사업에 처음으로 발을 들여놓게 되었다. (He came to set foot into a hazardous business for the first time.)

Q2Which Korean word means "rent"?

  • ① 틈새
  • ② 각본
  • ③ 임대료
  • ④ 들여놓다

임대료 means rent. 상가 임대료가 너무 올라 소상공인의 부담이 커졌다. (Commercial rent rose too much, increasing the burden on small merchants.)

Q3What does 거처 mean?

  • ① camp
  • ② open contest
  • ③ eaves
  • ④ residence

거처 means residence; dwelling; place to stay. 수재민들은 임시 거처에서 힘겨운 하루를 보냈다. (Flood victims spent a tough day in temporary shelter.)

Q4Which Korean word means "to show inside"?

  • ① 들여다보이다
  • ② 경연
  • ③ 임대료
  • ④ 옥상

들여다보이다 means to show inside; to be seen. 커튼을 치지 않으면 밖에서 집 안이 들여다보인다. (If curtains are not drawn, the inside of the house can be seen from outside.)

Q5What does 기어들다 mean?

  • ① in clusters
  • ② creep into
  • ③ building
  • ④ fellow enthusiast

기어들다 means creep into; crawl into; sink into. 도둑은 담장 밑 구멍으로 기어들었다. (The thief crawled in through a hole under the fence.)

Q6Which Korean word means "screenplay"?

  • ① 거처
  • ② 통풍
  • ③ 각본
  • ④ 개작

각본 means screenplay; script; scenario. 탄탄한 각본 덕분에 연극의 완성도가 한층 높아졌다. (Thanks to a solid script, the play's level of completeness was raised further.)
