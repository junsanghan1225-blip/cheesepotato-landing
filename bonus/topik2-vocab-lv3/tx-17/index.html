퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 그르다 mean?

  • ① by all means
  • ② wrong
  • ③ to cast out
  • ④ to aim for

그르다 means wrong; incorrect; false. 무엇이 옳고 그른지 명확히 가려야 한다. (One must clearly distinguish what is right and what is wrong.)

Q2Which Korean word means "affirmation"?

  • ① 난폭
  • ② 노골적
  • ③ 긍정
  • ④ 그르다

긍정 means affirmation; acknowledgement. 그는 제안에 대해 고개를 끄덕이며 긍정을 표했다. (He expressed affirmation by nodding at the proposal.)

Q3What does 기어이 mean?

  • ① to aim for
  • ② extreme vigor
  • ③ peek into
  • ④ by all means

기어이 means by all means; inevitably; at any cost. 그는 약속한 목표를 기어이 달성하고 말았다. (He inevitably achieved his promised goal in the end.)

Q4Which Korean word means "by all means"?

  • ① 기필코
  • ② 그르다
  • ③ 기껏
  • ④ 냉정

기필코 means by all means; without fail; definitely. 팀은 이번 경기에서 기필코 우승하겠다고 다짐했다. (The team resolved to win this match without fail.)

Q5What does 냉담하다 mean?

  • ① busy
  • ② cold
  • ③ by all means
  • ④ calmness

냉담하다 means cold; indifferent; callous. 새로운 정책에 대해 시민들의 반응은 매우 냉담했다. (Citizens' response to the new policy was very cold.)

Q6Which Korean word means "blatant"?

  • ① 기어이
  • ② 내치다
  • ③ 노골적
  • ④ 눈초리

노골적 means blatant; overt; undisguised. 상대방의 노골적인 무시는 큰 반발을 불러일으켰다. (The other party's overt contempt aroused a huge backlash.)
