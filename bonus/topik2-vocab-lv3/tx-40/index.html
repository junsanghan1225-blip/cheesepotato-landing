퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 뵈다 mean?

  • ① to look closely
  • ② see
  • ③ specially
  • ④ giving up

뵈다 means see; meet (humble). 그는 명절을 맞아 고향에 계신 부모님을 뵈러 이른 아침 길을 나섰다. (He set out early in morning to visit parents in hometown for holiday.)

Q2Which Korean word means "handshake"?

  • ① 이러다
  • ② 특징
  • ③ 악수
  • ④ 뵈다

악수 means handshake. 양국 정상은 회담장에 입장하며 굳은 악수로 신뢰를 확인했다. (Leaders of both nations confirmed trust with firm handshakes.)

Q3What does 살펴보다 mean?

  • ① debate
  • ② Dear
  • ③ honey
  • ④ to look closely

살펴보다 means to look closely; to examine; to check. 연구진은 실험 결과를 다각도로 살펴보며 원인을 분석했다. (Researchers examined experimental results from multiple angles and analyzed causes.)

Q4Which Korean word means "what to do (about it)"?

  • ① 어떡하다
  • ② 고백
  • ③ 악수
  • ④ 소문

어떡하다 means what to do (about it). 갑작스러운 사고 소식에 어떡해야 좋을지 갈피를 잡지 못했다. (Hearing sudden news of the accident, he couldn't figure out what to do.)

Q5What does 전달 mean?

  • ① farewell party
  • ② delivery
  • ③ thoughts
  • ④ recommendation

전달 means delivery; transmission. 구호 물품이 고립된 수재민들에게 안전하게 전달되었다. (Relief supplies were safely delivered to isolated flood victims.)

Q6Which Korean word means "characteristic"?

  • ① 살펴보다
  • ② 일부러
  • ③ 특징
  • ④ 게시

특징 means characteristic; feature. 한국 전통 건축의 주요 특징은 자연과의 조화에 있다. (The major characteristic of traditional Korean architecture lies in harmony with nature.)
