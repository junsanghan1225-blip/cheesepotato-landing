퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 협약 mean?

  • ① compulsory
  • ② agreement
  • ③ person concerned
  • ④ cold war

협약 means agreement; pact; accord. 참가국들은 온실가스 감축을 위한 국제 환경 협약을 체결했다. (Participating nations concluded international environmental agreements on greenhouse gas reductions.)

Q2Which Korean word means "confinement"?

  • ① 공정성
  • ② 남용
  • ③ 감금
  • ④ 협약

감금 means confinement; imprisonment; detention. 범인은 피해자를 방 안에 불법으로 감금한 혐의를 받는다. (The criminal is suspected of illegally confining the victim inside a room.)

Q3What does 강제적 mean?

  • ① foundation
  • ② toward North Korea
  • ③ rape
  • ④ compulsory

강제적 means compulsory; forced; coercive. 정부는 불법 점거에 대해 강제적 퇴거 조치를 내렸다. (The government issued compulsory eviction measures regarding the illegal occupation.)

Q4Which Korean word means "governmental authority"?

  • ① 공권력
  • ② 단서
  • ③ 감금
  • ④ 검찰

공권력 means governmental authority; public power. 국가는 공권력을 남용하여 시민을 탄압해서는 안 된다. (The state must not abuse governmental authority to suppress citizens.)

Q5What does 권익 mean?

  • ① spy
  • ② rights and interests
  • ③ arrest
  • ④ pistol

권익 means rights and interests. 협회는 소비자의 권익 향상을 위해 힘쓴다. (The association strives to improve consumers' rights and interests.)

Q6Which Korean word means "abuse"?

  • ① 강제적
  • ② 관련자
  • ③ 남용
  • ④ 뇌물

남용 means abuse; misuse. 권력의 남용을 막기 위한 제도적 장치가 필요하다. (Institutional mechanisms are needed to prevent the abuse of power.)
