퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 독재 mean?

  • ① overpraise
  • ② dictatorship
  • ③ positive response
  • ④ creativity

독재 means dictatorship; autocracy; tyranny. 오랜 독재 체제가 무너지고 민주 정부가 수립되었다. (The long dictatorship collapsed and a democratic government was established.)

Q2Which Korean word means "publication"?

  • ① 세밀하다
  • ② 처지
  • ③ 게재
  • ④ 독재

게재 means publication; insertion; posting. 신문사는 독자의 기고문을 사설 면에 전격 게재했다. (The newspaper promptly published the reader's contribution on the editorial page.)

Q3What does 과찬 mean?

  • ① free
  • ② to be clumsy
  • ③ inside story
  • ④ overpraise

과찬 means overpraise; excessive praise. 그는 동료들의 과찬에 겸손한 태도를 보였다. (He showed a humble attitude toward colleagues' excessive praise.)

Q4Which Korean word means "back-and-forth battle"?

  • ① 공방
  • ② 비단
  • ③ 게재
  • ④ 인위적

공방 means back-and-forth battle; verbal sparring. 법정에서 진실을 둘러싼 검찰과 변호인의 공방이 팽팽했다. (In the courtroom, battle between prosecution and defense over truth was tense.)

Q5What does 무언 mean?

  • ① to chase after
  • ② wordlessness
  • ③ to sense
  • ④ advantage

무언 means wordlessness; silence. 그는 무언의 압박을 견디지 못하고 진실을 털어놓았다. (Unable to endure the silent pressure, he confessed the truth.)

Q6Which Korean word means "situation"?

  • ① 과찬
  • ② 호응
  • ③ 처지
  • ④ 보잘것없다

처지 means situation; circumstances. 우리는 같은 처지에 놓인 동료들과 힘을 모았다. (We joined hands with colleagues placed in the same situation.)
