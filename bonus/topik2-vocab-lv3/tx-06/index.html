퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 가득하다 mean?

  • ① immensely
  • ② full
  • ③ awesome
  • ④ clearly

가득하다 means full; filled. 수확철을 맞은 과수원 나무마다 탐스러운 열매가 가득했다. (Orchard trees greeting harvest season were full of plump fruits.)

Q2Which Korean word means "common point"?

  • ① 당연히
  • ② 딱
  • ③ 공통점
  • ④ 가득하다

공통점 means common point. 연구진은 성공한 기업가들이 지닌 몇 가지 공통점을 분석했다. (The research team analyzed several common points of successful entrepreneurs.)

Q3What does 굉장히 mean?

  • ① separately
  • ② incompleteness
  • ③ unrelated
  • ④ immensely

굉장히 means immensely; terribly. 올해 여름은 기후 변화로 인해 굉장히 무더운 날씨가 이어졌다. (This summer, immensely scorching weather continued due to climate change.)

Q4Which Korean word means "exceed"?

  • ① 넘어서다
  • ② 불규칙
  • ③ 공통점
  • ④ 꽤

넘어서다 means exceed; cross; overcome. 그 영화는 개봉 열흘 만에 관객 천만 명을 넘어섰다. (The movie exceeded ten million viewers within ten days of release.)

Q5What does 대체로 mean?

  • ① common
  • ② generally
  • ③ tightly
  • ④ less

대체로 means generally; mostly. 전문가들은 올해 경제성장률 전망치에 대체로 동의했다. (Experts generally agreed on this year's economic growth forecasts.)

Q6Which Korean word means "exactly"?

  • ① 굉장히
  • ② 대단하다
  • ③ 딱
  • ④ 바탕

딱 means exactly; tightly; firmly. 의사의 권고에 따라 그는 술과 담배를 딱 끊고 운동을 시작했다. (Following doctor's advice, he stopped drinking and smoking firmly.)
