퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 내던지다 mean?

  • ① leap
  • ② to throw away
  • ③ drastically
  • ④ proper

내던지다 means to throw away; to fling; to discard. 그는 자신의 모든 것을 내던지고 새 사업을 시작했다. (He threw away everything he had and started a new business.)

Q2Which Korean word means "to stick to"?

  • ① 들어맞다
  • ② 유독
  • ③ 달라붙다
  • ④ 내던지다

달라붙다 means to stick to; to cling to; to tackle. 직원들은 프로젝트를 끝내기 위해 밤낮으로 달라붙었다. (Employees tackled the project day and night to finish it.)

Q3What does 도약 mean?

  • ① compare
  • ② regard
  • ③ to light
  • ④ leap

도약 means leap; jump; advancement. 중소기업이 기술 혁신을 통해 세계적 기업으로 도약했다. (The small business leaped into a global firm through technological innovation.)

Q4Which Korean word means "to be reversed"?

  • ① 뒤바뀌다
  • ② 톡톡히
  • ③ 달라붙다
  • ④ 동적

뒤바뀌다 means to be reversed; to be switched; to be turned upside down. 갑작스러운 사고로 인해 피해자와 가해자의 입장이 뒤바뀌었다. (Due to a sudden accident, the positions of the victim and perpetrator were reversed.)

Q5What does 막대하다 mean?

  • ① phased
  • ② to be huge
  • ③ breakthrough
  • ④ the most suitable

막대하다 means to be huge; to be enormous. 대형 화재로 인해 공장은 막대한 경제적 손실을 입었다. (Due to the large fire, the factory suffered enormous economic losses.)

Q6Which Korean word means "unusually"?

  • ① 도약
  • ② 대폭
  • ③ 유독
  • ④ 턱없이

유독 means unusually; particularly. 그는 수많은 직원 중에서도 유독 성실하여 칭찬을 받았다. (Among numerous staff members, he was praised for being uniquely diligent.)
