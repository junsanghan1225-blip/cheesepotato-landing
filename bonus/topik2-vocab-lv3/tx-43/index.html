Q1What does 걸려들다 mean?

  • ① lack
  • ② to fall into (a trap)
  • ③ crisis
  • ④ thoroughly

걸려들다 means to fall into (a trap); to be caught in. 늑대는 사냥꾼이 놓은 덫에 깊숙이 걸려들었다. (The wolf fell deeply into the trap set by the hunter.)

Q2Which Korean word means "feeling that generations have changed"?

  • ① 경청
  • ② 고진감래
  • ③ 격세지감
  • ④ 걸려들다

격세지감 means feeling that generations have changed; feeling the great changes of time. 허허벌판이던 마을이 번화가로 변해 격세지감을 느끼게 했다. (The village that was once an empty plain turned into a bustling street, evoking a sense of time's change.)

Q3What does 결여 mean?

  • ① intention
  • ② official language
  • ③ decision
  • ④ lack

결여 means lack; absence; deficiency. 그의 주장은 구체적인 근거 결여로 설득력을 얻지 못했다. (His argument failed to gain persuasiveness due to lack of concrete evidence.)

Q4Which Korean word means "to decide"?

  • ① 결정짓다
  • ② 공공연하다
  • ③ 격세지감
  • ④ 결점

결정짓다 means to decide; to determine; to finalize. 이번 선거 결과가 국가의 미래를 결정짓게 될 것이다. (The outcome of this election will determine the future of the nation.)

Q5What does 고사하다 mean?

  • ① aim
  • ② to say nothing of
  • ③ resolution
  • ④ adherence

고사하다 means to say nothing of; to be out of the question; to leave aside. 그는 1등은 고사하고 시험 합격조차 장담할 수 없었다. (Let alone first place, he could not even guarantee passing the exam.)

Q6Which Korean word means "sweet after bitter"?

  • ① 결여
  • ② 고비
  • ③ 고진감래
  • ④ 공개적

고진감래 means sweet after bitter; joy after suffering. 수많은 역경을 이겨내고 마침내 고진감래를 맛보았다. (After overcoming countless adversities, they finally tasted sweet success.)
