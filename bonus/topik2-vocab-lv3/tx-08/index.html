퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 광범위하다 mean?

  • ① terribly
  • ② wide-ranging
  • ③ by far
  • ④ sometimes

광범위하다 means wide-ranging; extensive; broad. 피해 지역이 워낙 광범위하여 복구에 시일이 걸린다. (The damaged area is so extensive that recovery takes considerable time.)

Q2Which Korean word means "extreme"?

  • ① 다분히
  • ② 더디다
  • ③ 극
  • ④ 광범위하다

극 means extreme; peak; pole. 선수들의 긴장감이 극에 달했다. (The players' tension reached its peak.)

Q3What does 끔찍이 mean?

  • ① all the more
  • ② tiny minority
  • ③ rapidness
  • ④ terribly

끔찍이 means terribly; extraordinarily; dearly. 할머니는 손주를 끔찍이 아끼고 사랑하신다. (Grandmother cares for and loves her grandchild dearly.)

Q4Which Korean word means "all of a sudden"?

  • ① 느닷없이
  • ② 지름
  • ③ 극
  • ④ 낱낱이

느닷없이 means all of a sudden; unexpectedly; abruptly. 평화롭던 마을에 느닷없이 큰 화재가 발생했다. (A large fire broke out all of a sudden in the peaceful village.)

Q5What does 대대적 mean?

  • ① ultimate
  • ② grand
  • ③ out of nowhere
  • ④ important

대대적 means grand; extensive; on a large scale. 경찰은 교통사고를 줄이기 위해 대대적인 음주 단속을 벌였다. (Police conducted extensive drunk-driving crackdowns to reduce traffic accidents.)

Q6Which Korean word means "slow"?

  • ① 끔찍이
  • ② 단연
  • ③ 더디다
  • ④ 대거

더디다 means slow; sluggish; belated. 철저한 검토를 거치느라 사업 진행이 다소 더디게 이루어졌다. (Due to thorough review, the project progress was made somewhat sluggishly.)
