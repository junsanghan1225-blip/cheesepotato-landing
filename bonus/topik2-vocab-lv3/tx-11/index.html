퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 발굴 mean?

  • ① scripture
  • ② excavation
  • ③ national liberation
  • ④ commemorate

발굴 means excavation; unearthing; discovery. 학회는 숨겨진 인재 발굴을 위해 창작 공모전을 열었다. (The academy opened a creative contest to discover hidden talent.)

Q2Which Korean word means "geomungo (traditional Korean six-stringed zither)"?

  • ① 관혼상제
  • ② 기념회
  • ③ 거문고
  • ④ 발굴

거문고 means geomungo (traditional Korean six-stringed zither). 전통 악기 중 거문고는 깊고 묵직한 소리를 낸다. (Among traditional instruments, the geomungo produces a deep and heavy sound.)

Q3What does 경전 mean?

  • ① returning home in glory
  • ② homogeneity
  • ③ masterpiece
  • ④ scripture

경전 means scripture; sacred text; canon. 고대 종교의 성스러운 경전은 지혜의 보고로 여겨진다. (Sacred scriptures of ancient religions are regarded as treasuries of wisdom.)

Q4Which Korean word means "custom"?

  • ① 관례
  • ② 대중성
  • ③ 거문고
  • ④ 관

관례 means custom; precedent; convention. 외교 행사에서는 전통적인 의전 관례를 철저히 따른다. (In diplomatic events, traditional protocol conventions are strictly followed.)

Q5What does 국보 mean?

  • ① Protestantism
  • ② national treasure
  • ③ succession
  • ④ icing on the cake

국보 means national treasure. 박물관에 소장된 백자 항아리가 마침내 국보로 지정되었다. (The white porcelain jar kept in the museum was finally designated as a national treasure.)

Q6Which Korean word means "commemorative meeting"?

  • ① 경전
  • ② 광복
  • ③ 기념회
  • ④ 다채롭다

기념회 means commemorative meeting; memorial gathering. 동문들은 창립 오십 주년 기념회를 성황리에 열었다. (Alumni held the 50th founding anniversary gathering with great success.)
