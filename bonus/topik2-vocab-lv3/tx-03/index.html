Q1What does 치매 mean?

  • ① bleeding
  • ② dementia
  • ③ easy
  • ④ to fester

치매 means dementia; Alzheimer's. 정부는 치매 조기 진단과 치료 지원을 위한 국가 제도를 강화했다. (The government strengthened national systems for early dementia diagnosis and treatment support.)

Q2Which Korean word means "occurrence of disease"?

  • ① 거동
  • ② 고달프다
  • ③ 발병
  • ④ 치매

발병 means occurrence of disease; outbreak. 연구진은 유전 질환의 조기 발병 기전을 규명했다. (The research team identified early onset mechanisms of genetic disorders.)

Q3What does 출혈 mean?

  • ① stiffness
  • ② to starve
  • ③ public health
  • ④ bleeding

출혈 means bleeding; hemorrhage. 응급 환자는 심각한 과다 출혈로 생명이 위독한 상태였다. (The emergency patient was in critical condition due to severe excessive bleeding.)

Q4Which Korean word means "refreshed"?

  • ① 개운하다
  • ② 구토
  • ③ 발병
  • ④ 가쁘다

개운하다 means refreshed; light; clear. 푹 자고 일어났더니 무겁던 머리가 맑고 개운하다. (After sleeping deeply and waking up, my heavy head feels clear and refreshed.)

Q5What does 거북하다 mean?

  • ① hearing loss
  • ② uncomfortable
  • ③ support
  • ④ spasm

거북하다 means uncomfortable; awkward; bloated. 낯선 사람들과 한자리에 앉아 있으니 마음이 거북했다. (Sitting in one place with strangers made me feel awkward.)

Q6Which Korean word means "exhausting"?

  • ① 출혈
  • ② 거뜬하다
  • ③ 고달프다
  • ④ 과민

고달프다 means exhausting; weary; tough; arduous. 타향살이의 고달픈 생활 속에서도 그는 꿈을 잃지 않았다. (Even amid the exhausting life of living away from home, he did not lose his dream.)
