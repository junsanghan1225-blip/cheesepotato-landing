Q1What does 부친 mean?

  • ① father-in-law (husband's father)
  • ② father (formal)
  • ③ carry someone on one's back
  • ④ elders

부친 means father (formal). 그는 부친의 유지를 받들어 장학 재단을 설립하고 사회에 환원했다. (Following his father's will, he set up scholarship foundations.)

Q2Which Korean word means "stepmother"?

  • ① 아랫사람
  • ② 외할아버지
  • ③ 새엄마
  • ④ 부친

새엄마 means stepmother. 가족 구성원의 따뜻한 배려는 새엄마와의 갈등을 해소하는 열쇠였다. (Family members' warm consideration was key to resolving conflicts with stepmother.)

Q3What does 시아버지 mean?

  • ① maternal uncle's wife
  • ② children
  • ③ blind date
  • ④ father-in-law (husband's father)

시아버지 means father-in-law (husband's father). 며느리는 건강이 쇠약해진 시아버지를 정성껏 병간호했다. (The daughter-in-law devotedly nursed her weakened father-in-law.)

Q4Which Korean word means "twin"?

  • ① 쌍둥이
  • ② 일행
  • ③ 새엄마
  • ④ 신혼부부

쌍둥이 means twin. 쌍둥이 자매는 서로 다른 개성을 지녔으나 깊은 유대감을 나누었다. (Twin sisters had different individuality but shared deep bonds.)

Q5What does 연인 mean?

  • ① cousin
  • ② lover
  • ③ newlywed
  • ④ maternal uncle

연인 means lover; sweetheart; couple. 두 사람은 서로의 꿈을 응원하며 오랜 연인 관계를 성실히 이어왔다. (The two cheered each other's dreams, continuing long lover relations.)

Q6Which Korean word means "maternal grandfather"?

  • ① 시아버지
  • ② 업다
  • ③ 외할아버지
  • ④ 윗사람

외할아버지 means maternal grandfather. 외할아버지는 평생 교직에 몸담으며 학생들을 올바르게 지도하셨다. (My maternal grandfather spent his lifetime teaching, guiding students uprightly.)
