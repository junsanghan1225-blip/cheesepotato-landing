Q2Which Korean word means "boarding"?

  • ① 욕실

Q3What does 잠그다 mean?

  • ② hot spring
  • ③ room number
  • ④ lock

Q4Which Korean word means "master bedroom"?

  • ① 안방
  • ② 바둑
  • ③ 하숙
  • ④ 복도

안방 means master bedroom; main room. 한국 전통 주택에서 안방은 집안의 여주인이 머무는 중심 공간이었다. (In Korean traditional homes, anbang was core space where mistress stayed.)

Q5What does 창가 mean?

  • ② window seat
  • ③ curtain
  • ④ outside a window

Q6Which Korean word means "collection"?
