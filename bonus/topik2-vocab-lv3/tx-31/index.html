Q1What does 늠름하다 mean?

  • ① academics and academic techniques
  • ② gallant
  • ③ teaching
  • ④ state expense

늠름하다 means gallant; manly; imposing; dignified. 단상에 선 군인들은 늠름한 태도로 경례를 올렸다. (The soldiers on the podium saluted with a dignified attitude.)

Q2Which Korean word means "potential"?

  • ① 학문적
  • ② 구절
  • ③ 잠재력
  • ④ 늠름하다

잠재력 means potential; latent ability. 그 신생 기업은 시장에서 높은 성장 잠재력을 인정받았다. (That startup company was recognized for high growth potential in the market.)

Q3What does 학술 mean?

  • ① public
  • ② enlighten
  • ③ grade
  • ④ academics and academic techniques

학술 means academics and academic techniques; being academic; being scientific. 교수들은 새로운 학술 연구 성과를 논문으로 발표했다. (Professors published new academic research achievements in papers.)

Q4Which Korean word means "humanities"?

  • ① 인문학
  • ② 기법
  • ③ 잠재력
  • ④ 정규

인문학 means humanities. 인문학적 소양은 급변하는 인공지능 시대에 더욱 강조된다. (Humanities literacy is emphasized even more in the rapidly changing AI era.)

Q5What does 견문 mean?

  • ① discipline
  • ② experience
  • ③ training
  • ④ consideration

견문 means experience; knowledge; observations. 그는 해외 유학을 통해 다채로운 문화와 견문을 넓혔다. (Through studying abroad, he broadened his knowledge and diverse cultural perspectives.)

Q6Which Korean word means "passage"?

  • ① 학술
  • ② 가르침
  • ③ 구절
  • ④ 귀납적

구절 means passage; verse; phrase. 그는 책 속에서 감명 깊게 읽은 구절을 낭독했다. (He read aloud a passage from the book that he had read with deep impression.)
