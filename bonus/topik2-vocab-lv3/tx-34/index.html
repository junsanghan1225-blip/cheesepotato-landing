퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 공략 mean?

  • ① financial industry
  • ② targeting
  • ③ foundation
  • ④ monopoly

공략 means targeting; strategy; capture. 감독은 상대 팀의 수비 약점을 집중 공략했다. (The coach aggressively targeted the weakness of the opposing team's defense.)

Q2Which Korean word means "customs"?

  • ① 기업체
  • ② 도태
  • ③ 관세
  • ④ 공략

관세 means customs; tariff. 정부는 물가 안정을 위해 일부 품목의 관세를 낮췄다. (The government lowered tariffs on some items to stabilize prices.)

Q3What does 금융업 mean?

  • ① foreign
  • ② national border
  • ③ mining industry
  • ④ financial industry

금융업 means financial industry; banking business. 정부는 금융업 혁신을 위한 규제 완화를 추진했다. (The government pushed for deregulation to foster innovation in the financial industry.)

Q4Which Korean word means "fund"?

  • ① 기금
  • ② 이주
  • ③ 관세
  • ④ 급락

기금 means fund; endowment. 정부는 중소기업 지원 기금을 대폭 확충했다. (The government significantly expanded the fund supporting small businesses.)

Q5What does 낙후 mean?

  • ① excess
  • ② falling behind
  • ③ sharp rise
  • ④ multinational

낙후 means falling behind; underdevelopment. 낙후 지역의 경제를 살리기 위한 지원 사업이 추진된다. (Support projects are promoted to revive the economy of underdeveloped areas.)

Q6Which Korean word means "elimination"?

  • ① 금융업
  • ② 기틀
  • ③ 도태
  • ④ 이주민

도태 means elimination; selection; weed out. 치열한 경쟁 속에서 부실한 기업들은 자연스럽게 도태되었다. (In fierce competition, insolvent companies were naturally weeded out.)
