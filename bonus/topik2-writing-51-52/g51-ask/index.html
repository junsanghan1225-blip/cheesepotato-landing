퀴즈 Quiz

Q1Which formula is the most polite and natural way to ask for information in an inquiry post in Q51?

  • ① 가르쳐 줘.
  • ② 알려 주시면 감사하겠습니다.
  • ③ 알려줘라.
  • ④ 빨리 말해.

'알려 주시면 감사하겠습니다' (I would be grateful if you let me know) is the standard polite request formula in Q51 writing.

Q2How do you honorifically express 'I am asking you' when writing to an office or teacher?

  • ① 물어봅니다
  • ② 여쭤봅니다
  • ③ 묻는다
  • ④ 말한다

여쭤보다 is the humble/honorific verb for 묻다 (to ask), used when inquiring to someone respected.

Q3Which sentence ending appropriately closes an application inquiry email asking for a reply?

  • ① 답변 기다릴게.
  • ② 빠른 답변 부탁드립니다.
  • ③ 답변해라.
  • ④ 답변을 썼다.

'빠른 답변 부탁드립니다' (Please reply promptly) is the standard deferential closing in formal inquiries.

Q4Fill in the blank: '개인 사정으로 수업 시간을 변경할 수 있는지 여쭤보고 ___.'

  • ① 싶습니다
  • ② 싶다
  • ③ 싶어
  • ④ 싶겠니

The writer expresses their polite desire to ask using deferential -고 싶습니다.

Q5Which phrase states an intention to sign up for a training course?

  • ① 강좌를 취소했습니다.
  • ② 강좌를 신청하려고 합니다.
  • ③ 강좌가 끝났습니다.
  • ④ 강좌를 가르칩니다.

'-(으)려고 합니다' expresses the writer's goal or intention to register for the course.
