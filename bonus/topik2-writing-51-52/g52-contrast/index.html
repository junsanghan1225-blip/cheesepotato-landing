퀴즈 Quiz

Q1Which transition word in Q52 indicates contrast between two opposing situations?

  • ① 따라서
  • ② 반면에
  • ③ 그러므로
  • ④ 왜냐하면

'반면에' (on the other hand) is the standard connector used to introduce an opposing or contrasting perspective.

Q2Fill in the blank for a contrastive sentence: '낮에는 기온이 높지만 밤에는 ___ 기온이 떨어진다.'

  • ① 반대로
  • ② 따라서
  • ③ 왜냐하면
  • ④ 즉

'반대로' (conversely) or '반면에' appropriately contrasts the warm daytime with the cold nighttime.

Q3Which phrase serves as a formal caution against side effects in Q52 plain form?

  • ① 부작용이 없도록 주의해야 한다.
  • ② 부작용이 없도록 주의하세요.
  • ③ 부작용이 없도록 주의해라.
  • ④ 부작용이 없도록 주의합니다.

'-아야/어야 한다' is the standard plain form expressing obligation or necessity in expository essays.

Q4What nuance does '오히려 역효과를 낳을 수 있다' express?

  • ① It will definitely succeed.
  • ② Contrary to expectation, it may produce an adverse, opposite result.
  • ③ It has no effect whatsoever.
  • ④ It was intended from the beginning.

오히려 ('on the contrary') combined with 역효과 ('adverse effect') highlights an unintended negative backlash.

Q5Which sentence ending is inappropriately casual for Q52?

  • ① 건강을 해칠 우려가 있다.
  • ② 무리한 다이어트는 피해야 해.
  • ③ 신중한 판단이 필요하다.
  • ④ 균형 있는 식단이 요구된다.

피해야 해 uses casual 반말; in Q52 expository prose, plain form (피해야 한다) is mandatory.
