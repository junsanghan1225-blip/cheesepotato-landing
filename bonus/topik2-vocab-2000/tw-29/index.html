퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 여건 mean?

  • ① mind and body
  • ② condition
  • ③ intake
  • ④ to be vivid

여건 means condition; circumstances. 근로자의 열악한 근무 여건을 개선할 필요가 있다. (There is a need to improve workers' poor working conditions.)

Q2Which Korean word means "risk"?

  • ① 위급하다
  • ② 여전하다
  • ③ 위험성
  • ④ 여건

위험성 means risk; danger. 의사는 환자에게 흡연의 치명적 위험성을 경고했다. (The doctor warned the patient of the fatal risks of smoking.)

Q3What does 심신 mean?

  • ① around the mouth
  • ② clearly
  • ③ prevention
  • ④ mind and body

심신 means mind and body. 명상과 규칙적인 운동은 심신의 안정을 유지해 준다. (Meditation and regular exercise maintain the stability of mind and body.)

Q4Which Korean word means "wonder drug"?

  • ① 신약
  • ② 향기롭다
  • ③ 위험성
  • ④ 헌혈

신약 means wonder drug; miracle drug. 제약회사는 수년간의 임상 시험 끝에 신약 허가를 받았다. (The pharmaceutical company received new drug approval after years of clinical trials.)

Q5What does 시리다 mean?

  • ① to be recognized
  • ② to be cold
  • ③ medical care
  • ④ infectious disease

시리다 means to be cold; to be chilled. 칼바람이 불어와 손발이 시리고 귀가 얼얼했다. (As biting wind blew, hands and feet were chilled and ears stung.)

Q6Which Korean word means "to remain unchanged"?

  • ① 심신
  • ② 섭취
  • ③ 여전하다
  • ④ 피어오르다

여전하다 means to remain unchanged. 세월이 십 년이나 흘렀지만 고향 풍경은 여전하다. (Though ten years have passed, the hometown landscape remains unchanged.)
