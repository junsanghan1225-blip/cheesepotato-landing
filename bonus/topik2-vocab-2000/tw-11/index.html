Q1What does 곧장 mean?

  • ① driver's license
  • ② straight
  • ③ job
  • ④ report

곧장 means straight; directly. 이 길을 따라 곧장 걸어가면 사거리가 나온다. (If you walk straight along this path, an intersection appears.)

Q2Which Korean word means "souvenir"?

  • ① 신입
  • ② 진행
  • ③ 기념품
  • ④ 곧장

기념품 means souvenir; keepsake. 행사 참석자 전원에게 소정의 기념품을 증정했다. (A small keepsake was presented to all attendees of the event.)

Q3What does 운전면허 mean?

  • ① printing
  • ② delay
  • ③ admission fee
  • ④ driver's license

운전면허 means driver's license. 차량을 운전할 때는 반드시 운전면허 소지가 필요하다. (When driving a vehicle, possession of a driver's license is definitely required.)

Q4Which Korean word means "to step on"?

  • ① 밟다
  • ② 회의실
  • ③ 기념품
  • ④ 막차

밟다 means to step on; to pedal. 그는 고향 땅을 다시 밟고 깊은 감격에 젖었다. (He stepped on his hometown soil again and was filled with deep emotion.)

Q5What does 구하다 mean?

  • ① course
  • ② look for
  • ③ means of transportation
  • ④ lead

구하다 means look for; obtain. 소방관은 불길 속에서 소중한 인명을 용감하게 구했다. (The firefighter bravely rescued precious human lives from flames.)

Q6Which Korean word means "progress"?

  • ① 운전면허
  • ② 일자리
  • ③ 진행
  • ④ 신청서

진행 means progress. 공사의 원활한 진행을 위해 안전 점검을 실시했다. (Safety inspections were conducted for smooth progress of construction.)
