Q1What does 년생 mean?

  • ① attractiveness
  • ② born in the year of
  • ③ sight
  • ④ elementary class

년생 means born in the year of. 그는 팔십년생 동갑내기 친구들과 오랜 우정을 이어오고 있다. (He has maintained long friendship with peers born in 1980.)

Q2Which Korean word means "old bachelor"?

  • ① 곱슬머리
  • ② 강당
  • ③ 노총각
  • ④ 년생

노총각 means old bachelor; unmarried older man. 명절마다 친척들은 노총각 조카에게 언제 장가를 갈 것인지 물었다. (Every holiday, relatives asked the bachelor nephew when he would get married.)

Q3What does 매력적 mean?

  • ① bald head
  • ② meal service
  • ③ peer
  • ④ attractiveness

매력적 means attractiveness. 도시의 고유한 전통과 현대적 감각의 조화는 관광객을 끄는 매력적 요소이다. (The harmony of urban unique traditions and modern sensibilities is an appealing element attracting tourists.)

Q4Which Korean word means "outward appearance"?

  • ① 겉보기
  • ② 교무실
  • ③ 노총각
  • ④ 거인

겉보기 means outward appearance; surface look. 그 물건은 겉보기와 달리 내구성이 매우 약했다. (That item, unlike its surface look, was very weak in durability.)

Q5What does 낯 mean?

  • ① old maid
  • ② face
  • ③ wrinkle
  • ④ familiar

낯 means face; countenance; honor; shame. 그는 큰 실수를 저지른 뒤 사람들을 볼 낯이 없었다. (After committing a big mistake, he had no face to see people.)

Q6Which Korean word means "auditorium"?

  • ① 매력적
  • ② 꼴
  • ③ 강당
  • ④ 공교육

강당 means auditorium; lecture hall; hall. 학교 강당에서는 전교생을 대상으로 한 명사 초청 강연이 진행되었다. (In the school auditorium, a guest celebrity lecture was held for all students.)
