퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 금리 mean?

  • ① district office
  • ② interest rate
  • ③ volunteer work
  • ④ exposure

금리 means interest rate. 중앙은행은 물가를 안정시키기 위해 기준 금리를 전격 인상했다. (The central bank decisively raised benchmark interest rates to stabilize prices.)

Q2Which Korean word means "flooding"?

  • ① 연대
  • ② 기부금
  • ③ 침수
  • ④ 금리

침수 means flooding; submersion. 지자체는 집중호우에 대비하여 지하차도 침수 방지 시설을 점검했다. (Local government checked underpass flood prevention facilities against heavy rains.)

Q3What does 구청 mean?

  • ① the public
  • ② disaster
  • ③ obstacle
  • ④ district office

구청 means district office. 구청은 주민 편의를 위해 민원 창구를 늘렸다. (The district office expanded civil service counters for resident convenience.)

Q4Which Korean word means "ordinary people"?

  • ① 서민
  • ② 악순환
  • ③ 침수
  • ④ 획일적

서민 means ordinary people; commoner. 정부는 서민들의 주거 안정을 위한 임대주택을 확대한다. (The government expands rental housing for residential stability of common citizens.)

Q5What does 악영향 mean?

  • ① wholesale
  • ② bad influence
  • ③ welfare
  • ④ the weak

악영향 means bad influence. 환경 오염은 생태계의 균형에 치명적인 악영향을 준다. (Environmental pollution brings fatal adverse effects to ecosystem balance.)

Q6Which Korean word means "donation"?

  • ① 구청
  • ② 자원봉사
  • ③ 기부금
  • ④ 붕괴

기부금 means donation; contribution; charity fund. 익명의 독지가가 난치병 환자들을 위해 거액의 기부금을 쾌척했다. (An anonymous benefactor donated a large sum of charity funds for patients with incurable diseases.)
