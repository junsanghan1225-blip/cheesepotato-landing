퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 되새기다 mean?

  • ① behave
  • ② to reflect on
  • ③ to calm down
  • ④ feeling of rejection

되새기다 means to reflect on; to ruminate. 우리는 지난 실패의 교훈을 다시금 되새겨야 한다. (We must once again ruminate on the lessons of past failure.)

Q2Which Korean word means "blankly"?

  • ① 미어지다
  • ② 감격스럽다
  • ③ 물끄러미
  • ④ 되새기다

물끄러미 means blankly; vacantly. 그는 아무 말 없이 옛 친구의 뒷모습을 물끄러미 지켜보았다. (Without a word, he silently watched the receding figure of his old friend.)

Q3What does 굴다 mean?

  • ① bitterish
  • ② whole
  • ③ thought (of doing)
  • ④ behave

굴다 means behave. 그는 어른들 앞에서는 언제나 예의 바르게 굴었다. (He always behaved politely in front of elders.)

Q4Which Korean word means "to be excited"?

  • ① 들뜨다
  • ② 거르다
  • ③ 물끄러미
  • ④ 골칫거리

들뜨다 means to be excited; to be agitated. 축제를 앞두고 거리 전체가 들뜬 분위기에 휩싸였다. (Ahead of the festival, the whole street was wrapped in an excited atmosphere.)

Q5What does 감돌다 mean?

  • ① to be utmost
  • ② to linger
  • ③ to be tough
  • ④ to be marvelous

감돌다 means to linger; to hover. 중요한 결정을 앞둔 회의실에 무거운 긴장감이 감돌았다. (Heavy tension lingered in the conference room ahead of a critical decision.)

Q6Which Korean word means "deeply moving"?

  • ① 굴다
  • ② 가라앉히다
  • ③ 감격스럽다
  • ④ 식단

감격스럽다 means deeply moving; overwhelming. 선수는 금메달을 목에 걸고 감격스러운 눈물을 흘렸다. (The athlete shed deeply touching tears with the gold medal around his neck.)
