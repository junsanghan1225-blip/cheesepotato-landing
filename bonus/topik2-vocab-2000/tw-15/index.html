퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 향 mean?

  • ① to tear
  • ② scent
  • ③ to come running
  • ④ regular customer

향 means scent; incense. 제사를 지낼 때 경건한 마음으로 향을 피웠다. (During memorial services, incense was lit with a devout mind.)

Q2Which Korean word means "expense"?

  • ① 구르다
  • ② 내밀다
  • ③ 지출
  • ④ 향

지출 means expense; expenditure. 물가 급등으로 인해 가계의 식비 지출이 크게 늘었다. (Due to the price surge, household food expenditures increased significantly.)

Q3What does 뜯다 mean?

  • ① returning home
  • ② long period of time
  • ③ circumstances
  • ④ to tear

뜯다 means to tear; to pluck. 봄이 오자 들판에서 파릇파릇한 나물을 뜯었다. (As spring came, they plucked fresh wild greens in the field.)

Q4Which Korean word means "replacement"?

  • ① 교체
  • ② 순간적
  • ③ 지출
  • ④ 잠들다

교체 means replacement; substitution. 감독은 경기 후반에 지친 선수를 신속히 교체했다. (The coach promptly replaced the tired player in the late second half.)

Q5What does 쫓아가다 mean?

  • ① classic
  • ② to chase
  • ③ to seize
  • ④ to bring

쫓아가다 means to chase; to follow after. 경찰은 달아나는 도둑의 뒤를 끝까지 쫓아갔다. (Police chased after the fleeing thief to the end.)

Q6Which Korean word means "protrude"?

  • ① 뜯다
  • ② 달려오다
  • ③ 내밀다
  • ④ 헌

내밀다 means protrude; stick out; extend. 그는 자신의 결백을 입증할 서류를 조사관 앞에 당당히 내밀었다. (He confidently presented documents proving his innocence before the investigator.)
