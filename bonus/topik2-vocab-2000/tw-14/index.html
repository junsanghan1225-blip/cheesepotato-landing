퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 소규모 mean?

  • ① individual
  • ② small scale
  • ③ close place
  • ④ symbol

소규모 means small scale; small-scale. 정부는 위기에 처한 소규모 자영업자들을 지원했다. (The government provided assistance to small-scale self-employed in crisis.)

Q2Which Korean word means "that much"?

  • ① 절반
  • ② 숙이다
  • ③ 그만큼
  • ④ 소규모

그만큼 means that much. 자유가 주어지는 만큼 책임의 무게 또한 그만큼 무거워진다. (As much as freedom is granted, the weight of responsibility also grows that much heavier.)

Q3What does 개별 mean?

  • ① straight
  • ② invitation
  • ③ incessant
  • ④ individual

개별 means individual; separate. 단체 여행 중에는 허락 없는 개별 행동을 삼가야 한다. (During group travels, one should refrain from unauthorized separate action.)

Q4Which Korean word means "one by one"?

  • ① 일일이
  • ② 보존
  • ③ 그만큼
  • ④ 밀리미터

일일이 means one by one; individually. 선생님은 학생들의 질문에 일일이 친절하게 답변해 주셨다. (The teacher kindly answered students' questions one by one.)

Q5What does 밑바닥 mean?

  • ① very (used with a negative)
  • ② bottom
  • ③ as many as
  • ④ opposite side

밑바닥 means bottom; bottom floor. 그는 밑바닥 생활에서 시작하여 자수성가했다. (He started from rock bottom life and became self-made.)

Q6Which Korean word means "to bow"?

  • ① 개별
  • ② 눈앞
  • ③ 숙이다
  • ④ 엮다

숙이다 means to bow; to bend down. 그는 자신의 잘못을 솔직히 인정하고 고개를 숙였다. (He honestly admitted his mistake and bowed his head.)
