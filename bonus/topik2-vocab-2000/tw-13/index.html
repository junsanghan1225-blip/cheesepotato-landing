퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 멀쩡하다 mean?

  • ① opportunity
  • ② to be intact
  • ③ as time goes on
  • ④ exceptionally

멀쩡하다 means to be intact; to be unscathed. 그는 큰 사고를 당했음에도 몸이 멀쩡했다. (Despite suffering a major accident, his body was completely fine.)

Q2Which Korean word means "to ache"?

  • ① 형성
  • ② 각종
  • ③ 쑤시다
  • ④ 멀쩡하다

쑤시다 means to ache; to throb. 온종일 무거운 짐을 날랐더니 온몸 구석구석이 쑤셨다. (Carrying heavy loads all day, every corner of my body throbbed.)

Q3What does 계기 mean?

  • ① being fundamental
  • ② around
  • ③ to suffer from
  • ④ opportunity

계기 means opportunity; chance. 그 실패는 그가 더 크게 성장하는 중요한 계기가 되었다. (That failure became an important turning point for him to grow bigger.)

Q4Which Korean word means "by itself"?

  • ① 저절로
  • ② 극히
  • ③ 쑤시다
  • ④ 단계

저절로 means by itself; naturally; automatically. 좋은 음악을 들으니 입가에 미소가 저절로 번졌다. (Listening to good music, a smile spread naturally across my lips.)

Q5What does 대비 mean?

  • ① consciousness
  • ② comparison
  • ③ classification
  • ④ fixation

대비 means comparison; contrast. 자연재해에 대비해 비상 대피 훈련을 철저히 실시했다. (They thoroughly conducted emergency evacuation drills in preparation for natural disasters.)

Q6Which Korean word means "varieties"?

  • ① 계기
  • ② 갈수록
  • ③ 각종
  • ④ 그리

각종 means varieties; all kinds; all sorts. 백화점은 각종 신상품을 한눈에 볼 수 있도록 진열했다. (The department store displayed all kinds of new items for a glance.)
