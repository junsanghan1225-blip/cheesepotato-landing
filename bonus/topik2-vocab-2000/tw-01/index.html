퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 되찾다 mean?

  • ① prevention
  • ② to recover
  • ③ wrist
  • ④ symptom

되찾다 means to recover; to regain. 어려운 상황에서도 평정심을 되찾아야 한다. (One must regain composure even in difficult situations.)

Q2Which Korean word means "memory"?

  • ① 처방
  • ② 몸살
  • ③ 기억력
  • ④ 되찾다

기억력 means memory. 규칙적인 독서와 운동은 노년기 기억력 증진에 좋다. (Regular reading and exercise are good for improving memory in old age.)

Q3What does 예방 mean?

  • ① break
  • ② difference
  • ③ motion
  • ④ prevention

예방 means prevention. 산업 재해를 줄이기 위해서는 철저한 사전 예방 수칙 준수가 필수적이다. (To reduce industrial accidents, thorough adherence to prior prevention rules is essential.)

Q4Which Korean word means "otolaryngology"?

  • ① 이비인후과
  • ② 비하다
  • ③ 기억력
  • ④ 안과

이비인후과 means otolaryngology; ENT clinic. 환자는 심한 비염 증상으로 이비인후과에서 진료를 받았다. (The patient received treatment at the ENT clinic for severe rhinitis symptoms.)

Q5What does 손등 mean?

  • ① lean on
  • ② back of the hand
  • ③ liquid medicine
  • ④ whole body

손등 means back of the hand. 아이는 흐르는 눈물을 손등으로 쓱 훔쳤다. (The child wiped flowing tears with the back of the hand.)

Q6Which Korean word means "body aching all over"?

  • ① 예방
  • ② 손목
  • ③ 몸살
  • ④ 점차

몸살 means body aching all over. 몸살 기운이 있을 때는 푹 쉬며 땀을 내는 것이 좋다. (When having flu ache symptoms, it is good to rest well and sweat.)
