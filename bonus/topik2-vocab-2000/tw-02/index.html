퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 신약 mean?

  • ① healing completely
  • ② wonder drug
  • ③ hypertension
  • ④ blood

신약 means wonder drug; miracle drug. 제약회사는 수년간의 임상 시험 끝에 신약 허가를 받았다. (The pharmaceutical company received new drug approval after years of clinical trials.)

Q2Which Korean word means "to be cold"?

  • ① 경과
  • ② 덧나다
  • ③ 시리다
  • ④ 신약

시리다 means to be cold; to be chilled. 칼바람이 불어와 손발이 시리고 귀가 얼얼했다. (As biting wind blew, hands and feet were chilled and ears stung.)

Q3What does 완치 mean?

  • ① diabetes
  • ② lungs
  • ③ infectious disease
  • ④ healing completely

완치 means healing completely. 초기 발견과 적절한 치료가 병행된다면 암 환자의 완치율을 크게 높일 수 있다. (If early detection and appropriate treatment are paired, complete cure rates of cancer patients can be greatly increased.)

Q4Which Korean word means "infection"?

  • ① 감염
  • ② 치아
  • ③ 시리다
  • ④ 한의원

감염 means infection; contagion. 병원 측은 바이러스의 추가 감염 경로를 철저히 차단했다. (The hospital thoroughly blocked further infection routes of the virus.)

Q5What does 구급차 mean?

  • ① intake
  • ② ambulance
  • ③ emergency
  • ④ intractable disease

구급차 means ambulance. 구급차가 사이렌을 울리며 지나가자 차량들이 길을 양보했다. (As the ambulance passed sounding its siren, vehicles yielded the way.)

Q6Which Korean word means "to fester"?

  • ① 완치
  • ② 고혈압
  • ③ 덧나다
  • ④ 입가

덧나다 means to fester; to get infected; to worsen. 환자가 제때 치료를 받지 않아 가벼웠던 염증이 심하게 덧났다. (Because the patient was not treated in time, mild inflammation worsened severely.)
