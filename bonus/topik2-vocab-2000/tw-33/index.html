퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 동문 mean?

  • ① course
  • ② alumnus
  • ③ taking an exam
  • ④ learning

동문 means alumnus; fellow graduate; schoolmate. 그는 같은 고등학교 동문으로서 사회 여러 분야에서 서로 협력했다. (As schoolmates from the same high school, they cooperated across diverse fields.)

Q2Which Korean word means "demonstration"?

  • ① 수료
  • ② 통학
  • ③ 시범
  • ④ 동문

시범 means demonstration; trial. 새로운 시스템의 시범 운영을 성공리에 마쳤다. (They successfully finished the trial operation of the new system.)

Q3What does 강좌 mean?

  • ① reading room
  • ② private education
  • ③ concentration
  • ④ course

강좌 means course; lecture series. 대학은 시민을 위한 교양 강좌를 개설했다. (The university opened a liberal arts lecture series for citizens.)

Q4Which Korean word means "written language"?

  • ① 문어
  • ② 명사
  • ③ 시범
  • ④ 낙방

문어 means written language; literary style. 공식 보고서나 학술 논문에는 문어 표현을 활용한다. (Written expressions are utilized in official reports and academic papers.)

Q5What does 어미 mean?

  • ① bullying
  • ② (grammatical) ending
  • ③ numerical figure
  • ④ language study

어미 means (grammatical) ending; mother animal. 어미 새는 둥지 속 새끼들에게 부지런히 먹이를 날랐다. (The mother bird diligently carried food to the babies in the nest.)

Q6Which Korean word means "commuting to school"?

  • ① 강좌
  • ② 수험
  • ③ 통학
  • ④ 강연

통학 means commuting to school. 외곽 지역 학생들을 위해 전용 통학 버스를 운영한다. (Dedicated school buses are operated for students in suburban areas.)
