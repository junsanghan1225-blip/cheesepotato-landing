퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 활기차다 mean?

  • ① to be at a loss
  • ② to be energetic
  • ③ to be tough
  • ④ fusion

활기차다 means to be energetic; to be lively. 시장 골목에는 상인들의 활기찬 목소리가 넘쳐난다. (Merchant's energetic voices overflow in the market alleys.)

Q2Which Korean word means "(stamping) repeatedly"?

  • ① 차분하다
  • ② 조개
  • ③ 동동
  • ④ 활기차다

동동 means (stamping) repeatedly; bobbing; floating lightly. 맑은 연못 위에 떨어진 단풍잎이 동동 떠다닌다. (Maple leaves that fell on the clear pond float lightly about.)

Q3What does 막막하다 mean?

  • ① to salt
  • ② to stimulate
  • ③ anxiety
  • ④ to be at a loss

막막하다 means to be at a loss; to be bleak. 갑작스러운 사고 소식에 무엇을 할지 막막하였다. (Hearing the sudden news of the accident, he was at a loss what to do.)

Q4Which Korean word means "to stroke"?

  • ① 어루만지다
  • ② 곡물
  • ③ 동동
  • ④ 뽐내다

어루만지다 means to stroke; to caress. 따뜻한 위로의 한마디가 상처받은 마음을 어루만졌다. (A single word of warm comfort soothed the wounded heart.)

Q5What does 판 mean?

  • ① to be heartwarming
  • ② counter for pizza and games
  • ③ invariably
  • ④ plentifully

판 means counter for pizza and games; board; scene. 노인들이 공원에서 바둑판을 사이에 두고 마주 앉았다. (The elderly sat facing each other across a baduk board in the park.)

Q6Which Korean word means "clam"?

  • ① 막막하다
  • ② 질기다
  • ③ 조개
  • ④ 식수

조개 means clam; shellfish. 신선한 조개를 넣고 맑게 끓인 국물 맛이 일품이다. (The taste of clear broth boiled with fresh clams is superb.)
