퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 보증금 mean?

  • ① tile-roofed house
  • ② security deposit
  • ③ study
  • ④ to paint

보증금 means security deposit; guarantee money. 임대차 계약 만료 시 임차인은 보증금을 전액 돌려받을 권리가 있다. (Upon expiration of the lease contract, the lessee has the right to receive the deposit in full.)

Q2Which Korean word means "remodeling"?

  • ① 때다
  • ② 들어앉다
  • ③ 개조
  • ④ 보증금

개조 means remodeling; alteration; conversion. 건물의 내부 구조 개조를 통해 공간 효율을 극대화했다. (Through structural alteration of the building interior, space efficiency was maximized.)

Q3What does 기와집 mean?

  • ① single room
  • ② singing ability
  • ③ ring
  • ④ tile-roofed house

기와집 means tile-roofed house; traditional Korean tiled house. 과거 양반들은 웅장하고 품격 있는 기와집을 짓고 생활했다. (In the past, noblemen built grand and dignified tile-roofed houses and lived there.)

Q4Which Korean word means "to belong to"?

  • ① 딸리다
  • ② 가면
  • ③ 개조
  • ④ 남향

딸리다 means to belong to; to be attached to; to fall short. 그는 부양해야 할 딸린 식구가 많아 궂은일도 마다하지 않았다. (Having many attached family members to support, he did not refuse rough work.)

Q5What does 울타리 mean?

  • ① house
  • ② fence
  • ③ railing
  • ④ soundproofing

울타리 means fence; hedge. 가족은 험한 세상에서 든든한 울타리가 되어 준다. (Family serves as a dependable protective fence in a harsh world.)

Q6Which Korean word means "to stay indoors"?

  • ① 기와집
  • ② 서재
  • ③ 들어앉다
  • ④ 가곡

들어앉다 means to stay indoors; to hole up; to be settled in. 그는 퇴직 후 번화한 도심을 떠나 깊은 산골에 들어앉아 여생을 보냈다. (After retirement, he left the bustling city, settled in deep mountains, and spent the rest of his life.)
