Q1What does 외래 mean?

  • ① calligraphy
  • ② foreign
  • ③ to lead
  • ④ antique

외래 means foreign; outpatient. 외래 어종의 무분별한 유입은 고유 생태계를 교란한다. (Indiscriminate introduction of foreign species disrupts the native ecosystem.)

Q2Which Korean word means "aesthetic"?

  • ① 가야금
  • ② 고구려
  • ③ 미적
  • ④ 외래

미적 means aesthetic; artistic. 디자인의 발전은 대중의 미적 감각을 한 단계 끌어올리는 역할을 했다. (Advancement of design played a role in elevating public aesthetic sensibilities.)

Q3What does 서예 mean?

  • ① gye (traditional mutual aid club)
  • ② royal court
  • ③ brush
  • ④ calligraphy

서예 means calligraphy. 전통 서예는 한 획 한 획에 작가의 깊은 인품과 정신을 담아낸다. (Traditional calligraphy imbues the creator's deep character and spirit in every stroke.)

Q4Which Korean word means "traditional culture"?

  • ① 전통문화
  • ② 궁
  • ③ 미적
  • ④ 장례

전통문화 means traditional culture. 우리는 선조들이 남긴 소중한 전통문화를 계승하여 발전시켜야 한다. (We must inherit precious traditional cultures bequeathed by ancestors and develop them modernly.)

Q5What does 건국 mean?

  • ① beauty
  • ② founding of a nation
  • ③ orchestra
  • ④ family events (weddings and funerals)

건국 means founding of a nation; national foundation. 역사학자들은 고조선의 건국 이념에 대해 깊이 있게 연구했다. (Historians studied in depth the founding ideology of Gojoseon.)

Q6Which Korean word means "Goguryeo (ancient Korean kingdom)"?

  • ① 서예
  • ② 거느리다
  • ③ 고구려
  • ④ 관습

고구려 means Goguryeo (ancient Korean kingdom). 고구려는 광개토대왕 때 광대한 영토를 개척하여 전성기를 누렸다. (Goguryeo enjoyed its golden age pioneering vast territory under King Gwanggaeto.)
