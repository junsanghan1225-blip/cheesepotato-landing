퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 자극적 mean?

  • ① residential area
  • ② stimulating
  • ③ track and field
  • ④ goat

자극적 means stimulating; provocative. 자극적인 기사 제목은 독자의 눈길을 끌기 위한 수단이다. (Sensational article headlines are a means to draw readers' eyes.)

Q2Which Korean word means "to sew"?

  • ① 원작
  • ② 진화
  • ③ 꿰매다
  • ④ 자극적

꿰매다 means to sew; to stitch. 의사는 깊게 찢어진 환자의 상처 부위를 꼼꼼히 꿰맸다. (The doctor meticulously stitched the patient's deeply torn wound area.)

Q3What does 주거지 mean?

  • ① race
  • ② natural disaster
  • ③ rest one's head on
  • ④ residential area

주거지 means residential area; dwelling. 신도시 개발로 대규모 현대식 주거지가 조성되었다. (A large-scale modern residential area was established through new town development.)

Q4Which Korean word means "to show"?

  • ① 선보이다
  • ② 유해
  • ③ 꿰매다
  • ④ 울타리

선보이다 means to show; to showcase. 기업은 혁신적인 기술을 박람회에서 선보였다. (The enterprise showcased innovative technology at the expo.)

Q5What does 탈락 mean?

  • ① electric light
  • ② being eliminated
  • ③ resident
  • ④ failure

탈락 means being eliminated; failure. 축구 대표팀은 예선에서 1점 차이로 탈락했다. (The national soccer team was eliminated in the preliminaries by a 1-point gap.)

Q6Which Korean word means "development"?

  • ① 주거지
  • ② 육상
  • ③ 진화
  • ④ 소모

진화 means development; advancement; evolution. 인간은 오랜 세월을 거쳐 환경에 맞게 진화해 왔다. (Humans have evolved over a long time to fit the environment.)
