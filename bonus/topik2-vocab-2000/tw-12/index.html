퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 끝맺다 mean?

  • ① monotonous
  • ② end
  • ③ to point at
  • ④ dripping

끝맺다 means end; finish. 강연자는 청중에게 희망의 메시지를 전하며 발표를 끝맺었다. (The lecturer concluded the presentation delivering messages of hope to the audience.)

Q2Which Korean word means "to show"?

  • ① 들락거리다
  • ② 떠들썩하다
  • ③ 내보이다
  • ④ 끝맺다

내보이다 means to show; to display; to reveal. 그는 남들 앞에서 자신의 감정을 좀처럼 겉으로 내보이지 않았다. (He rarely revealed his emotions outward in front of others.)

Q3What does 단조롭다 mean?

  • ① to patch up
  • ② rush out
  • ③ to be pressed
  • ④ monotonous

단조롭다 means monotonous; dull; simple. 그의 음악은 화려한 기교 없이 다소 단조로운 리듬을 띠었다. (His music carried a somewhat monotonous rhythm without flamboyant technique.)

Q4Which Korean word means "to be turned inside out"?

  • ① 뒤집히다
  • ② 뛰쳐나가다
  • ③ 내보이다
  • ④ 뒤엎다

뒤집히다 means to be turned inside out; to capsize; to be overturned. 거센 폭풍우를 만난 작은 어선이 바다 한가운데서 뒤집히고 말았다. (The small fishing boat encountering a rough storm ended up capsizing at sea.)

Q5What does 들이밀다 mean?

  • ① to strike down
  • ② to push forward
  • ③ to beat
  • ④ to step on

들이밀다 means to push forward; to thrust into; to stick in. 그는 계약서에 서명하라며 서류를 내 눈앞에 불쑥 들이밀었다. (He abruptly thrust documents before my eyes telling me to sign.)

Q6Which Korean word means "to be noisy"?

  • ① 단조롭다
  • ② 들이대다
  • ③ 떠들썩하다
  • ④ 뛰어오다

떠들썩하다 means to be noisy; to be clamorous; to be bustling. 우승 소식이 전해지자 온 동네가 잔치 분위기로 떠들썩했다. (When victory news was conveyed, the whole village bustled with feast atmosphere.)
