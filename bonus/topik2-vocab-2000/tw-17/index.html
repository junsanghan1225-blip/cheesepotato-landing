퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 근사하다 mean?

  • ① to go too far
  • ② wonderful
  • ③ to feel choked up
  • ④ empty

근사하다 means wonderful; marvelous; splendid. 그는 멋진 정장을 차려입고 근사한 자태를 뽐내며 나타났다. (He appeared showing off a splendid appearance dressed in a sharp suit.)

Q2Which Korean word means "optimistic"?

  • ① 떳떳하다
  • ② 고단하다
  • ③ 낙관적
  • ④ 근사하다

낙관적 means optimistic. 정부는 미래 경제 전망에 대해 낙관적 견해를 유지했다. (The government maintained an optimistic view regarding future economic outlook.)

Q3What does 너무하다 mean?

  • ① to be desolate
  • ② anxiety
  • ③ secretly
  • ④ to go too far

너무하다 means to go too far; to be unreasonable; to be too harsh. 친구들은 그의 지나친 장난에 대해 너무하다고 비판했다. (Friends criticized his excessive prank as being too harsh.)

Q4Which Korean word means "thick"?

  • ① 두텁다
  • ② 동동
  • ③ 낙관적
  • ④ 되돌아보다

두텁다 means thick; deep; close (relationship/friendship). 두 나라는 오랜 교류를 통해 두터운 신뢰 관계를 쌓아 왔다. (The two nations have built a close relationship of trust through long exchanges.)

Q5What does 눈살 mean?

  • ① perseverance
  • ② brow
  • ③ to reverse
  • ④ to be sad

눈살 means brow; eyebrows. 공공장소에서의 무질서한 행동은 타인의 눈살을 찌푸리게 한다. (Disorderly behavior in public places makes others frown their brows.)

Q6Which Korean word means "to be exhausted"?

  • ① 너무하다
  • ② 찡하다
  • ③ 고단하다
  • ④ 훈훈하다

고단하다 means to be exhausted; to be weary. 퇴근길 사람들의 얼굴에 고단한 기색이 역력하다. (Signs of weariness are evident on the faces of people leaving work.)
