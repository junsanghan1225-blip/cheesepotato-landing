퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 폭설 mean?

  • ① existence
  • ② heavy snow
  • ③ expose
  • ④ belief

폭설 means heavy snow; high snowfall. 기상청은 강원 산간 지역에 대설경보와 폭설 주의를 당부했다. (The weather agency urged heavy snow warnings in Gangwon mountain areas.)

Q2Which Korean word means "enormous"?

  • ① 돌아보다
  • ② 속이다
  • ③ 엄청나다
  • ④ 폭설

엄청나다 means enormous; colossal; stupendous. 태풍으로 인해 농가가 엄청난 피해를 입었다. (Farming households suffered enormous damage due to the typhoon.)

Q3What does 존재 mean?

  • ① consideration
  • ② to be precious
  • ③ active
  • ④ existence

존재 means existence; presence. 인간은 사회적 관계 속에서 존재 의미를 찾는다. (Humans find the meaning of existence within social relations.)

Q4Which Korean word means "elaborately"?

  • ① 꼼꼼히
  • ② 풍기다
  • ③ 엄청나다
  • ④ 귀중하다

꼼꼼히 means elaborately; punctiliously; carefully. 직원은 서류의 오타와 오류를 꼼꼼히 살폈다. (The employee carefully inspected typos and errors in the document.)

Q5What does 마음먹다 mean?

  • ① valuably
  • ② make up one's mind
  • ③ to be very big
  • ④ straighten

마음먹다 means make up one's mind; be determined. 사람은 한 번 마음먹은 일은 끝까지 해내야 한다. (Once someone makes up one's mind, one must carry it through.)

Q6Which Korean word means "deceive"?

  • ① 존재
  • ② 드러내다
  • ③ 속이다
  • ④ 완벽

속이다 means deceive. 가게 주인은 원산지를 속여 팔다가 적발되었다. (The store owner was caught selling falsely labeling origins.)
