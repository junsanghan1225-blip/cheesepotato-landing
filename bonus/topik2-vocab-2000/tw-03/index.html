Q1What does 하체 mean?

  • ① relieve oneself (urinate or defecate)
  • ② lower body
  • ③ disappear
  • ④ to pull up

하체 means lower body. 규칙적인 걷기 운동은 튼튼한 하체를 만드는 데 좋다. (Regular walking exercise is good for building a strong lower body.)

Q2Which Korean word means "general"?

  • ① 화상
  • ② 노화
  • ③ 대장
  • ④ 하체

대장 means general; leader; chief; large intestine. 의사는 내시경 검사를 통해 환자의 대장 상태를 정밀하게 살폈다. (The doctor closely examined the condition of the patient's large intestine via endoscopy.)

Q3What does 누다 mean?

  • ① rumbling (stomach sound)
  • ② continuation
  • ③ spinning (dizzily)
  • ④ relieve oneself (urinate or defecate)

누다 means relieve oneself (urinate or defecate). 의사는 환자에게 소변을 하루 몇 번 누는지 물었다. (The doctor asked the patient how many times a day they urinate.)

Q4Which Korean word means "blood pressure"?

  • ① 혈압
  • ② 지속적
  • ③ 대장
  • ④ 우울증

혈압 means blood pressure. 규칙적인 유산소 운동은 정상 혈압을 유지해 준다. (Regular aerobic exercise helps maintain normal blood pressure.)

Q5What does 감기다 mean?

  • ① armpit
  • ② be shut-eyed
  • ③ paralysis
  • ④ forgetfulness

감기다 means be shut-eyed. 피로가 누적된 환자는 약을 먹자마자 조용히 눈이 감겼다. (The patient with accumulated fatigue closed his eyes quietly as soon as he took medicine.)

Q6Which Korean word means "aging"?

  • ① 누다
  • ② 가시다
  • ③ 노화
  • ④ 전환

노화 means aging; getting old; senescence. 피부 노화를 방지하려면 외출 시 자외선 차단제를 꼼꼼히 발라야 한다. (To prevent skin aging, one must apply sunscreen carefully when going out.)
