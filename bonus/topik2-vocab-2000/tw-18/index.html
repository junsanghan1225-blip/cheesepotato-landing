퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 도전 mean?

  • ① glance
  • ② challenge
  • ③ to be wasteful
  • ④ to revive

도전 means challenge. 끝없는 도전과 열정이 마침내 역사적인 기록을 낳았다. (Endless challenges and passion finally produced a historic record.)

Q2Which Korean word means "practice"?

  • ① 서운하다
  • ② 끌어안다
  • ③ 실천
  • ④ 도전

실천 means practice; action. 작은 환경 보호 습관을 일상에서 실천하는 태도가 중요하다. (The attitude of practicing small environmental protection habits in daily life is important.)

Q3What does 눈길 mean?

  • ① resolution
  • ② flutter
  • ③ make someone go ahead
  • ④ glance

눈길 means glance. 폭설이 내린 뒤 얼어붙은 눈길에서는 감속 운전이 필수적이다. (On snowy roads frozen after heavy snow, reduced-speed driving is essential.)

Q4Which Korean word means "to be forgotten"?

  • ① 잊히다
  • ② 마음속
  • ③ 실천
  • ④ 웃음소리

잊히다 means to be forgotten. 그 사건은 사람들의 기억 속에서 점차 잊혀졌다. (That incident was gradually forgotten in people's memories.)

Q5What does 안타까워하다 mean?

  • ① confront
  • ② feel sorry
  • ③ to be regrettable
  • ④ to sink down

안타까워하다 means feel sorry. 주민들은 화재로 집을 잃은 이웃을 몹시 안타까워했다. (Residents felt deeply sorry for neighbors who lost houses in the fire.)

Q6Which Korean word means "hug"?

  • ① 눈길
  • ② 아깝다
  • ③ 끌어안다
  • ④ 마냥

끌어안다 means hug; embrace. 그는 모든 고통을 혼자 끌어안고 묵묵히 견뎠다. (He took on all pain alone and endured silently.)
