퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 감성 mean?

  • ① to be overwhelmed
  • ② sensibility
  • ③ resignation
  • ④ sentimental

감성 means sensibility; sensitivity. 작가는 서정적인 필치로 독자들의 섬세한 감성을 자극했다. (The author stimulated readers' delicate sensibilities with lyrical touches.)

Q2Which Korean word means "to show off"?

  • ① 욕구
  • ② 감미롭다
  • ③ 뽐내다
  • ④ 감성

뽐내다 means to show off; to boast. 자신의 재주를 지나치게 뽐내면 반감을 살 수 있다. (Excessively bragging about one's talent may provoke animosity.)

Q3What does 벅차다 mean?

  • ① deep emotion
  • ② admiration
  • ③ to stroke
  • ④ to be overwhelmed

벅차다 means to be overwhelmed; to be beyond one's power; to be full (with joy). 어려운 역경을 딛고 우승 트로피를 안자 가슴 벅찬 감동이 밀려왔다. (Embracing the championship trophy overcoming hardships, overwhelming emotion surged in his heart.)

Q4Which Korean word means "sense of alienation"?

  • ① 소외감
  • ② 감정적
  • ③ 뽐내다
  • ④ 상실

소외감 means sense of alienation; feeling of isolation. 급격한 디지털화 속에서 노년층이 겪는 정보 소외감을 해소해야 한다. (The sense of digital alienation experienced by seniors amidst rapid digitalization must be resolved.)

Q5What does 간절히 mean?

  • ① invariably
  • ② earnestly
  • ③ to be proud
  • ④ treasuring

간절히 means earnestly; desperately; ardently. 그는 조국의 평화적 통일을 마음 깊이 간절히 염원했다. (He ardently prayed deep in his heart for peaceful reunification of his fatherland.)

Q6Which Korean word means "sweet"?

  • ① 벅차다
  • ② 체념
  • ③ 감미롭다
  • ④ 감성적

감미롭다 means sweet; melodious; mellow. 가수의 감미로운 목소리가 조용한 밤공기를 부드럽게 감쌌다. (The singer's melodious voice softly enveloped the quiet night air.)
