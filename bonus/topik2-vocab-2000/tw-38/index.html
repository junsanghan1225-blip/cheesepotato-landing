Q1What does 공공시설 mean?

  • ① persuasiveness
  • ② public facility
  • ③ mistake
  • ④ decisive

공공시설 means public facility; public infrastructure. 지자체는 주민 안전을 위해 노후 공공시설을 대대적으로 보수했다. (Local authorities extensively repaired deteriorated public facilities for resident safety.)

Q2Which Korean word means "feigned innocence"?

  • ① 신중히
  • ② 간접
  • ③ 시치미
  • ④ 공공시설

시치미 means feigned innocence; dissimulation. 그는 잘못을 저지르고도 뻔뻔하게 시치미를 뗐다. (He feigned innocence brazenly even after committing a mistake.)

Q3What does 설득력 mean?

  • ① as much as possible
  • ② terminal
  • ③ strongly
  • ④ persuasiveness

설득력 means persuasiveness. 그의 주장은 논리적인 근거가 부족하여 설득력을 잃었다. (His argument lacked logical grounds and lost persuasiveness.)

Q4Which Korean word means "essential"?

  • ① 본질적
  • ② 출처
  • ③ 시치미
  • ④ 매사

본질적 means essential; fundamental; intrinsic. 두 견해 사이에는 본질적 차이가 존재한다. (An essential difference exists between the two viewpoints.)

Q5What does 창의적 mean?

  • ① official
  • ② creative
  • ③ being complete
  • ④ reflection

창의적 means creative. 복잡한 문제일수록 창의적 접근법이 돌파구를 마련한다. (With complex problems, creative approaches provide breakthroughs.)

Q6Which Korean word means "indirectness"?

  • ① 설득력
  • ② 오류
  • ③ 간접
  • ④ 거부

간접 means indirectness. 공공장소에서의 간접흡연 피해를 방지하기 위해 금연 구역을 확대했다. (To prevent secondhand smoke damages in public spaces, nonsmoking zones were expanded.)
