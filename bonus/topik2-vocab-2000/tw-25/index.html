Q1What does 개막식 mean?

  • ① diving
  • ② opening ceremony
  • ③ organism
  • ④ goat

개막식 means opening ceremony. 화려한 불꽃놀이와 함께 동계 올림픽 개막식이 거행되었다. (The opening ceremony of the Winter Olympics was held with fancy fireworks.)

Q2Which Korean word means "gold medal"?

  • ① 따라잡다
  • ② 진화
  • ③ 금메달
  • ④ 개막식

금메달 means gold medal. 그는 혹독한 훈련을 묵묵히 이겨내고 시상대 맨 위에서 금메달을 목에 걸었다. (He quietly endured severe training and hung the gold medal around his neck.)

Q3What does 다이빙 mean?

  • ① to fly in
  • ② giraffe
  • ③ to fish
  • ④ diving

다이빙 means diving. 선수는 공중에서 완벽한 회전을 선보이며 멋진 다이빙 연기를 펼쳤다. (The athlete presented a splendid diving performance showcasing perfect rotations in the air.)

Q4Which Korean word means "mountain climbing"?

  • ① 등반
  • ② 공룡
  • ③ 금메달
  • ④ 대결

등반 means mountain climbing; mountaineering; ascent. 원정대는 혹독한 날씨를 뚫고 에베레스트 정상 등반에 성공했다. (The expedition succeeded in mountain climbing to the summit of Everest.)

Q5What does 곤충 mean?

  • ① spectator
  • ② insect
  • ③ billiards
  • ④ wild

곤충 means insect; bug. 학생들은 여름 방학 과제로 야외에서 곤충을 채집했다. (Students collected insects outdoors as summer vacation assignments.)

Q6Which Korean word means "development"?

  • ① 다이빙
  • ② 생물
  • ③ 진화
  • ④ 가축

진화 means development; advancement; evolution. 인간은 오랜 세월을 거쳐 환경에 맞게 진화해 왔다. (Humans have evolved over a long time to fit the environment.)
