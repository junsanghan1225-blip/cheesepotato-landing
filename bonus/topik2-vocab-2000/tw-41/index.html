퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 나돌다 mean?

  • ① systematic
  • ② to circulate
  • ③ strongly
  • ④ essential

나돌다 means to circulate; to roam outside; to be rumored. 그는 집안일은 돌보지 않고 주말마다 밖으로 나돌았다. (He did not look after housework and roamed outside every weekend.)

Q2Which Korean word means "fault"?

  • ① 시치미
  • ② 매사
  • ③ 탓
  • ④ 나돌다

탓 means fault; reason. 갑작스러운 궂은 날씨 탓에 야외 행사가 부득이 취소되었다. (The outdoor event was inevitably canceled due to sudden bad weather.)

Q3What does 체계적 mean?

  • ① being complete
  • ② creative
  • ③ to be obvious
  • ④ systematic

체계적 means systematic. 공장은 체계적인 생산 관리를 통해 효율을 극대화했다. (The factory maximized efficiency through systematic production management.)

Q4Which Korean word means "formality"?

  • ① 격식
  • ② 오류
  • ③ 탓
  • ④ 시각적

격식 means formality. 공식 행사에서는 정해진 격식을 갖추어야 한다. (One must observe the prescribed formality at official events.)

Q5What does 다방면 mean?

  • ① alternative
  • ② many fields
  • ③ original
  • ④ persuasiveness

다방면 means many fields; many sides. 회사는 다방면의 인재를 적극 영입하여 사업을 확장했다. (The company expanded business by actively recruiting talents in diverse fields.)

Q6Which Korean word means "everything"?

  • ① 체계적
  • ② 강력히
  • ③ 매사
  • ④ 신중히

매사 means everything; all matters. 매사에 성실한 태도를 지녀야 신뢰를 얻는다. (One must maintain a diligent attitude in all matters to gain trust.)
