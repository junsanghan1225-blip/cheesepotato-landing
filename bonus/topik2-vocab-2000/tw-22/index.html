Q1What does 다발 mean?

  • ① electric light
  • ② bunch
  • ③ fluorescent lamp
  • ④ redevelopment

다발 means bunch; bundle; bouquet. 은행원은 금고에서 두툼한 지폐 다발을 꺼내어 확인했다. (The teller took out thick bundles of banknotes from the vault and verified.)

Q2Which Korean word means "every nook and corner"?

  • ① 함
  • ② 건축물
  • ③ 구석구석
  • ④ 다발

구석구석 means every nook and corner; everywhere. 경찰은 산속 구석구석을 수색하며 실종자를 찾았다. (Police searched every nook and cranny in the mountain looking for the missing person.)

Q3What does 전등 mean?

  • ① owner
  • ② resident
  • ③ here and there
  • ④ electric light

전등 means electric light; lamp. 수명이 다한 전등을 새것으로 교체했다. (The electric bulb that reached the end of its life was replaced with a new one.)

Q4Which Korean word means "shelf"?

  • ① 선반
  • ② 주거지
  • ③ 구석구석
  • ④ 베다

선반 means shelf; rack; ledge. 벽에 튼튼한 나무 선반을 설치하여 자주 읽는 책들을 가지런히 정리했다. (Installing sturdy wooden shelves on the wall, he neatly organized books read frequently.)

Q5What does 주거 mean?

  • ① tax notice
  • ② dwelling
  • ③ to sew
  • ④ residential area

주거 means dwelling; housing. 쾌적한 주거 공간의 확보는 인간다운 삶의 기본 조건이다. (Securing pleasant residential space is a basic condition for decent living.)

Q6Which Korean word means "building"?

  • ① 전등
  • ② 형광등
  • ③ 건축물
  • ④ 거주

건축물 means building; architectural structure. 역사적 가치가 높은 고대 건축물은 국가 문화재로 지정되어 보호된다. (Ancient buildings of high historical value are designated as state cultural assets and protected.)
