퀴즈 Quiz

Q1In storytelling, how do particles typically shift when introducing a character and then describing them?

  • ① First 은/는, then 이/가
  • ② First 이/가 (introducing), then 은/는 (already known)
  • ③ Always 이/가
  • ④ Always 은/는

A new character is first introduced with 이/가, and once established in the context, they become the topic marked by 은/는.

Q2Read the sequence: '어제 길에서 강아지 한 마리를 보았어요. 강아지___ 꼬리를 흔들었어요.' Which particle fits best in the second sentence?

  • ① 는
  • ② 로
  • ③ 에서
  • ④ 보다

The puppy was already introduced in the previous sentence, so it is now known topic information taking 는.

Q3Which answer appropriately matches the question: '누가 범인이에요?' (Who is the culprit)?

  • ① 저 사람이 범인이에요.
  • ② 저 사람은 범인이에요.
  • ③ 저 사람을 범인이에요.
  • ④ 저 사람에 범인이에요.

The question asks 'who' (누가), which demands specific identification marked by the subject particle 이.

Q4What is the difference between '이 책이 좋아요' and '이 책은 좋아요'?

  • ① 이 책이 좋아요 is incorrect.
  • ② 이 책이 좋아요 focuses specifically on this book, while 이 책은 좋아요 contrasts it with other books.
  • ③ They mean opposite things.
  • ④ 이 책은 좋아요 cannot be used.

이 책이 marks this specific book, while 이 책은 sets this book apart, often implying contrast with other books.

Q5Fill in the blank: '저 방에 누군가 서 있어요. 그 사람___ 누구일까요?'

  • ① 은
  • ② 을
  • ③ 에
  • ④ 로

The person was already mentioned in the first sentence, so 그 사람은 uses 은 as the established topic.
