퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does (이)나 mean in '사과를 다섯 개나 먹었어요'?

  • ① Choice between five apples or fewer
  • ② Surprise that eating five apples is a large amount
  • ③ Only five apples
  • ④ With five apples

Attached to a number and counter (다섯 개), (이)나 expresses surprise that the quantity is larger than expected.

Q2What does (이)나 mean in '시간이 있으면 차나 한잔합시다'?

  • ① As many as
  • ② Suggesting an option casually (something like tea)
  • ③ Only tea
  • ④ Never tea

Attached to a noun without a counter, (이)나 here suggests an acceptable casual option (tea or something).

Q3Which sentence uses (이)나 to present a choice between two alternatives?

  • ① 어제 열 시간이나 잤어요.
  • ② 부산이나 제주도로 여행 가고 싶어요.
  • ③ 책을 열 권이나 읽었어요.
  • ④ 선물이 스무 개나 들어왔어요.

부산이나 제주도 expresses a choice between two destinations (Busan or Jeju Island).

Q4Which noun ending with a vowel correctly takes 나 instead of 이나 for choice?

  • ① 물
  • ② 피자
  • ③ 가방
  • ④ 지하철

피자 ends with a vowel (자), so it takes 나 (피자나 햄버거), while the others end with consonants taking 이나.

Q5Fill in the blank expressing shock at waiting time: '친구가 늦어서 정류장에서 한 시간___ 기다렸어요.'

  • ① 이나
  • ② 나
  • ③ 밖에
  • ④ 도

시간 ends with a final consonant (ㄴ), so 이나 expresses that waiting for a whole hour was a surprisingly long time.
