퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 마다 mean in '아침마다 조깅을 해요'?

  • ① Only this morning
  • ② Every morning without exception
  • ③ Like morning
  • ④ Than morning

The particle 마다 indicates repetition without exception (every morning).

Q2Does 마다 change form after a final consonant?

  • ① Yes, it becomes 이마다.
  • ② No, it attaches directly as 마다 to all nouns.
  • ③ It becomes 으로마다.
  • ④ It drops after vowels.

The particle 마다 attaches directly to all nouns without form change.

Q3Which sentence expresses: 'Every person has a different taste'?

  • ① 사람마다 취향이 달라요.
  • ② 사람보다 취향이 달라요.
  • ③ 사람처럼 취향이 달라요.
  • ④ 사람에게 취향이 달라요.

사람마다 means each and every person, expressing individual differences.

Q4Fill in the blank: '지하철은 출근 시간에 삼 분___ 한 대씩 와요.'

  • ① 마다
  • ② 보다
  • ③ 처럼
  • ④ 쯤

삼 분마다 means every three minutes, indicating regular time intervals.

Q5Which phrase has an INCORRECT particle choice for 'every weekend'?

  • ① 주말마다
  • ② 주말이마다
  • ③ 토요일마다
  • ④ 일요일마다

마다 attaches directly without adding 이; 주말이마다 is ungrammatical.
