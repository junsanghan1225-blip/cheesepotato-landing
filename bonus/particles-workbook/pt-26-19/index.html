퀴즈 Quiz

Q1In the sentence '수박이 사과보다 커요', which fruit is bigger?

  • ① Apple
  • ② Watermelon
  • ③ Both are equal
  • ④ Cannot be determined

The particle 보다 attaches to the baseline of comparison (사과). So watermelon is bigger than apple.

Q2Which word is often used alongside 보다 to emphasize 'more'?

  • ① 더
  • ② 덜
  • ③ 안
  • ④ 못

더 (more) commonly reinforces comparative statements using 보다 (for example, 어제보다 더 더워요).

Q3Does 보다 change form when attached to a noun with a final consonant?

  • ① Yes, it becomes 이보다.
  • ② No, it is always 보다 on all nouns.
  • ③ It becomes 으로보다.
  • ④ It becomes 과보다.

The particle 보다 has a constant form and attaches directly to nouns with or without a final consonant.

Q4Which sentence correctly expresses: 'Today is warmer than yesterday'?

  • ① 오늘이 어제보다 따뜻해요.
  • ② 오늘이 어제처럼 따뜻해요.
  • ③ 오늘이 어제까지 따뜻해요.
  • ④ 오늘이 어제에게 따뜻해요.

어제보다 attaches 보다 to yesterday as the point of comparison, meaning warmer than yesterday.

Q5Fill in the blank: '이번 시험은 지난번 시험___ 훨씬 쉬웠어요.'

  • ① 보다
  • ② 처럼
  • ③ 마다
  • ④ 이나

보다 attaches to 지난번 시험 to mean much easier than the previous exam.
