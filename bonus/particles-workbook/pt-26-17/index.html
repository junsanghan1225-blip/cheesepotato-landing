퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does the particle 쯤 mean when attached to a number or time?

  • ① Exactly
  • ② About / around / approximately
  • ③ Only
  • ④ Never

쯤 indicates an approximation, estimate, or rough guess (about, around).

Q2Does the form of 쯤 change depending on whether the noun ends with a final consonant?

  • ① Yes, it changes to 이쯤 after a consonant.
  • ② No, it is always 쯤 regardless of final consonants.
  • ③ Yes, it drops after vowels.
  • ④ It only attaches to vowels.

The particle 쯤 has a single fixed form and attaches directly to nouns whether they have a final consonant or not.

Q3Which sentence correctly uses 쯤 to estimate time?

  • ① 내일 오후 두 시쯤 커피숍에서 만나요.
  • ② 내일 오후 두 시이쯤 커피숍에서 만나요.
  • ③ 내일 오후 두 시을쯤 커피숍에서 만나요.
  • ④ 내일 오후 두 시로쯤 커피숍에서 만나요.

쯤 attaches directly to time nouns: 두 시쯤 means around 2 o'clock.

Q4Which question asks for an approximate count using 쯤?

  • ① 학생이 몇 명쯤 왔어요?
  • ② 학생이 몇 명을 왔어요?
  • ③ 학생이 몇 명으로 왔어요?
  • ④ 학생이 몇 명에 왔어요?

몇 명쯤 means approximately how many people.

Q5Fill in the blank: '역까지 걸어가면 십오 분___ 걸려요.'

  • ① 쯤
  • ② 이쯤
  • ③ 과
  • ④ 보다

십오 분쯤 means about 15 minutes, expressing an estimated duration.
