퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What is the difference between 에게 and 한테?

  • ① 에게 is for objects, while 한테 is for people.
  • ② 에게 is commonly used in both writing and formal speech, while 한테 is more casual and conversational.
  • ③ 에게 is honorific, while 한테 is impolite.
  • ④ There is no difference in tone or register.

Both attach to people or animals, but 에게 is standard/formal/written, whereas 한테 is informal and conversational.

Q2Which particle is the HONORIFIC form of 에게/한테 used when directing an action to an elder?

  • ① 에서
  • ② 께
  • ③ 으로
  • ④ 의

께 is the honorific dative particle used instead of 에게/한테 when giving something to an elder or respected person.

Q3Can 에게/한테 attach to an inanimate building like 도서관?

  • ① Yes, always.
  • ② No, inanimate objects and locations take 에, not 에게/한테.
  • ③ Only in questions.
  • ④ Only when writing letters.

에게/한테 is only used with people or animals. Inanimate objects and locations take the particle 에.

Q4Which sentence correctly uses an honorific dative particle?

  • ① 할머니한테 꽃을 드렸어요.
  • ② 할머니께 꽃을 드렸어요.
  • ③ 할머니에게 꽃을 드렸어요.
  • ④ 할머니를 꽃을 드렸어요.

When offering something to grandparents (elders), the honorific particle 께 must be used with the verb 드리다.

Q5Which particle completes: '짝꿍___ 연필 한 자루를 빌려주었어요'?

  • ① 에게
  • ② 에
  • ③ 을
  • ④ 으로

짝꿍 (classmate) is a person receiving the lent item, so the dative particle 에게 (or 한테) is used.
