퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What do 처럼 and 같이 mean when attached to a noun in '천사처럼 웃어요'?

  • ① Together with
  • ② Like / as (comparing resemblance)
  • ③ Because of
  • ④ Toward

Attached to a noun, 처럼 or 같이 means like or as, drawing a comparison of resemblance.

Q2Which sentence has the same meaning as '가족처럼 지내요'?

  • ① 가족같이 지내요.
  • ② 가족에게 지내요.
  • ③ 가족으로 지내요.
  • ④ 가족보다 지내요.

The particles 처럼 and 같이 are interchangeable when expressing resemblance (like family).

Q3Does 처럼 change form based on the presence of a final consonant?

  • ① Yes, it changes to 이처럼.
  • ② No, it attaches directly as 처럼 to all nouns.
  • ③ Only after vowels.
  • ④ Only after consonants.

The particle 처럼 has an invariant form and attaches directly to any noun.

Q4Which sentence correctly describes someone singing like a bird?

  • ① 새처럼 맑은 목소리로 노래해요.
  • ② 새으로 맑은 목소리로 노래해요.
  • ③ 새에게 맑은 목소리로 노래해요.
  • ④ 새보다만 맑은 목소리로 노래해요.

새처럼 means like a bird, comparing the voice to a bird.

Q5Fill in the blank: '창밖 풍경이 한 폭의 그림___ 아름다워요.'

  • ① 처럼
  • ② 보다
  • ③ 마다
  • ④ 쯤

그림처럼 (or 그림같이) means like a painting, depicting poetic resemblance.
