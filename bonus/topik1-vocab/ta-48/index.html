퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 모임 mean?

  • ① others
  • ② gathering
  • ③ adult
  • ④ content

모임 means gathering; club meeting. 주말 저녁에 동호회 친구들과 정기 모임을 가졌어요. (On weekend evening, I had a regular gathering with club friends.)

Q2Which Korean word means "each other"?

  • ① 어리다
  • ② 한글
  • ③ 서로
  • ④ 복잡하다

서로 means each other; mutually. 좋은 친구는 기쁜 일과 슬픈 일을 서로 믿고 나눠요. (Good friends trust each other and share happy and sad things.)

Q3What does 어른 mean?

  • ① way
  • ② ask
  • ③ document
  • ④ adult

어른 means adult; elder. 어른을 보면 먼저 인사해요. (When I see an elder, I greet first.)

Q4Which Korean word means "way"?

  • ① 방법
  • ② 도움
  • ③ 차
  • ④ 서로

방법 means way; method. 새로 산 전자제품의 올바른 사용 방법을 꼼꼼히 읽어요. (I carefully read the proper method of use for the newly purchased electronics.)

Q5What does 소리 mean?

  • ① luggage
  • ② sound
  • ③ name (honorific)
  • ④ age

소리 means sound; noise. 창밖에서 빗소리가 시원하게 들려요. (The sound of rain is heard refreshingly from outside the window.)

Q6Which Korean word means "to be complicated"?

  • ① 남
  • ② 어른
  • ③ 복잡하다
  • ④ 내용

복잡하다 means to be complicated; to be crowded. 퇴근 시간의 서울 도심 도로는 차가 많아서 참 복잡해요. (Downtown Seoul roads during evening rush hour have many cars and are very crowded.)
