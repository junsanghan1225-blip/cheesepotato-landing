퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 손가락 mean?

  • ① right hand
  • ② finger
  • ③ knee
  • ④ buttocks

손가락 means finger. 다친 손가락에 반창고를 붙였어요. (I put a band-aid on my injured finger.)

Q2Which Korean word means "chest"?

  • ① 머리카락
  • ② 어깨
  • ③ 가슴
  • ④ 피

가슴 means chest; heart. 숨을 깊이 쉬니 가슴이 시원해요. (Breathing deeply refreshes my chest.)

Q3What does 무릎 mean?

  • ① palm
  • ② lips
  • ③ recover
  • ④ knee

무릎 means knee; lap. 무릎 위에 담요를 덮고 쉬어요. (I cover my lap with a blanket and rest.)

Q4Which Korean word means "palm"?

  • ① 손바닥
  • ② 이마
  • ③ 힘
  • ④ 가슴

손바닥 means palm. 손바닥 위에 작은 동전을 올려놓아요. (I put a small coin on my palm.)

Q5What does 왼손 mean?

  • ① tongue
  • ② left hand
  • ③ point
  • ④ toe

왼손 means left hand. 왼손에 반지를 꼈어요. (I wore a ring on my left hand.)

Q6Which Korean word means "blood"?

  • ① 오른손
  • ② 무릎
  • ③ 피
  • ④ 엉덩이

피 means blood. 병원에서 피 검사를 받아요. (I get a blood test at the hospital.)
