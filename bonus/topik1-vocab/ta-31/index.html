퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 혼자 mean?

  • ① person (honorific)
  • ② alone
  • ③ foreigner
  • ④ man

혼자 means alone; by oneself. 조용히 생각을 정리하며 혼자 영화를 봤어요. (I watched a movie alone, quietly organizing my thoughts.)

Q2Which Korean word means "together"?

  • ① 여러분
  • ② 친절하다
  • ③ 함께
  • ④ 할머니

함께 means together. 가족들과 함께 저녁 식사를 하며 이야기를 나눠요. (I share stories having dinner together with my family.)

Q3What does 외국인 mean?

  • ① nationality
  • ② to be close
  • ③ family
  • ④ foreigner

외국인 means foreigner; foreign national. 교실에 외국인 친구가 많아요. (There are many foreign friends in the classroom.)

Q4Which Korean word means "nationality"?

  • ① 국적
  • ② 친절
  • ③ 부모님
  • ④ 함께

국적 means nationality; citizenship. 그분의 국적은 한국이에요. (His nationality is Korean.)

Q5What does 여자 mean?

  • ① younger sibling
  • ② woman
  • ③ person
  • ④ middle-aged woman

여자 means woman; female. 많은 여자 손님들이 가게에 왔어요. (Many female customers came to the shop.)

Q6Which Korean word means "grandmother"?

  • ① 분
  • ② 외국인
  • ③ 할머니
  • ④ 남자

할머니 means grandmother; elderly woman. 방학마다 시골에 계신 할머니를 찾아봬요. (Every vacation, I visit my grandmother in the countryside.)
