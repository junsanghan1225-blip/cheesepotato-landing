퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 막히다 mean?

  • ① entrance
  • ② be blocked
  • ③ city
  • ④ downtown

막히다 means be blocked; be closed; be clogged. 세면대 구멍이 머리카락으로 막혔어요. (The sink hole was clogged with hair.)

Q2Which Korean word means "Gwangju"?

  • ① 대전
  • ② 시골
  • ③ 광주
  • ④ 출구

광주 means Gwangju. 광주에는 맛있는 음식이 참 많아요. (There is really a lot of delicious food in Gwangju.)

Q3What does 도시 mean?

  • ① building
  • ② underground walkway
  • ③ walk
  • ④ city

도시 means city. 복잡한 도시를 떠나 여행을 가요. (I leave the crowded city and go traveling.)

Q4Which Korean word means "building"?

  • ① 빌딩
  • ② 주차장
  • ③ 지나가다
  • ④ 광주

빌딩 means building. 회사 빌딩 로비에서 친구를 만나요. (I meet my friend in the lobby of the office building.)

Q5What does 울산 mean?

  • ① crosswalk
  • ② Ulsan
  • ③ distance
  • ④ neighborhood

울산 means Ulsan. 기차를 타고 울산에 가요. (I go to Ulsan by train.)

Q6Which Korean word means "exit"?

  • ① 입구
  • ② 도시
  • ③ 출구
  • ④ 시내

출구 means exit. 지하철 출구를 찾았어요. (I found the subway exit.)
