Q1What does 접시 mean?

  • ① to steam
  • ② plate
  • ③ snack
  • ④ tofu

접시 means plate; dish. 접시를 깨끗이 닦아요. (I wipe the plate cleanly.)

Q2Which Korean word means "fry"?

  • ① 국
  • ② 찌개
  • ③ 튀기다
  • ④ 달걀

튀기다 means fry; deep-fry. 바삭하게 튀겨 냅니다. (I fry it out crisply.)

Q3What does 과자 mean?

  • ① fish (as food)
  • ② egg
  • ③ plate
  • ④ snack

과자 means snack. 마트에서 바삭한 과자를 샀어요. (I bought crispy cookies at the mart.)

Q4Which Korean word means "fish (as food)"?

  • ① 생선
  • ② 계란
  • ③ 떡
  • ④ 튀기다

생선 means fish (as food). 시장에서 싱싱한 생선을 샀어요. (I bought fresh fish at the market.)

Q5What does 감자 mean?

  • ① tofu
  • ② potato
  • ③ knife
  • ④ noodle

감자 means potato. 시장 야채 가게에서 햇감자를 샀어요. (I bought fresh potatoes at the market vegetable shop.)

Q6Which Korean word means "egg"?

  • ① 칼
  • ② 국수
  • ③ 달걀
  • ④ 감자

달걀 means egg. 마트에서 신선한 달걀 한 판을 샀어요. (I bought a tray of fresh eggs at the mart.)
