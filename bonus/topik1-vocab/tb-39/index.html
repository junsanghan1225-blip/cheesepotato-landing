Q1What does 입학 mean?

  • ② admission to school
  • ③ attendance
  • ④ handwriting

입학 means admission to school; entrance. 입학 축하 선물을 줘요. (I give an admission congratulatory gift.)

Q2Which Korean word means "being late"?

  • ① 초등학생
  • ② 학원
  • ③ 지각
  • ④ 일기

지각 means being late; tardiness. 지각하지 않게 서둘러요. (Hurry up so as not to be late.)

Q3What does 출석 mean?

  • ① memorize
  • ② appropriate
  • ③ result
  • ④ attendance

출석 means attendance; presence. 모든 수업에 출석했어요. (I attended all classes.)

Q4Which Korean word means "memorize"?

  • ① 외우다
  • ② 제출하다
  • ③ 가위
  • ④ 지각

외우다 means memorize. 시험을 위해 문법을 외웠어요. (I memorized grammar for the exam.)

Q5What does 지우다 mean?

  • ① be wrong
  • ② erase
  • ③ middle school student
  • ④ semester

지우다 means erase. 지우개로 잘못 쓴 글자를 지웠어요. (I erased the misspelled letter with an eraser.)

Q6Which Korean word means "diary"?

  • ① 중학교
  • ② 출석
  • ③ 일기
  • ④ 글씨

일기 means diary; journal. 한국어로 짧은 일기를 써요. (I write a short diary entry in Korean.)
