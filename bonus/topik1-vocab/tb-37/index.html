Q1What does 이모 mean?

  • ① children
  • ② maternal aunt
  • ③ police
  • ④ reporter

이모 means maternal aunt. 이모가 맛있는 음식을 줘요. (My aunt gives me delicious food.)

Q2Which Korean word means "brothers"?

  • ① 간호사
  • ② 군인
  • ③ 형제
  • ④ 선수

형제 means brothers; siblings. 형제끼리 서로 도와요. (Brothers help each other.)

Q3What does 경찰 mean?

  • ① professor
  • ② president
  • ③ be born
  • ④ police

경찰 means police; policing. 길을 잃어서 경찰의 도움을 받았어요. (I got lost and received help from the police.)

Q4Which Korean word means "professor"?

  • ① 교수
  • ② 부장
  • ③ 약사
  • ④ 형제

교수 means professor. 교수님의 연구실을 찾아갔어요. (I visited the professor's office.)

Q5What does 변호사 mean?

  • ① announcer
  • ② lawyer
  • ③ nephew
  • ④ civil servant

변호사 means lawyer; attorney. 친절한 변호사의 도움을 받았어요. (I received help from a kind attorney.)

Q6Which Korean word means "athlete"?

  • ① 자식
  • ② 경찰
  • ③ 선수
  • ④ 기자

선수 means athlete; player. 국가대표 선수가 되려고 매일 훈련해요. (He trains daily to become a national athlete.)
