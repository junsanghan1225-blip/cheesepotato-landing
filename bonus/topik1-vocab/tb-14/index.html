퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 깨끗이 mean?

  • ① to wake up
  • ② cleanly
  • ③ to open (eyes)
  • ④ push

깨끗이 means cleanly; neatly. 방을 깨끗이 청소하고 환기해요. (I clean the room neatly and air it out.)

Q2Which Korean word means "nap"?

  • ① 더럽다
  • ② 목적
  • ③ 낮잠
  • ④ 뽑다

낮잠 means nap; daytime sleep. 낮잠을 조금 자고 나니 개운해요. (After taking a short nap, I feel refreshed.)

Q3What does 뜨다 mean?

  • ① bath
  • ② inconvenience
  • ③ stand up
  • ④ to open (eyes)

뜨다 means to open (eyes); to float; to rise (sun). 동쪽 바다에서 붉은 해가 떠요. (The red sun rises from the eastern sea.)

Q4Which Korean word means "bath"?

  • ① 목욕
  • ② 보이다
  • ③ 얻다
  • ④ 낮잠

목욕 means bath. 욕조에서 편안하게 목욕을 즐겨요. (I comfortably enjoy a bath in the bathtub.)

Q5What does 반드시 mean?

  • ① brushing one's teeth
  • ② must
  • ③ to take out
  • ④ to adjust

반드시 means must; certainly. 외출할 때 문을 반드시 잠그세요. (Make sure you certainly lock the door when going out.)

Q6Which Korean word means "pull"?

  • ① 깨다
  • ② 뜨다
  • ③ 뽑다
  • ④ 밀다

뽑다 means pull; pick; pluck. 우리 반 대표를 투표로 뽑았어요. (We elected our class representative by vote.)
