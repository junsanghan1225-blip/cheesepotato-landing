Q1What does 밥하다 mean?

  • ① to eat
  • ② cook rice
  • ③ gimbap
  • ④ kimchi jjigae

밥하다 means cook rice. 전기밥솥으로 밥하고 국을 끓여요. (I cook rice with the electric rice cooker and boil soup.)

Q2Which Korean word means "meal"?

  • ① 빵
  • ② 케이크
  • ③ 밥
  • ④ 감

밥 means meal; cooked rice. 점심시간에 친구와 따뜻한 밥을 먹어요. (I eat a warm meal with a friend at lunchtime.)

Q3What does 김밥 mean?

  • ① bulgogi
  • ② galbitang
  • ③ strawberry
  • ④ gimbap

김밥 means gimbap; seaweed rice roll. 소풍 갈 때 먹으려고 아침에 참치 김밥을 맛있게 싸요. (In the morning, I make delicious tuna gimbap to eat on a picnic.)

Q4Which Korean word means "bulgogi"?

  • ① 불고기
  • ② 갈비
  • ③ 된장찌개
  • ④ 밥

불고기 means bulgogi. 식당에서 불고기 2인분을 시켰어요. (I ordered 2 servings of bulgogi at the restaurant.)

Q5What does 수박 mean?

  • ① naengmyeon
  • ② watermelon
  • ③ food
  • ④ bibimbap

수박 means watermelon. 시장에서 큰 수박 한 통을 샀어요. (I bought a large whole watermelon at the market.)

Q6Which Korean word means "persimmon"?

  • ① 먹다
  • ② 김밥
  • ③ 감
  • ④ 김치찌개

감 means persimmon. 나무에 주황색 감이 열렸어요. (Orange persimmons grew on the tree.)
