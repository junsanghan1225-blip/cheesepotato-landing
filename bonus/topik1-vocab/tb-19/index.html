퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 영하 mean?

  • ① cloud
  • ② below zero
  • ③ fog
  • ④ sunlight

영하 means below zero; sub-zero. 오늘 아침 기온은 영하 오 도예요. (This morning's temperature is minus five degrees.)

Q2Which Korean word means "temperature"?

  • ① 쌀쌀하다
  • ② 햇빛
  • ③ 기온
  • ④ 노력

기온 means temperature; air temperature. 겨울에는 기온이 영하로 내려가요. (In winter, the temperature drops below zero.)

Q3What does 안개 mean?

  • ① monsoon
  • ② to go well
  • ③ to be familiar
  • ④ fog

안개 means fog; mist. 안개 때문에 앞이 잘 안 보여요. (I cannot see ahead well because of fog.)

Q4Which Korean word means "monsoon"?

  • ① 장마
  • ② 급하다
  • ③ 분위기
  • ④ 기온

장마 means monsoon; rainy season. 장마철에는 비가 자주 와요. (It rains often during rainy season.)

Q5What does 믿다 mean?

  • ① decision
  • ② believe
  • ③ to stop
  • ④ freeze

믿다 means believe; trust. 약속을 꼭 지킬 거라고 믿어요. (I believe you will surely keep the promise.)

Q6Which Korean word means "to want"?

  • ① 구름
  • ② 안개
  • ③ 원하다
  • ④ 햇빛

원하다 means to want; to wish. 제가 진정으로 원하는 직업이에요. (It is the job that I truly want.)
