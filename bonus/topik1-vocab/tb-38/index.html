퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 늙다 mean?

  • ① age (honorific)
  • ② to grow old
  • ③ slim
  • ④ handsome

늙다 means to grow old; age. 나이가 들어도 마음은 늙지 않아요. (Even as age increases, the heart does not age.)

Q2Which Korean word means "young person"?

  • ① 모습
  • ② 못생기다
  • ③ 청년
  • ④ 고등학생

청년 means young person; youth. 청년들이 모여 토론해요. (Young people gather to debate.)

Q3What does 날씬하다 mean?

  • ① stylishness
  • ② high school
  • ③ kindergarten
  • ④ slim

날씬하다 means slim; slender. 날씬한 핏의 원피스를 입었어요. (I wore a slim-fit dress.)

Q4Which Korean word means "stylishness"?

  • ① 멋
  • ② 결석
  • ③ 여학생
  • ④ 청년

멋 means stylishness; being fashionable; being chic. 한복은 전통의 멋이 담겨 있어요. (Hanbok embodies the stylish charm of tradition.)

Q5What does 정문 mean?

  • ① timetable
  • ② main gate
  • ③ to be young
  • ④ to resemble

정문 means main gate; front gate. 정문으로 버스가 들어와요. (A bus enters through the main gate.)

Q6Which Korean word means "high school student"?

  • ① 연세
  • ② 날씬하다
  • ③ 고등학생
  • ④ 잘생기다

고등학생 means high school student. 고등학생들이 버스에 많이 타요. (Many high school students board the bus.)
