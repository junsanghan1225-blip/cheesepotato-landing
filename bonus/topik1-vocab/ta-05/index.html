퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 삼 mean?

  • ① three
  • ② ginseng
  • ③ thirty
  • ④ five

삼 means ginseng. 한국에서 생활한 지 올해로 벌써 삼 년이 되었어요. (It has already been three years this year since I lived in Korea.)

Q2Which Korean word means "seven"?

  • ① 반
  • ② 넷
  • ③ 일곱
  • ④ 서른

일곱 means seven. 바구니 속에 신선하고 빨간 사과가 일곱 개 들어 있어요. (There are seven fresh and red apples inside the basket.)

Q3What does 삼십 mean?

  • ① nine
  • ② six
  • ③ ginseng
  • ④ thirty

삼십 means thirty; 30. 책의 가격은 삼십 달러예요. (The price of the book is thirty dollars.)

Q4Which Korean word means "nine"?

  • ① 아홉
  • ② 여덟
  • ③ 칠
  • ④ 일곱

아홉 means nine; 9. 사과가 바구니에 아홉 개 있어요. (There are nine apples in the basket.)

Q5What does 이십 mean?

  • ① six
  • ② twenty
  • ③ medicine
  • ④ many

이십 means twenty. 이 책은 이십 달러예요. (This book is twenty dollars.)

Q6Which Korean word means "thirty"?

  • ① 약
  • ② 하나씩
  • ③ 서른
  • ④ 여덟

서른 means thirty. 서른 살이 되면 독립하고 싶어요. (I want to be independent when I turn thirty.)
