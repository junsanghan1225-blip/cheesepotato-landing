퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 밖 mean?

  • ① front
  • ② outside
  • ③ nearby
  • ④ top

밖 means outside. 날씨가 좋아서 집 밖으로 산책을 나가요. (The weather is good, so I go outside the house for a walk.)

Q2Which Korean word means "back"?

  • ① 오른쪽
  • ② 옆
  • ③ 뒤
  • ④ 선물하다

뒤 means back; behind; after. 건물 뒤에 작은 정원이 있어서 산책하기 좋아요. (There is a small garden behind the building, so it is nice to walk.)

Q3What does 근처 mean?

  • ① to be far
  • ② category
  • ③ clothes
  • ④ nearby

근처 means nearby; vicinity. 집 근처에 있는 편의점에 가서 따뜻한 음료를 사요. (I go to a convenience store near my house and buy a warm drink.)

Q4Which Korean word means "to be far"?

  • ① 멀다
  • ② 가운데
  • ③ 생신
  • ④ 뒤

멀다 means to be far. 역에서 집이 멀지 않아요. (The house is not far from the station.)

Q5What does 어디에서나 mean?

  • ① culture
  • ② anywhere
  • ③ here
  • ④ between

어디에서나 means anywhere; everywhere. 한국 노래는 어디에서나 들려요. (Korean songs are heard everywhere.)

Q6Which Korean word means "give a gift"?

  • ① 앞
  • ② 근처
  • ③ 선물하다
  • ④ 위

선물하다 means give a gift. 어머니께 꽃다발을 선물하고 싶어요. (I want to gift a flower bouquet to my mother.)
