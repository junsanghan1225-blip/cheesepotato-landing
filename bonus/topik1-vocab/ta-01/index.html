퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 건강 mean?

  • ① hospital
  • ② health
  • ③ animal hospital
  • ④ mouth

건강 means health. 건강을 유지하기 위해 꾸준히 유산소 운동을 해요. (To maintain health, I consistently do aerobic exercise.)

Q2Which Korean word means "disease"?

  • ① 약국
  • ② 눈
  • ③ 병
  • ④ 팔

병 means disease. 감기라는 병에 걸렸어요. (I caught an illness called a cold.)

Q3What does 동물병원 mean?

  • ① body
  • ② height
  • ③ face
  • ④ animal hospital

동물병원 means animal hospital; veterinary clinic. 집 근처 동물병원에서 주사를 맞혀요. (I get vaccine shots at the vet clinic near my house.)

Q4Which Korean word means "body"?

  • ① 몸
  • ② 목
  • ③ 발
  • ④ 병

몸 means body. 환절기에는 따뜻한 물을 자주 마셔서 몸을 보호해요. (In seasonal transitions, I protect my body by drinking warm water often.)

Q5What does 배 mean?

  • ① leg
  • ② stomach
  • ③ doctor
  • ④ hand

배 means stomach; belly. 점심을 많이 먹어서 저녁때까지 배가 전혀 안 고파요. (I ate a lot for lunch, so I'm not hungry at all until dinner.)

Q6Which Korean word means "arm"?

  • ① 병원
  • ② 동물병원
  • ③ 팔
  • ④ 입

팔 means arm. 행운의 숫자 팔을 아주 좋아해요. (I really like the lucky number eight.)
