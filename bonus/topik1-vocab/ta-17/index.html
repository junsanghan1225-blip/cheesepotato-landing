퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 지나다 mean?

  • ① last year
  • ② pass
  • ③ to be warm
  • ④ winter

지나다 means pass; elapse. 약국을 지나서 오른쪽으로 가세요. (Pass the pharmacy and go right.)

Q2Which Korean word means "August"?

  • ① 날씨
  • ② 바람
  • ③ 팔월
  • ④ 시원하다

팔월 means August. 팔월 십오일은 한국의 광복절이에요. (August 15th is Korea's National Liberation Day.)

Q3What does 따뜻하다 mean?

  • ① to be hot (weather)
  • ② blow
  • ③ to be cloudy
  • ④ to be warm

따뜻하다 means to be warm. 추운 겨울에는 따뜻한 차가 최고예요. (Warm tea is the best in cold winter.)

Q4Which Korean word means "to be hot (weather)"?

  • ① 덥다
  • ② 따뜻해지다
  • ③ 봄
  • ④ 팔월

덥다 means to be hot (weather). 여름에는 날씨가 무척 덥고 습해요. (In summer, the weather is very hot and humid.)

Q5What does 계절 mean?

  • ① autumn
  • ② season
  • ③ July
  • ④ umbrella

계절 means season. 봄에서 여름으로 계절이 바뀌며 날씨가 점점 더워져요. (As the season changes from spring to summer, the weather gets warmer.)

Q6Which Korean word means "to be cool"?

  • ① 지난해
  • ② 따뜻하다
  • ③ 시원하다
  • ④ 겨울

시원하다 means to be cool; to be refreshing. 시원한 물을 한 잔 마셔요. (I drink a glass of cool water.)
