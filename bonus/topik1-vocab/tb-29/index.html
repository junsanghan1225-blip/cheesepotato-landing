Q2Which Korean word means "washing"?

  • ① 휴지
  • ② 꽃병
  • ③ 빨래
  • ④ 옷걸이

빨래 means washing; laundry. 날씨가 맑아서 이불 빨래를 널었어요. (The weather was sunny, so I hung out the blanket laundry.)

Q3What does 휴지 mean?

  • ① dining table
  • ② washing machine
  • ③ bookshelf
  • ④ tissue

Q4Which Korean word means "dining table"?

  • ① 식탁

  • ④ 빨래

Q5What does 냉장고 mean?

Q6Which Korean word means "sofa"?
