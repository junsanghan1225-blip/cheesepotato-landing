Q1What does 문 mean?

  • ① to open
  • ② door
  • ③ kitchen
  • ④ to draw

문 means door; gate. 방이 추워서 문을 닫았어요. (The room was cold, so I closed the door.)

Q2Which Korean word means "toilet"?

  • ① 거실
  • ② 추다
  • ③ 화장실
  • ④ 피아노

화장실 means toilet; restroom; bathroom. 지하철역 안에 있는 깨끗한 화장실을 이용해요. (I use the clean restroom inside the subway station.)

Q3What does 부엌 mean?

  • ① sing
  • ② party
  • ③ photo
  • ④ kitchen

부엌 means kitchen. 부엌 싱크대를 깨끗하게 청소해요. (I clean the kitchen sink cleanly.)

Q4Which Korean word means "sing"?

  • ① 노래하다
  • ② 춤추다
  • ③ 듣다
  • ④ 화장실

노래하다 means sing. 기분이 좋아서 크게 노래했어요. (I felt good, so I sang out loud.)

Q5What does 춤 mean?

  • ① to hear
  • ② dance
  • ③ toilet
  • ④ hobby

춤 means dance; dancing. 한국 전통 춤을 배우고 싶어요. (I want to learn traditional Korean dance.)

Q6Which Korean word means "piano"?

  • ① 열다
  • ② 부엌
  • ③ 피아노
  • ④ 그리다

피아노 means piano. 거실 한쪽에 작은 피아노가 있어요. (There is a small piano on one side of the living room.)
