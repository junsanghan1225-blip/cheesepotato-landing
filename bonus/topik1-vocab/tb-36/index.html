Q1What does 데려오다 mean?

  • ① married couple
  • ② to bring (someone)
  • ③ family member
  • ④ father (hon.)

데려오다 means to bring (someone). 학교에서 동생을 데려올게요. (I will bring my sibling from school.)

Q2Which Korean word means "senior"?

  • ① 시어머니
  • ② 아들
  • ③ 선배
  • ④ 애인

선배 means senior. 동아리 선배들과 함께 점심을 먹어요. (I have lunch with seniors from the club.)

Q3What does 식구 mean?

  • ① honeymoon
  • ② child
  • ③ mom
  • ④ family member

식구 means family member; family. 온 식구가 둘러앉아 저녁을 먹어요. (The whole family sits around to eat dinner.)

Q4Which Korean word means "honeymoon"?

  • ① 신혼여행
  • ② 안다
  • ③ 어머님
  • ④ 선배

신혼여행 means honeymoon. 신혼여행에서 찍은 사진이 예뻐요. (Photos taken on the honeymoon are pretty.)

Q5What does 아빠 mean?

  • ① child
  • ② dad
  • ③ wife / lady
  • ④ groom

아빠 means dad; daddy. 아빠가 맛있는 간식을 사 오셨어요. (Dad bought and brought delicious snacks.)

Q6Which Korean word means "lover"?

  • ① 부부
  • ② 식구
  • ③ 애인
  • ④ 아버님

애인 means lover; partner. 애인에게 줄 선물을 골랐어요. (I picked a gift to give to my partner.)
