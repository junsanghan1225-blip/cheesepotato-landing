퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 벌다 mean?

  • ① foreign exchange
  • ② make
  • ③ free
  • ④ stop

벌다 means make; earn. 열심히 일해서 돈을 많이 벌었어요. (I worked hard and earned a lot of money.)

Q2Which Korean word means "free of charge"?

  • ① 모으다
  • ② 잊다
  • ③ 공짜
  • ④ 눕다

공짜 means free of charge; freebie. 음료수를 공짜로 하나 더 받았어요. (I received one more beverage for free.)

Q3What does 무료 mean?

  • ① fall down
  • ② to cut
  • ③ laundromat
  • ④ free

무료 means free; free of charge. 가게에서 무료 음료수를 제공해요. (The shop offers free drinks.)

Q4Which Korean word means "fall down"?

  • ① 넘어지다
  • ② 서다
  • ③ 비누
  • ④ 공짜

넘어지다 means fall down; trip over; tumble over. 아이가 넘어지지 않게 손을 잡아요. (I hold the child's hand so they don't fall.)

Q5What does 마치다 mean?

  • ① separately
  • ② complete
  • ③ pay back
  • ④ fare

마치다 means complete; finish. 수업을 마치고 식당에 가요. (After finishing class, I go to the cafeteria.)

Q6Which Korean word means "to lie down"?

  • ① 환전
  • ② 무료
  • ③ 눕다
  • ④ 그만

눕다 means to lie down. 방바닥에 누워 잠시 쉬었어요. (I lay down on the room floor and rested briefly.)
