Q1What does 걸리다 mean?

  • ① to be late
  • ② to catch (cold)
  • ③ month
  • ④ Friday

걸리다 means to catch (cold); to take time. 환절기에는 감기에 걸리지 않게 조심하세요. (Please be careful not to catch a cold during the change of seasons.)

Q2Which Korean word means "first"?

  • ① 밤
  • ② 저녁
  • ③ 먼저
  • ④ 오전

먼저 means first; beforehand. 식사하기 전에 손을 먼저 깨끗이 씻어요. (Before having a meal, I wash my hands cleanly first.)

Q3What does 월 mean?

  • ① Sunday
  • ② morning (a.m.)
  • ③ to catch (cold)
  • ④ month

월 means month; being monthly. 봄이 시작되는 삼 월에는 따뜻한 바람이 불어요. (In March, when spring begins, a warm breeze blows.)

Q4Which Korean word means "Sunday"?

  • ① 일요일
  • ② 늦게
  • ③ 날마다
  • ④ 먼저

일요일 means Sunday. 일요일에는 가족과 함께 편안하게 휴식을 취해요. (On Sundays, I rest comfortably with my family.)

Q5What does 나중 mean?

  • ① last week
  • ② later
  • ③ Saturday
  • ④ Monday

나중 means later; after. 지금은 바쁘니까 자세한 이야기는 나중에 나눠요. (Because I am busy now, let's share detailed stories later.)

Q6Which Korean word means "morning (a.m.)"?

  • ① 늦다
  • ② 월
  • ③ 오전
  • ④ 금요일

오전 means morning (a.m.). 오전에 도서관에서 공부하고 오후에 운동해요. (I study at the library in the morning and exercise in the afternoon.)
