퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 다녀오다 mean?

  • ① appointment
  • ② go and come back
  • ③ post office
  • ④ to get along

다녀오다 means go and come back. 오전 아홉 시에 학교에 다녀와서 집에서 점심을 먹어요. (I go to school at 9 a.m. and return home to eat lunch.)

Q2Which Korean word means "to return"?

  • ① 또
  • ② 편리하다
  • ③ 돌아오다
  • ④ 이것

돌아오다 means to return; to come back. 수업이 모두 끝나고 오후 늦게 기숙사로 돌아와요. (All classes end and I return to the dorm late in the afternoon.)

Q3What does 우체국 mean?

  • ① hair salon
  • ② meal
  • ③ public bathhouse
  • ④ post office

우체국 means post office. 편지를 부치러 우체국에 가요. (I go to the post office to mail a letter.)

Q4Which Korean word means "hair salon"?

  • ① 미용실
  • ② 시작되다
  • ③ 담배
  • ④ 돌아오다

미용실 means hair salon; beauty parlor. 동네 미용실 원장님이 친절해요. (The neighborhood hair salon owner is kind.)

Q5What does 배달하다 mean?

  • ① continue
  • ② to deliver
  • ③ to walk
  • ④ to check

배달하다 means to deliver. 신문을 아침마다 집 앞에 배달해요. (They deliver newspapers in front of houses every morning.)

Q6Which Korean word means "this"?

  • ① 약속
  • ② 우체국
  • ③ 이것
  • ④ 지내다

이것 means this; this thing. 이것은 제가 좋아하는 책이에요. (This is a book that I like.)
