Q1What does 북쪽 mean?

  • ① to fall into
  • ② north
  • ③ signal
  • ④ train ticket

북쪽 means north. 북쪽 지방은 겨울에 눈이 많이 와요. (The northern region gets a lot of snow in winter.)

Q2Which Korean word means "three-way intersection"?

  • ① 세우다
  • ② 떠나다
  • ③ 삼거리
  • ④ 소풍

삼거리 means three-way intersection. 삼거리에서 오른쪽 길로 가세요. (Please take the right road at the three-way intersection.)

Q3What does 신호 mean?

  • ① pedestrian overpass
  • ② tourist attraction
  • ③ overseas travel
  • ④ signal

신호 means signal; sign. 빨간 신호에 차를 멈추었어요. (I stopped the car on the red signal.)

Q4Which Korean word means "pedestrian overpass"?

  • ① 육교
  • ② 관광객
  • ③ 해외
  • ④ 삼거리

육교 means pedestrian overpass; footbridge. 육교 계단을 올라가요. (Climb the steps of the overpass.)

Q5What does 관광 mean?

  • ① to visit
  • ② tourism
  • ③ crossroads
  • ④ traffic light

관광 means tourism. 서울 시내 관광을 하루 동안 했어요. (I did sightseeing in downtown Seoul for a day.)

Q6Which Korean word means "trip"?

  • ① 빠지다
  • ② 신호
  • ③ 소풍
  • ④ 기차표

소풍 means trip; picnic; outing. 소풍 갈 때 김밥을 맛있게 싸요. (I pack kimbap deliciously when going on a picnic.)
