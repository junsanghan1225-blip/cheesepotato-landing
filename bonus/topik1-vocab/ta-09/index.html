Q1What does 앉다 mean?

  • ① to lift
  • ② to sit
  • ③ to sleep
  • ④ to attend

앉다 means to sit. 다리가 아파서 빈 의자에 잠깐 앉아요. (Because my legs hurt, I sit on an empty chair for a moment.)

Q2Which Korean word means "rest"?

  • ① 매일
  • ② 찾다
  • ③ 쉬다
  • ④ 준비하다

쉬다 means rest; take a break. 주말에는 집에서 음악을 들으며 푹 쉬어요. (On weekends, I rest soundly at home listening to music.)

Q3What does 자다 mean?

  • ① to put in
  • ② to come in
  • ③ walk
  • ④ to sleep

자다 means to sleep. 피곤해서 어제는 저녁 일찍 푹 잤어요. (Because I was tired, I slept soundly early in the evening yesterday.)

Q4Which Korean word means "to put in"?

  • ① 넣다
  • ② 잠
  • ③ 조심하다
  • ④ 쉬다

넣다 means to put in; to insert. 외출하기 전에 필요한 소지품을 가방에 넣어요. (Before going out, I put necessary belongings into my bag.)

Q5What does 씻다 mean?

  • ① birthday
  • ② to wash
  • ③ to be busy
  • ④ to get up

씻다 means to wash; to clean. 밥을 먹기 전에 비누로 손을 깨끗하게 씻어요. (Before eating, I wash my hands cleanly with soap.)

Q6Which Korean word means "to prepare"?

  • ① 들다
  • ② 자다
  • ③ 준비하다
  • ④ 다니다

준비하다 means to prepare. 내일 발표를 위해 수업 자료를 꼼꼼히 준비해요. (I prepare class materials meticulously for tomorrow's presentation.)
