Q1What does 아이 mean?

  • ① grandfather
  • ② child
  • ③ younger brother
  • ④ wife

아이 means child. 놀이터에서 어린아이들이 밝게 웃으며 뛰어놀아요. (Young children laugh brightly and run around on the playground.)

Q2Which Korean word means "father"?

  • ① 아기
  • ② 사귀다
  • ③ 아버지
  • ④ 사장님

아버지 means father. 퇴근하고 오신 아버지께 공손하게 인사를 드려요. (I greet father politely when he returns home from work.)

Q3What does 남동생 mean?

  • ① daughter
  • ② older brother (for females)
  • ③ actor
  • ④ younger brother

남동생 means younger brother. 남동생에게 선물을 주었어요. (I gave a present to my younger brother.)

Q4Which Korean word means "daughter"?

  • ① 딸
  • ② 여동생
  • ③ 주인
  • ④ 아버지

딸 means daughter. 딸의 생일에 예쁜 인형을 주었어요. (I gave a pretty doll for my daughter's birthday.)

Q5What does 언니 mean?

  • ① photographer
  • ② older sister (for females)
  • ③ help
  • ④ husband

언니 means older sister (for females). 언니와 함께 카페에서 이야기해요. (I chat with my older sister at a cafe.)

Q6Which Korean word means "boss"?

  • ① 할아버지
  • ② 남동생
  • ③ 사장님
  • ④ 아내

사장님 means boss; owner (of a shop); company president. 식당 사장님께서 친절하게 따뜻한 반찬을 더 주셨어요. (The restaurant owner kindly gave more warm side dishes.)
