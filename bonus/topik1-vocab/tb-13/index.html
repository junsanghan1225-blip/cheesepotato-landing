퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 자꾸 mean?

  • ① turn
  • ② frequently
  • ③ to dream (a dream)
  • ④ lose

자꾸 means frequently; repeatedly; again and again. 전화벨이 자꾸 울려요. (The phone bell rings repeatedly.)

Q2Which Korean word means "touch"?

  • ① 걸음
  • ② 외출
  • ③ 만지다
  • ④ 간단히

만지다 means touch. 박물관 작품을 만지지 마세요. (Please do not touch the museum artworks.)

Q3What does 꾸다 mean?

  • ① to hurry
  • ② to bring
  • ③ opportunity
  • ④ to dream (a dream)

꾸다 means to dream (a dream). 친구에게 돈을 조금 꿨어요. (I borrowed a bit of money from a friend.)

Q4Which Korean word means "to hurry"?

  • ① 서두르다
  • ② 끊다
  • ③ 경험
  • ④ 만지다

서두르다 means to hurry; to rush. 시간이 없으니 발걸음을 서두르세요. (There is no time, so please hurry your steps.)

Q5What does 풀다 mean?

  • ① decision
  • ② untie
  • ③ to close (eyes)
  • ④ complete

풀다 means untie. 어려운 수학 문제를 혼자 풀었어요. (I solved the difficult math problem by myself.)

Q6Which Korean word means "simply"?

  • ① 차례
  • ② 꾸다
  • ③ 간단히
  • ④ 잃어버리다

간단히 means simply; briefly. 자기소개를 간단히 해 주세요. (Please introduce yourself briefly.)
