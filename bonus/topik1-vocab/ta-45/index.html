Q1What does 내다 mean?

  • ① to gather
  • ② to pay
  • ③ to put down
  • ④ mart

내다 means to pay; to submit. 식사가 끝나고 식당 카운터에서 밥값을 내요. (After finishing the meal, I pay the bill at the restaurant counter.)

Q2Which Korean word means "to take"?

  • ① 두다
  • ② 할인
  • ③ 가져가다
  • ④ 벌써

가져가다 means to take; to bring along. 오후에 비 소식이 있어서 가방에 우산을 챙겨 가져가요. (There is rain in the forecast this afternoon, so I pack and take an umbrella.)

Q3What does 놓다 mean?

  • ① one day
  • ② now
  • ③ always
  • ④ to put down

놓다 means to put down; to place. 책상 위에 펜을 놓아요. (I put a pen on the desk.)

Q4Which Korean word means "one day"?

  • ① 한번
  • ② 오래
  • ③ 갑자기
  • ④ 가져가다

한번 means one day; once. 이 노래를 한번 들어 보세요. (Please listen to this song once.)

Q5What does 아까 mean?

  • ① already
  • ② a moment ago
  • ③ seat
  • ④ to decide

아까 means a moment ago. 아까 점심을 많이 먹어서 아직 배가 안 고파요. (Because I ate a lot of lunch earlier, I am not hungry yet.)

Q6Which Korean word means "already"?

  • ① 자리
  • ② 정하다
  • ③ 벌써
  • ④ 아까

벌써 means already; so soon. 기차가 빠르게 달려 벌써 서울역에 도착했어요. (The train ran quickly and already arrived at Seoul Station.)
