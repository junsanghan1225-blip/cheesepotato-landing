퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 평소 mean?

  • ① first
  • ② ordinary times
  • ③ the second day
  • ④ four days

평소 means ordinary times; usual. 평소에 운동을 자주 해요. (I exercise often usually.)

Q2Which Korean word means "sometimes"?

  • ① 오랜만
  • ② 그날
  • ③ 가끔
  • ④ 마지막

가끔 means sometimes. 고향 친구들에게 가끔 연락해요. (I occasionally contact my hometown friends.)

Q3What does 이틀 mean?

  • ① past
  • ② same time
  • ③ three days
  • ④ the second day

이틀 means the second day. 약속 날짜가 이틀 앞으로 다가왔어요. (The appointment date has come two days ahead.)

Q4Which Korean word means "past"?

  • ① 과거
  • ② 달력
  • ③ 방금
  • ④ 가끔

과거 means past. 과거에 한국에 산 적이 있어요. (I have lived in Korea in the past.)

Q5What does 다음날 mean?

  • ① every week
  • ② next day
  • ③ always
  • ④ every month

다음날 means next day; following day. 다음날 아침 일찍 공항으로 떠나요. (I leave for the airport early the next morning.)

Q6Which Korean word means "last"?

  • ① 첫
  • ② 이틀
  • ③ 마지막
  • ④ 나흘

마지막 means last; final. 시험의 마지막 문제를 풀었어요. (I solved the last question of the exam.)
