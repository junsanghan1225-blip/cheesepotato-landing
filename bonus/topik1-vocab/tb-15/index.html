Q1What does 잃다 mean?

  • ① commit an error
  • ② lose
  • ③ directly
  • ④ convenience

잃다 means lose. 방향을 잃지 마세요. (Do not lose your way.)

Q2Which Korean word means "to catch"?

  • ① 줍다
  • ② 펴다
  • ③ 잡다
  • ④ 흘리다

잡다 means to catch; to hold. 날아가는 공을 잡았어요. (I caught the flying ball.)

Q3What does 직접 mean?

  • ① turn on (device)
  • ② wave
  • ③ envelope
  • ④ directly

직접 means directly; in person. 선생님을 직접 만나요. (I meet the teacher in person.)

Q4Which Korean word means "turn on (device)"?

  • ① 틀다
  • ② 헤어지다
  • ③ 택배
  • ④ 잡다

틀다 means turn on (device); twist. 텔레비전을 틀었어요. (I turned on the television.)

Q5What does 행동 mean?

  • ① vending machine
  • ② behavior
  • ③ sleep
  • ④ to come visit

행동 means behavior; action. 그의 행동을 눈여겨보았어요. (I watched his actions carefully.)

Q6Which Korean word means "to spill"?

  • ① 잘못하다
  • ② 직접
  • ③ 흘리다
  • ④ 편리

흘리다 means to spill; to shed; to drop. 감동해서 눈물을 흘려요. (I shed tears being touched.)
