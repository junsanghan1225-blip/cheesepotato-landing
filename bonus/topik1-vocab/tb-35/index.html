퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 남녀 mean?

  • ① male
  • ② men and women
  • ③ by oneself
  • ④ uncle

남녀 means men and women; both sexes. 남녀 학생들이 함께 모여 토론해요. (Male and female students gather together to discuss.)

Q2Which Korean word means "rich person"?

  • ① 성격
  • ② 착하다
  • ③ 부자
  • ④ 결혼

부자 means rich person. 부자가 되어 이웃을 돕고 싶어요. (I want to become wealthy and help neighbors.)

Q3What does 스스로 mean?

  • ① oneself
  • ② our country
  • ③ to take (someone along)
  • ④ by oneself

스스로 means by oneself; voluntarily. 스스로 결정하고 책임을 져요. (I decide on my own and take responsibility.)

Q4Which Korean word means "oneself"?

  • ① 자신
  • ② 친척
  • ③ 노인
  • ④ 부자

자신 means oneself; confidence. 시험에 자신이 있어요. (I have confidence in the exam.)

Q5What does 관계 mean?

  • ① paternal aunt
  • ② relationship
  • ③ to be smart
  • ④ to match

관계 means relationship; relation. 두 사람의 관계가 아주 가까워요. (The relationship between the two is very close.)

Q6Which Korean word means "marriage"?

  • ① 남성
  • ② 스스로
  • ③ 결혼
  • ④ 삼촌

결혼 means marriage; wedding; union; matrimony. 결혼해서 행복하게 살고 있어요. (I got married and am living happily.)
