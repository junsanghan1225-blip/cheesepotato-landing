퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 특별히 mean?

  • ① kind
  • ② specially
  • ③ to cross
  • ④ a couple of

특별히 means specially; particularly; especially. 특별히 부탁할 일이 있어요. (I have a favor to ask especially.)

Q2Which Korean word means "number"?

  • ① 나머지
  • ② 대부분
  • ③ 숫자
  • ④ 서너

숫자 means number; figure. 일부터 십까지 숫자를 세어요. (I count numbers from one to ten.)

Q3What does 넘다 mean?

  • ① generation
  • ② to subtract
  • ③ part
  • ④ to cross

넘다 means to cross; to exceed. 산길을 넘어 이웃 마을로 가요. (I go over the mountain path to the neighboring village.)

Q4Which Korean word means "generation"?

  • ① 대
  • ② 번째
  • ③ 예순
  • ④ 숫자

대 means generation; unit counter. 집에 컴퓨터 두 대를 두고 써요. (I keep and use two computers at home.)

Q5What does 둘째 mean?

  • ① fifty
  • ② second
  • ③ order
  • ④ fourth

둘째 means second; 2nd. 제 둘째 동생은 초등학생이에요. (My second younger sibling is an elementary student.)

Q6Which Korean word means "three or four"?

  • ① 종류
  • ② 넘다
  • ③ 서너
  • ④ 두세

서너 means three or four. 사과 서너 개만 사 오세요. (Please buy and bring three or four apples.)
