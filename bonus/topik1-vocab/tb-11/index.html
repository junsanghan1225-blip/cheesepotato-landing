Q1What does 반바지 mean?

  • ① ring
  • ② shorts
  • ③ swimsuit
  • ④ sportswear

반바지 means shorts; short pants. 반바지에 흰 티셔츠를 입었어요. (I wore a white T-shirt with shorts.)

Q2Which Korean word means "size"?

  • ① 손수건
  • ② 와이셔츠
  • ③ 사이즈
  • ④ 화장품

사이즈 means size; measurement. 옷이 조금 작아서 큰 사이즈로 바꿔요. (The clothes are small, so I change to a larger size.)

Q3What does 수영복 mean?

  • ① suit (western style)
  • ② blue jeans
  • ③ calculation
  • ④ swimsuit

수영복 means swimsuit; bathing suit. 바다에 가려고 새 수영복을 샀어요. (I bought a new swimsuit to go to the sea.)

Q4Which Korean word means "suit (western style)"?

  • ① 양복
  • ② 유행
  • ③ 동전
  • ④ 사이즈

양복 means suit (western style). 백화점에서 남성 양복을 맞췄어요. (I had a men's suit tailored at the department store.)

Q5What does 원피스 mean?

  • ① bank account book
  • ② one-piece dress
  • ③ blouse
  • ④ sweater

원피스 means one-piece dress; dress. 원피스를 한 벌 샀어요. (I bought a one-piece dress.)

Q6Which Korean word means "cosmetics"?

  • ① 반지
  • ② 수영복
  • ③ 화장품
  • ④ 운동복

화장품 means cosmetics; makeup. 피부에 맞는 화장품을 써요. (I use cosmetics that suit my skin.)
