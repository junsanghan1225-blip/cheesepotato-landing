퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 슬프다 mean?

  • ① happy
  • ② to be sad
  • ③ being sorry
  • ④ to cook

슬프다 means to be sad; to be sorrowful. 슬픈 영화를 보고 나서 눈물이 주르륵 흘렀어요. (After watching a sad movie, tears streamed down.)

Q2Which Korean word means "to want to"?

  • ① 후회하다
  • ② 걱정되다
  • ③ 싶어지다
  • ④ 재미없다

싶어지다 means to want to; to come to desire. 노래를 들으니 춤추고 싶어져요. (Listening to the song, I want to dance.)

Q3What does 기쁘다 mean?

  • ① be worried
  • ② not interesting
  • ③ to be sad
  • ④ happy

기쁘다 means happy; glad. 시험에 합격해서 정말 기쁩니다. (I am truly happy because I passed the test.)

Q4Which Korean word means "to become comfortable"?

  • ① 편안해지다
  • ② 사랑
  • ③ 요리
  • ④ 싶어지다

편안해지다 means to become comfortable. 집에 돌아오니 몸이 편안해졌어요. (My body felt comfortable after coming home.)

Q5What does 미안 mean?

  • ① to cook
  • ② being sorry
  • ③ to be uncomfortable
  • ④ agonize

미안 means being sorry. 기다리게 해서 정말 미안해요. (I am really sorry to have kept you waiting.)

Q6Which Korean word means "not interesting"?

  • ① 행복하다
  • ② 미안
  • ③ 재미없다
  • ④ 요리

재미없다 means not interesting; boring. 재미없는 책을 읽다가 졸았어요. (I dozed off while reading an uninteresting book.)
