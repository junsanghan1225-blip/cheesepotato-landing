퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 뭐 mean?

  • ① where
  • ② what (casual)
  • ③ how
  • ④ to tidy up

뭐 means what (casual). 오늘 점심 메뉴로 뭐 먹을래요? (What do you want to eat for lunch today?)

Q2Which Korean word means "which"?

  • ① 어떤
  • ② 청소하다
  • ③ 어느
  • ④ 빗자루

어느 means which; some. 두 옷 중에서 어느 옷이 더 마음에 들어요? (Between the two clothes, which one do you like more?)

Q3What does 어떻다 mean?

  • ① to be clean
  • ② to repair
  • ③ desk
  • ④ how

어떻다 means how; what ... is like. 오늘 날씨가 어떤지 창밖을 내다봐요. (I look out the window to see how the weather is today.)

Q4Which Korean word means "to be clean"?

  • ① 깨끗하다
  • ② 깨끗해지다
  • ③ 켜다
  • ④ 어느

깨끗하다 means to be clean. 방을 깨끗하게 청소하니 기분이 상쾌해요. (Cleaning the room cleanly makes me feel refreshed.)

Q5What does 청소 mean?

  • ① to turn off
  • ② cleaning
  • ③ who
  • ④ what (formal)

청소 means cleaning. 주말 아침에 온 가족이 모여 집 안 대청소를 해요. (On weekend mornings, the whole family gathers and does a big house cleaning.)

Q6Which Korean word means "broom"?

  • ① 어디
  • ② 어떻다
  • ③ 빗자루
  • ④ 정리하다

빗자루 means broom. 마당 구석에 큰 빗자루를 두었어요. (I placed a big broom in the corner of the yard.)
