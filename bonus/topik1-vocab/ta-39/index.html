퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 알려주다 mean?

  • ① mean
  • ② to inform
  • ③ being famous
  • ④ letter

알려주다 means to inform; to let know. 새로운 연락처를 친구에게 알려주세요. (Please let your friend know your new contact information.)

Q2Which Korean word means "well"?

  • ① 아니다
  • ② 보내다
  • ③ 글쎄요
  • ④ 전화번호

글쎄요 means well; hm. 글쎄요, 생각해 보고 말씀드릴게요. (Well, let me think about it and tell you.)

Q3What does 유명 mean?

  • ① call
  • ② hello (on phone)
  • ③ to ask for
  • ④ being famous

유명 means being famous; being popular. 유명한 관광지에 사람이 많아요. (There are many people at famous tourist attractions.)

Q4Which Korean word means "call"?

  • ① 전화하다
  • ② 통화하다
  • ③ 묻다
  • ④ 글쎄요

전화하다 means call; phone. 저녁 식사 후에 가족들에게 안부 전화를 해요. (After dinner, I call my family to check on them.)

Q5What does 연락드리다 mean?

  • ① guidance
  • ② to contact
  • ③ okay
  • ④ telephone

연락드리다 means to contact; to get in touch. 도착해서 부모님께 연락드렸어요. (I arrived and contacted my parents.)

Q6Which Korean word means "phone number"?

  • ① 뜻하다
  • ② 유명
  • ③ 전화번호
  • ④ 편지

전화번호 means phone number. 전화번호가 바뀌어서 알려 드려요. (My phone number has changed, so I let you know.)
