퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 시계 mean?

  • ① lunch time
  • ② clock
  • ③ sun
  • ④ just

시계 means clock; watch. 시계를 보고 시간을 확인해요. (I look at the clock and check the time.)

Q2Which Korean word means "meanwhile"?

  • ① 도
  • ② 덕분
  • ③ 그동안
  • ④ 반찬

그동안 means meanwhile; during that time. 그동안 한국어 실력이 늘었어요. (During that time, my Korean skills improved.)

Q3What does 해 mean?

  • ① to wish
  • ② chopsticks
  • ③ (in -ㄹ 수 있다) can
  • ④ sun

해 means sun; year. 새해가 되면 가족들과 함께 일출을 보러 가요. (When the new year comes, I go to see the sunrise with my family.)

Q4Which Korean word means "to wish"?

  • ① 바라다
  • ② 즐겁다
  • ③ 배달
  • ④ 그동안

바라다 means to wish; to hope. 새해에는 온 가족의 건강과 성공을 간절히 바라요. (In the new year, I sincerely wish for the health and success of the whole family.)

Q5What does 편하다 mean?

  • ① cafe
  • ② to be comfortable
  • ③ future
  • ④ to be cold

편하다 means to be comfortable; to be easy. 이 신발은 오래 걸어도 발이 아주 편해요. (These shoes keep my feet very comfortable even when walking long.)

Q6Which Korean word means "side dish"?

  • ① 점심시간
  • ② 해
  • ③ 반찬
  • ④ 그냥

반찬 means side dish. 식탁에 맛있는 반찬이 많아요. (There are many delicious side dishes on the table.)
