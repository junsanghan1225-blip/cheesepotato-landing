퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 메시지 mean?

  • ① contact information
  • ② message
  • ③ refusal
  • ④ mobile phone

메시지 means message. 메시지를 확인하고 바로 답장해요. (I check the message and reply immediately.)

Q2Which Korean word means "phone conversation"?

  • ① 올림
  • ② 혹시
  • ③ 통화
  • ④ 전화기

통화 means phone conversation; call. 지금 통화 괜찮으세요? (Are you okay to talk on the phone now?)

Q3What does 거절 mean?

  • ① ask
  • ② mail
  • ③ center
  • ④ refusal

거절 means refusal; rejection; denial. 친구의 제안을 거절하기 미안해요. (I feel sorry to decline my friend's suggestion.)

Q4Which Korean word means "ask"?

  • ① 여쭙다
  • ② 누르다
  • ③ 항공권
  • ④ 통화

여쭙다 means ask; inquire (honorific). 한 가지 여쭙고 싶어요. (I would like to ask one thing.)

Q5What does 연결 mean?

  • ① aviation
  • ② connection
  • ③ postcard
  • ④ to send

연결 means connection. 컴퓨터와 모니터를 케이블로 연결해요. (I connect the computer and monitor with a cable.)

Q6Which Korean word means "telephone"?

  • ① 연락처
  • ② 거절
  • ③ 전화기
  • ④ 휴대폰

전화기 means telephone; phone device. 새 전화기를 샀어요. (I bought a new telephone.)
