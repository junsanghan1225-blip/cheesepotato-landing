Q1What does 수영장 mean?

  • ① hike
  • ② swimming pool
  • ③ walking
  • ④ soccer

수영장 means swimming pool. 주말 아침마다 건강을 위해 동네 수영장에 다녀요. (Every weekend morning, I attend the local swimming pool for my health.)

Q2Which Korean word means "water play"?

  • ① 미끄럼틀
  • ② 트랙
  • ③ 물놀이
  • ④ 치다

물놀이 means water play; swimming. 아이들이 수영장에서 물놀이를 즐겨요. (Children enjoy playing in the water at the pool.)

Q3What does 걷기 mean?

  • ① warm-up
  • ② baseball
  • ③ dog
  • ④ walking

걷기 means walking. 건강을 위해 걷기 습관을 길러요. (I build a walking habit for my health.)

Q4Which Korean word means "warm-up"?

  • ① 준비운동
  • ② 스키
  • ③ 테니스
  • ④ 물놀이

준비운동 means warm-up. 준비운동으로 몸의 긴장을 풀어요. (I release body tension with warm-up exercise.)

Q5What does 농구 mean?

  • ① Taekwondo
  • ② basketball
  • ③ swim
  • ④ kick

농구 means basketball. 텔레비전으로 농구 경기를 봤어요. (I watched a basketball game on TV.)

Q6Which Korean word means "to play (a racket or ball sport, an instrument)"?

  • ① 등산
  • ② 걷기
  • ③ 치다
  • ④ 축구

치다 means to play (a racket or ball sport, an instrument); to hit. 어릴 때부터 피아노를 쳤어요. (I have played the piano since childhood.)
