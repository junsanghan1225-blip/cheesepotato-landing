퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 기침 mean?

  • ① smell
  • ② cough
  • ③ stomachache
  • ④ to be sick

기침 means cough; coughing. 기침을 할 때는 입을 가리세요. (Please cover your mouth when you cough.)

Q2Which Korean word means "headache"?

  • ① 바르다
  • ② 콧물
  • ③ 두통
  • ④ 다양

두통 means headache. 스트레스를 받으면 두통이 생겨요. (When I get stressed, a headache occurs.)

Q3What does 배탈 mean?

  • ① sneeze
  • ② to increase
  • ③ to climb
  • ④ stomachache

배탈 means stomachache; upset stomach; stomach upset. 배탈 때문에 죽을 끓여 먹어요. (I boil and eat porridge because of an upset stomach.)

Q4Which Korean word means "sneeze"?

  • ① 재채기
  • ② 변하다
  • ③ 오래되다
  • ④ 두통

재채기 means sneeze; sneezing. 자꾸 재채기를 해요. (I keep sneezing.)

Q5What does 그대로 mean?

  • ① to open
  • ② as it is
  • ③ be injured
  • ④ wound

그대로 means as it is; intact. 선생님 말씀을 그대로 따라 해요. (I follow the teacher's words as they are.)

Q6Which Korean word means "diversity"?

  • ① 냄새
  • ② 배탈
  • ③ 다양
  • ④ 편찮다

다양 means diversity; variety. 다양한 사람들과 이야기하며 배워요. (I learn while talking with diverse people.)
