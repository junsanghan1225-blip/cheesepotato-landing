퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 가게 mean?

  • ① convenience store
  • ② shop
  • ③ to sell
  • ④ coupon

가게 means shop; store. 집 앞 작은 과일 가게에서 달콤한 딸기를 샀어요. (I bought sweet strawberries at the small fruit shop in front of my house.)

Q2Which Korean word means "thing"?

  • ① 바꾸다
  • ② 전통시장
  • ③ 물건
  • ④ 백화점

물건 means thing; item; goods. 필요한 물건을 사기 위해 마트에 들렀어요. (I stopped by the supermarket to buy needed items.)

Q3What does 팔다 mean?

  • ① to choose
  • ② grocery shopping
  • ③ today
  • ④ to sell

팔다 means to sell. 주말 벼룩시장에서 안 쓰는 헌 책과 물건을 팔아요. (At the weekend flea market, I sell used books and unused items.)

Q4Which Korean word means "to choose"?

  • ① 고르다
  • ② 우표
  • ③ 슈퍼마켓
  • ④ 물건

고르다 means to choose; to select. 친구의 생일 축하 파티에 줄 예쁜 선물을 골라요. (I choose a pretty gift to give at my friend's birthday celebration party.)

Q5What does 영업시간 mean?

  • ① shopping
  • ② business hours
  • ③ to be cheap
  • ④ price

영업시간 means business hours. 문 앞에 영업시간 안내가 붙었어요. (A notice of business hours is posted on the door.)

Q6Which Korean word means "department store"?

  • ① 편의점
  • ② 팔다
  • ③ 백화점
  • ④ 쿠폰

백화점 means department store. 주말이라 백화점에 사람이 많아요. (There are many people at the department store on weekends.)
