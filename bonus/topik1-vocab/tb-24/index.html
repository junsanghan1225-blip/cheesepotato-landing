Q1What does 만두 mean?

  • ① seaweed soup
  • ② mandu
  • ③ lettuce
  • ④ spaghetti

만두 means mandu; dumpling. 분식집에서 고기만두를 주문했어요. (I ordered meat dumplings at the snack bar.)

Q2Which Korean word means "snack food"?

  • ① 삼겹살
  • ② 순두부찌개
  • ③ 분식
  • ④ 야채

분식 means snack food; flour-based food. 분식은 싸고 간편해서 인기가 많아요. (Snack food is popular because it is cheap and convenient.)

Q3What does 상추 mean?

  • ① spoon and chopsticks
  • ② chew
  • ③ jajangmyeon
  • ④ lettuce

상추 means lettuce. 텃밭에서 싱싱한 상추를 땄어요. (I picked fresh lettuce in the vegetable garden.)

Q4Which Korean word means "spoon and chopsticks"?

  • ① 수저
  • ② 식품
  • ③ 일식
  • ④ 분식

수저 means spoon and chopsticks. 수저통에서 숟가락을 꺼냈어요. (I took a spoon out of the cutlery holder.)

Q5What does 식빵 mean?

  • ① udon
  • ② loaf bread
  • ③ fried rice
  • ④ sandwich

식빵 means loaf bread; white bread. 식빵에 달콤한 딸기잼을 발라요. (I spread sweet strawberry jam on loaf bread.)

Q6Which Korean word means "vegetables"?

  • ① 미역국
  • ② 상추
  • ③ 야채
  • ④ 스파게티

야채 means vegetables; greens. 시장에서 신선한 야채를 샀어요. (I bought fresh vegetables at the market.)
