Q1What does 안빈낙도 mean?

  • ① the more the better
  • ② living comfortably in poverty while enjoying the righteous path
  • ③ turning a misfortune into a blessing
  • ④ caught in a dilemma

안빈낙도 means living comfortably in poverty while enjoying the righteous path; simple contented life. 시골로 내려간 선배는 텃밭을 가꾸며 안빈낙도의 삶을 즐겨요. (My senior who moved to the countryside enjoys a simple, contented life cultivating a garden.)

Q2Which four-character idiom means "indomitable"?

  • ① 과유불급
  • ② 자업자득
  • ③ 백절불굴
  • ④ 천편일률

백절불굴 means indomitable; undaunted by repeated setbacks; never giving up. 수차례 시험에 낙방했음에도 백절불굴의 각오로 마침내 합격했어요. (Despite failing the exam multiple times, he finally passed with an indomitable resolve.)

Q3What does 사면초가 mean?

  • ① looking on with folded arms
  • ② insolent
  • ③ countless
  • ④ surrounded by enemies on all sides

사면초가 means surrounded by enemies on all sides; utterly isolated. 믿었던 동료들마저 등을 돌리니 사면초가에 빠진 기분이었어요. (When even the colleagues I trusted turned their backs, I felt utterly isolated and trapped.)

Q4Which four-character idiom means "desperate crisis"?

  • ① 절체절명
  • ② 문전성시
  • ③ 탁상공론
  • ④ 자화자찬

절체절명 means desperate crisis; facing life-or-death crossroads. 배가 암초에 부딪쳐 침몰하기 직전의 절체절명 순간에 구조대가 왔어요. (Rescue arrived at the desperate life-or-death moment right before the ship struck reefs and sank.)

Q5What does 일장춘몽 mean?

  • ① bluffing
  • ② an empty spring dream
  • ③ clear at a glance
  • ④ as different as heaven and earth

일장춘몽 means an empty spring dream; transient glory; vanity of vanities. 젊은 날의 화려했던 부귀영화도 나이 드니 일장춘몽 같더라고요. (The glamorous wealth and fame of my youth now feels like a fleeting spring dream.)

Q6Which four-character idiom means "divine intervention"?

  • ① 지피지기
  • ② 망연자실
  • ③ 천우신조
  • ④ 일거양득

천우신조 means divine intervention; help by heaven and deities; miraculous stroke of luck. 대형 사고였는데 다친 사람 없이 끝난 것은 천우신조 덕분이에요. (Ending without injured people despite a major accident was thanks to a divine stroke of luck.)
