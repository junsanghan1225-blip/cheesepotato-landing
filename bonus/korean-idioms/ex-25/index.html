퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 유유상종 mean?

  • ① the more the better
  • ② like attracts like
  • ③ like father, like son
  • ④ ingratitude

유유상종 means like attracts like; people of the same kind flock together. 운동 좋아하는 사람들끼리 금방 모이는 걸 보면 유유상종이에요. (Seeing how people who like sports gather so quickly, like attracts like.)

Q2Which four-character idiom means "erudite"?

  • ① 진퇴양난
  • ② 동병상련
  • ③ 박학다식
  • ④ 우유부단

박학다식 means erudite; learned; possessing encyclopedic knowledge. 역사와 과학을 아우르며 술술 설명하시는 교수님은 정말 박학다식해요. (The professor who smoothly explains across history and science is truly erudite.)

Q3What does 주경야독 mean?

  • ① exaggerating a minor matter
  • ② hitting the bull's-eye every time
  • ③ marital vow for a hundred years
  • ④ working by day and studying by night

주경야독 means working by day and studying by night; hard study. 낮에는 직장에 다니고 밤에는 대학원에서 주경야독하고 있어요. (I work at my office during the day and study at night in graduate school.)

Q4Which four-character idiom means "he who tied the knot must untie it"?

  • ① 결자해지
  • ② 전무후무
  • ③ 난형난제
  • ④ 유유자적

결자해지 means he who tied the knot must untie it; one who caused the problem should solve it. 문제를 일으킨 당사자가 결자해지의 자세로 책임지고 수습해야 해요. (The party who caused the problem should take responsibility and resolve it in the spirit of solving one's own knot.)

Q5What does 일벌백계 mean?

  • ① desperate crisis
  • ② punishing one as a warning to many
  • ③ Mencius' mother moving three times for education
  • ④ indomitable

일벌백계 means punishing one as a warning to many; making an example of someone. 음주 운전은 무고한 사람을 해치므로 일벌백계로 엄벌해야 해요. (Because drunk driving harms innocent people, it must be severely punished to warn everyone.)

Q6Which four-character idiom means "arguing pro and con"?

  • ① 만시지탄
  • ② 망연자실
  • ③ 갑론을박
  • ④ 일거양득

갑론을박 means arguing pro and con; heated debates back and forth. 주차장 유료화 문제를 놓고 아파트 입주민들 사이에 갑론을박이 벌어졌어요. (A heated back-and-forth dispute arose among apartment tenants over paid parking.)
