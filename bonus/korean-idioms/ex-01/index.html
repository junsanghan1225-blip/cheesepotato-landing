퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 숨을 쉬다 mean?

  • ① to be loose-lipped
  • ② to breathe
  • ③ lip service
  • ④ to be humiliated

숨을 쉬다 means to breathe; to take a breath; to respire. 미세먼지가 너무 심한 날에는 바깥에서 마스크 없이 숨을 쉬기조차 힘들어요. (On days with severe fine dust, it is hard even to breathe outside without masks.)

Q2Which idiom means "to use one's strength"?

  • ① 바람을 피우다
  • ② 손에 땀을 쥐다
  • ③ 힘을 쓰다
  • ④ 귀를 기울이다

힘을 쓰다 means to use one's strength; to exert influence or pull strings. 무거운 냉장고를 혼자 옮기느라 허리에 온 힘을 쓰는 바람에 통증이 생겼어요. (Moving the heavy fridge alone, using all my strength strained my back and caused pain.)

Q3What does 땀을 흘리다 mean?

  • ① to click one's tongue
  • ② to fall to one's knees
  • ③ to take the first step
  • ④ to sweat

땀을 흘리다 means to sweat; to work by the sweat of one's brow. 여름철 뙤약볕 아래서 밭일을 하느라 온몸이 흠뻑 젖도록 땀을 흘렸어요. (Doing field work under the summer sun, I sweated until soaked all over.)

Q4Which idiom means "to be ripped off"?

  • ① 바가지를 쓰다
  • ② 입맛을 돋우다
  • ③ 갈피를 못 잡다
  • ④ 혀를 내두르다

바가지를 쓰다 means to be ripped off; to be overcharged. 관광지 식당에서 평소 가격보다 세 배나 비싸게 바가지를 썼어요. (At a tourist site restaurant, I was ripped off paying triple the normal price.)

Q5What does 잠이 들다 mean?

  • ① to be caught red-handed
  • ② to fall asleep
  • ③ to keep a promise
  • ④ to take responsibility

잠이 들다 means to fall asleep; to drop off to sleep. 하루 종일 산행을 다녀와서 너무 피곤했던지 눕자마자 바로 잠이 들었어요. (So tired after hiking all day, I fell asleep right after lying down.)

Q6Which idiom means "to make time"?

  • ① 손을 잡다
  • ② 영향을 미치다
  • ③ 시간을 내다
  • ④ 손이 크다

시간을 내다 means to make time; to spare some time for someone or something. 바쁘신 와중에도 소중한 시간을 내어 참석해 주셔서 진심으로 감사합니다. (Thank you very much for sparing precious time to attend amidst your busy schedule.)
