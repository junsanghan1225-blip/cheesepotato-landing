Q1What does 과유불급 mean?

  • ① the more the better
  • ② excess is as bad as deficiency
  • ③ working by day and studying by night
  • ④ matching words with deeds

과유불급 means excess is as bad as deficiency; moderation in all things. 건강에 좋은 영양제라도 과유불급이니 적정량을 지켜서 드세요. (Even for health supplements, too much is as bad as too little, so take the proper dose.)

Q2Which four-character idiom means "occurring not once or twice"?

  • ① 구사일생
  • ② 화기애애
  • ③ 비일비재
  • ④ 감언이설

비일비재 means occurring not once or twice; common; frequent. 휴대폰을 보며 걷다가 행인과 부딪치는 사고는 비일비재해요. (Collisions with pedestrians while walking and staring at smartphones happen all the time.)

Q3What does 일사천리 mean?

  • ① a third party reaping the profit while two others quarrel
  • ② useless thing
  • ③ marital vow for a hundred years
  • ④ progressing smoothly and rapidly without a hitch

일사천리 means progressing smoothly and rapidly without a hitch. 모두가 적극 협조해 준 덕분에 계약 체결이 일사천리로 진행됐어요. (Thanks to everyone's active cooperation, contract signing proceeded swimmingly without a hitch.)

Q4Which four-character idiom means "full of ups and downs"?

  • ① 파란만장
  • ② 살신성인
  • ③ 난형난제
  • ④ 호가호위

파란만장 means full of ups and downs; turbulent; full of vicissitudes. 할머니의 파란만장했던 지난 삶 이야기를 들으며 눈물을 흘렸어요. (I shed tears listening to the story of my grandmother's past life full of ups and downs.)

Q5What does 살기등등 mean?

  • ① treasuring dearly
  • ② radiating murderous intent
  • ③ relationship like water and fish
  • ④ cutting and polishing jade

살기등등 means radiating murderous intent; fierce and menacing atmosphere. 골목길에서 살기등등한 눈빛으로 시비를 거는 사람을 마주쳤어요. (In the alley, I encountered a person picking a fight with menacing, fierce glare in the eyes.)

Q6Which four-character idiom means "living free and leisurely without worldly cares"?

  • ① 일벌백계
  • ② 망연자실
  • ③ 유유자적
  • ④ 일거양득

유유자적 means living free and leisurely without worldly cares; taking things easy. 은퇴 후에는 바닷가 마을에서 유유자적 낚시나 하고 싶어요. (After retirement, I want to live leisurely, fishing in a quiet seaside village.)
