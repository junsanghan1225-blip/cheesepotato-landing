퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 호가호위 mean?

  • ① the more the better
  • ② borrowing someone else's authority to bully others
  • ③ working by day and studying by night
  • ④ alternating between joy and grief

호가호위 means borrowing someone else's authority to bully others; swaggering in borrowed plumes. 부장의 친척이라고 호가호위하며 거들먹거리면 다들 싫어해요. (Everyone dislikes it when someone swaggers around borrowing the manager's power as his relative.)

Q2Which four-character idiom means "ingratitude"?

  • ① 자포자기
  • ② 화기애애
  • ③ 배은망덕
  • ④ 노심초사

배은망덕 means ingratitude; biting the hand that feeds you. 키워 준 부모님의 뜻을 무시하고 떠나다니 배은망덕한 짓이에요. (Disregarding the will of the parents who raised you is an act of biting the hand that fed you.)

Q3What does 아전인수 mean?

  • ① a third party reaping the profit while two others quarrel
  • ② winning every battle
  • ③ enemies in the same boat
  • ④ seeking one's own advantage

아전인수 means seeking one's own advantage; self-serving interpretation. 규정을 자기한테 유리하게만 해석하는 건 아전인수격 주장이에요. (Interpreting rules only to one's own benefit is a self-serving argument.)

Q4Which four-character idiom means "bluffing"?

  • ① 허장성세
  • ② 비일비재
  • ③ 기사회생
  • ④ 대동소이

허장성세 means bluffing; putting on a brave front with empty bluster. 겉으로는 부자 행세를 하지만 실상은 빚더미에 오른 허장성세였어요. (Pretending to be rich on the outside was mere empty bluffing, while in truth drowned in debt.)

Q5What does 교언영색 mean?

  • ① treasuring dearly
  • ② sweet talk and flattering manners
  • ③ relationship like water and fish
  • ④ innocent and naive

교언영색 means sweet talk and flattering manners; glib speech and an oily smile. 교언영색으로 남을 속이려는 사람의 말은 믿지 않는 게 좋아요. (It is better not to trust words from someone trying to deceive others with flattering manners.)

Q6Which four-character idiom means "deceiving others with shallow tricks"?

  • ① 무소불위
  • ② 안분지족
  • ③ 조삼모사
  • ④ 일석이조

조삼모사 means deceiving others with shallow tricks; playing fast and loose. 가격을 올려놓고 할인해 주는 척하는 건 조삼모사나 다름없어요. (Raising prices and pretending to discount them is no different from shallow deception.)
