Q1What does 수어지교 mean?

  • ① the more the better
  • ② relationship like water and fish
  • ③ turning a misfortune into a blessing
  • ④ caught in a dilemma

수어지교 means relationship like water and fish; inseparable friendship or partnership. 창업 동기와 저는 오랜 시간 수어지교의 우정을 나누고 있어요. (My startup co-founder and I have shared an inseparable friendship for a long time.)

Q2Which four-character idiom means "an intimate friend who understands one's heart"?

  • ① 과유불급
  • ② 자업자득
  • ③ 지기지우
  • ④ 침소봉대

지기지우 means an intimate friend who understands one's heart; soulmate; true confidant. 오랜 세월을 함께하며 비밀을 나누는 지기지우가 있어 든든해요. (It is reassuring to have a bosom friend with whom I have shared secrets over many years.)

Q3What does 괄목상대 mean?

  • ① looking on with folded arms
  • ② caught in one's own trap
  • ③ occurring not once or twice
  • ④ rubbing one's eyes and looking at someone anew

괄목상대 means rubbing one's eyes and looking at someone anew; astonishing growth. 매일 밤 연습하더니 그 후배의 피아노 실력이 괄목상대하게 늘었어요. (Practicing every night, that junior's piano skills improved so remarkably one rubs one's eyes.)

Q4Which four-character idiom means "Mencius' mother moving three times for education"?

  • ① 맹모삼천
  • ② 구우일모
  • ③ 상전벽해
  • ④ 절체절명

맹모삼천 means Mencius' mother moving three times for education; parents moving for ideal study environment. 학군 좋은 동네로 이사하는 부모들을 보면 맹모삼천이 떠올라요. (Seeing parents move to neighborhoods with good school districts brings Mencius' mother to mind.)

Q5What does 타산지석 mean?

  • ① crying wine and selling vinegar
  • ② learning from another's faults
  • ③ borrowing someone else's authority to bully others
  • ④ as different as heaven and earth

타산지석 means learning from another's faults; a negative example to learn from. 동료의 부주의한 실패를 타산지석으로 삼아 우리는 실수를 예방했어요. (Taking our colleague's careless failure as a lesson, we prevented similar mistakes.)

Q6Which four-character idiom means "sacrificing one's own life for righteousness and others"?

  • ① 파란만장
  • ② 망연자실
  • ③ 살신성인
  • ④ 일거양득

살신성인 means sacrificing one's own life for righteousness and others. 불길 속에 뛰어들어 아이를 구하고 숨진 의인의 살신성인에 울컥해요. (Moved to tears by the righteous hero's self-sacrifice, leaping into flames to save a child.)
