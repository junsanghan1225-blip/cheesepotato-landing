퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 고진감래 mean?

  • ① giving an irrelevant or off-topic answer
  • ② sweetness comes after bitterness
  • ③ like father, like son
  • ④ existing in name only

고진감래 means sweetness comes after bitterness; joy after hardship. 힘든 시험공부를 오래 견뎠으니 이제 고진감래가 찾아올 거예요. (You endured tough exam prep for long, so sweet success will surely come now.)

Q2Which four-character idiom means "alternating between joy and grief"?

  • ① 오리무중
  • ② 일장춘몽
  • ③ 일희일비
  • ④ 오매불망

일희일비 means alternating between joy and grief; swayed by petty gains. 주식 시세가 조금 오르내린다고 일희일비하면 마음만 불안해져요. (If you ride an emotional roller coaster over small stock price swings, you will only grow anxious.)

Q3What does 각골난망 mean?

  • ① erudite
  • ② a drop in the ocean
  • ③ mulberry fields turn into blue seas
  • ④ gratitude etched into one's bones

각골난망 means gratitude etched into one's bones; unforgettable kindness. 가난했던 시절 베풀어 주신 은혜는 각골난망하여 평생 잊지 않겠습니다. (The kindness you bestowed in poor times is deeply etched in my bones, and I will never forget it.)

Q4Which four-character idiom means "fellow sufferers pity each other"?

  • ① 동병상련
  • ② 견물생심
  • ③ 당랑거철
  • ④ 호가호위

동병상련 means fellow sufferers pity each other; mutual sympathy. 같은 시험에서 떨어진 친구끼리 동병상련의 마음으로 위로했어요. (Friends who failed the same exam comforted each other with shared sympathy.)

Q5What does 설상가상 mean?

  • ① making continuous progress without hindrance
  • ② misfortunes never come singly
  • ③ relationship like water and fish
  • ④ indomitable

설상가상 means misfortunes never come singly; adding frost to snow. 지갑을 잃어버렸는데 설상가상으로 휴대폰 배터리마저 다 닳았어요. (I lost my wallet, and to make matters worse, my phone battery died completely.)

Q6Which four-character idiom means "crowded with visitors"?

  • ① 동량지재
  • ② 안분지족
  • ③ 문전성시
  • ④ 일거양득

문전성시 means crowded with visitors; customers swarming like a bustling market. 방송에 맛집으로 소개된 이후 식당 앞은 연일 문전성시를 이뤄요. (Ever since introduced as a tasty spot on TV, the restaurant gate swarms with guests daily.)
