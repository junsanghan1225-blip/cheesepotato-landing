Q1What does 골치를 앓다 mean?

  • ① to be bold
  • ② to suffer a headache over
  • ③ to roll up one's sleeves
  • ④ to push someone into doing something

골치를 앓다 means to suffer a headache over; to be plagued by troubles. 주차 공간이 부족해서 아파트 주민들이 매일 골치를 앓고 있어요. (Because of insufficient parking, apartment residents suffer headaches daily.)

Q2Which idiom means "to be on the edge of one's seat"?

  • ① 허리띠를 졸라매다
  • ② 혀를 차다
  • ③ 손에 땀을 쥐다
  • ④ 벽에 부딪히다

손에 땀을 쥐다 means to be on the edge of one's seat; breathless suspense. 월드컵 축구 경기 결승전은 손에 땀을 쥐게 할 만큼 박진감이 넘쳤어요. (The World Cup final match was so thrilling that it kept everyone on the edge of their seats.)

Q3What does 찬물을 끼얹다 mean?

  • ① to act as a go-between
  • ② to put an end to
  • ③ to rack one's brain
  • ④ to throw cold water on

찬물을 끼얹다 means to throw cold water on; to dampen the mood or progress. 축제 분위기로 들떠 있는 자리에 부서장이 찬물을 끼얹는 쓴소리를 했어요. (In a festive mood, the department head poured cold water with bitter remarks.)

Q4Which idiom means "to sweat bullets"?

  • ① 진땀을 흘리다
  • ② 활기를 띠다
  • ③ 발자취를 남기다
  • ④ 약속을 지키다

진땀을 흘리다 means to sweat bullets; to have a grueling, tough time dealing with something. 면접관의 날카로운 압박 질문에 대답하느라 진땀을 흘렸어요. (Answering the interviewer's sharp grilling questions made me sweat bullets.)

Q5What does 가슴에 못을 박다 mean?

  • ① to stall for time
  • ② to break someone's heart
  • ③ to hold hands
  • ④ to break a promise

가슴에 못을 박다 means to break someone's heart; to deeply wound someone's feelings. 부모님 가슴에 못을 박는 심한 말을 하고 나서 며칠 동안 후회했어요. (I regretted for days after uttering harsh words driving nails into my parents' hearts.)

Q6Which idiom means "to be moved to tears"?

  • ① 한숨을 쉬다
  • ② 소식을 전하다
  • ③ 눈시울이 붉어지다
  • ④ 입이 가볍다

눈시울이 붉어지다 means to be moved to tears; eyes welling up with tears. 어머니의 낡은 손을 꼭 잡는 순간 나도 모르게 눈시울이 붉어졌어요. (Holding my mother's worn hands tightly, tears welled up in my eyes unknowingly.)
