퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 가슴이 벅차다 mean?

  • ① to look familiar
  • ② one's heart is full
  • ③ to be dumbfounded or amazed
  • ④ to stop at nothing

가슴이 벅차다 means one's heart is full; to be overwhelmed with emotion or joy. 오랜 인고 끝에 태극기를 들고 시상대 맨 위에 서니 가슴이 벅찼어요. (Standing atop the podium with national flag after long toil, my heart swelled.)

Q2Which idiom means "to worry oneself sick"?

  • ① 피땀을 흘리다
  • ② 한술 더 뜨다
  • ③ 속을 태우다
  • ④ 발목을 잡히다

속을 태우다 means to worry oneself sick; to be deeply anxious and fretting. 아이의 연락이 밤늦게까지 닿지 않아서 부모가 속을 태웠어요. (With the child unreachable until late at night, parents were worried sick.)

Q3What does 어안이 벙벙하다 mean?

  • ① to stand by with folded arms
  • ② to take the first step
  • ③ to be caught red-handed
  • ④ to be dumbfounded

어안이 벙벙하다 means to be dumbfounded; to be struck dumb with astonishment. 생각지도 못했던 복권 1등에 당첨되자 너무 놀라서 어안이 벙벙했어요. (Winning first prize in lottery unexpectedly, I was too shocked and dumbfounded.)

Q4Which idiom means "to be scared out of one's wits"?

  • ① 간이 콩알만 해지다
  • ② 골머리를 썩이다
  • ③ 시간이 지나다
  • ④ 마음을 잡다

간이 콩알만 해지다 means to be scared out of one's wits; to be terrified. 갑자기 등 뒤에서 큰 소리가 나서 간이 콩알만 해졌어요. (Hearing a sudden loud noise behind my back, I was scared out of my wits.)

Q5What does 바람을 피우다 mean?

  • ① to spend time
  • ② to cheat on someone
  • ③ to cast a glance at
  • ④ to seize an opportunity

바람을 피우다 means to cheat on someone; to have an affair; to be unfaithful. 남편이 다른 사람과 바람을 피우다가 들통나서 큰 위기에 처했어요. (The husband fell into a crisis when his affair with someone else was caught.)

Q6Which idiom means "to beat one's chest in deep sorrow, regret, or despair"?

  • ① 꿈을 꾸다
  • ② 자리를 비우다
  • ③ 가슴을 치다
  • ④ 손이 크다

가슴을 치다 means to beat one's chest in deep sorrow, regret, or despair. 공부할 기회를 놓치고 나이 들어 후회하며 가슴을 쳤어요. (Missing study opportunities and regretting in old age, I beat my chest.)
