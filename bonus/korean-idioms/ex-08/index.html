퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 낯이 설다 mean?

  • ① to be bold
  • ② to be unfamiliar
  • ③ to look away
  • ④ to close one's eyes tight

낯이 설다 means to be unfamiliar; strange or unaccustomed. 처음 가 본 도시의 골목길은 모든 풍경이 낯이 설어 긴장되었어요. (In the alleyways of the newly visited city, every scene felt unfamiliar and tense.)

Q2Which idiom means "to cut ties with"?

  • ① 진땀을 흘리다
  • ② 입에 침이 마르다
  • ③ 담을 쌓다
  • ④ 뒷짐을 지다

담을 쌓다 means to cut ties with; to build a wall against; to have nothing to do with. 운동과 담을 쌓고 살았더니 계단 몇 개만 올라도 숨이 차요. (Living with a wall built against exercise, even a few stairs make me breathless.)

Q3What does 등을 떠밀다 mean?

  • ① to happen in rapid succession
  • ② to whet one's appetite
  • ③ to be embroiled in scandal
  • ④ to push someone into doing something

등을 떠밀다 means to push someone into doing something; to nudge or pressure. 친구들이 등을 떠미는 바람에 얼떨결에 장기자랑 무대에 올라갔어요. (With friends pushing my back, I ended up on the talent show stage in a daze.)

Q4Which idiom means "to have a wide circle of acquaintances"?

  • ① 발이 넓다
  • ② 허를 찌르다
  • ③ 발자취를 남기다
  • ④ 시간에 쫓기다

발이 넓다 means to have a wide circle of acquaintances; to be well-connected. 그 친구는 발이 넓어서 어느 모임에 가나 아는 사람이 있어요. (That friend is so well-connected that he knows someone in every gathering.)

Q5What does 파리를 날리다 mean?

  • ① to make time
  • ② to have no customers
  • ③ to do something in all sincerity
  • ④ to place hopes on

파리를 날리다 means to have no customers; business is dead; to sit around shooing flies. 경기 침체로 상가 골목의 많은 식당들이 손님이 없어 파리를 날리고 있어요. (Due to recession, many restaurants in commercial alleys sit shooing flies.)

Q6Which idiom means "to be caught red-handed"?

  • ① 숨을 쉬다
  • ② 연락이 닿다
  • ③ 뒷덜미를 잡히다
  • ④ 입이 가볍다

뒷덜미를 잡히다 means to be caught red-handed; to have one's dirty secret or vulnerability seized. 교묘하게 횡령하던 범인이 내부 감사에서 마침내 뒷덜미를 잡혔어요. (The embezzler who had been so clever was finally caught in an internal audit.)
