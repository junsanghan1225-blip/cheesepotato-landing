Q1What does 진퇴양난 mean?

  • ① the more the better
  • ② caught in a dilemma
  • ③ turning a misfortune into a blessing
  • ④ alternating between joy and grief

진퇴양난 means caught in a dilemma; between a rock and a hard place. 앞으로 가자니 길이 막히고 뒤로 돌리자니 진퇴양난의 상황이에요. (Moving forward roads are blocked, turning back is tough, caught in a real dilemma.)

Q2Which four-character idiom means "feeling that a whole generation has passed"?

  • ① 학수고대
  • ② 권선징악
  • ③ 격세지감
  • ④ 감언이설

격세지감 means feeling that a whole generation has passed; sense of time's huge gap. 공중전화 줄 서던 시절을 떠올리면 휴대폰 세상은 격세지감이에요. (Recalling days of queuing for payphones, today's smartphone world feels worlds apart.)

Q3What does 환골탈태 mean?

  • ① putting the cart before the horse
  • ② useless thing
  • ③ surrounded by enemies on all sides
  • ④ undergoing a complete transformation

환골탈태 means undergoing a complete transformation; turning over a new leaf; born anew. 낡은 골목길이 카페 거리로 환골탈태하여 손님이 넘쳐나요. (The old alley underwent a complete transformation into a cafe street and is now crowded with visitors.)

Q4Which four-character idiom means "neck and neck match"?

  • ① 백중지세
  • ② 비일비재
  • ③ 양두구육
  • ④ 대동소이

백중지세 means neck and neck match; closely matched contest; evenly matched power. 두 팀의 전력이 워낙 뛰어나 이번 결승전은 백중지세의 접전이에요. (Because power of both teams is superior, this final is a closely matched, neck-and-neck battle.)

Q5What does 천편일률 mean?

  • ① treasuring dearly
  • ② monotonous conformity
  • ③ relationship like water and fish
  • ④ cutting and polishing jade

천편일률 means monotonous conformity; all cut from the same cloth. 자기만의 개성 없이 천편일률적인 답변만 외워 가면 면접에서 떨어져요. (If you only memorize uniform cliché answers without individuality, you will fail the interview.)

Q6Which four-character idiom means "lamenting that it is too late"?

  • ① 일벌백계
  • ② 망연자실
  • ③ 만시지탄
  • ④ 일거양득

만시지탄 means lamenting that it is too late; regretting missed opportunities. 건강을 잃고 나서야 운동을 시작했으니 만시지탄이 아닐 수 없어요. (Starting exercise only after losing one's health, it is nothing short of lamenting too late.)
