퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 감언이설 mean?

  • ① the more the better
  • ② sweet talk
  • ③ working by day and studying by night
  • ④ repaying a favor even from beyond the grave

감언이설 means sweet talk; honeyed words to deceive others. 사기꾼의 감언이설에 속아서 평생 모은 돈을 몽땅 잃을 뻔했어요. (I nearly lost all my life savings by falling for the fraudster's sweet talk.)

Q2Which four-character idiom means "clear at a glance"?

  • ① 학수고대
  • ② 화기애애
  • ③ 일목요연
  • ④ 노심초사

일목요연 means clear at a glance; obvious at first sight. 복잡한 수치를 도표로 정리하니 내용이 일목요연하게 보여요. (Organizing complex figures into a chart makes the content clear at a single glance.)

Q3What does 자화자찬 mean?

  • ① seeking one's own advantage
  • ② hitting the bull's-eye every time
  • ③ surrounded by enemies on all sides
  • ④ singing one's own praises

자화자찬 means singing one's own praises; blowing one's own horn. 자화자찬처럼 들리겠지만 이번 요리는 제가 먹어도 최고예요. (It may sound like blowing my own horn, but even to me, this dish is the best.)

Q4Which four-character idiom means "hearing one and knowing ten"?

  • ① 문일지십
  • ② 부지기수
  • ③ 용두사미
  • ④ 대동소이

문일지십 means hearing one and knowing ten; exceptionally sharp-witted and quick to understand. 새로 들어온 인턴은 문일지십이라 하나를 가르치면 척척 해내요. (The newly arrived intern is so quick on the uptake that teaching one thing leads to getting it done promptly.)

Q5What does 우유부단 mean?

  • ① trembling in constant fear and apprehension
  • ② indecisive
  • ③ relationship like water and fish
  • ④ innocent and naive

우유부단 means indecisive; wishy-washy; wavering. 우유부단하게 망설이다가는 좋은 기회를 다른 사람에게 뺏겨요. (If you waver indecisively, you will lose precious opportunities to others.)

Q6Which four-character idiom means "armchair discussion"?

  • ① 무소불위
  • ② 안분지족
  • ③ 탁상공론
  • ④ 일거양득

탁상공론 means armchair discussion; unpractical desk theory. 현장도 안 가 보고 사무실에서만 세운 계획은 탁상공론에 그치기 쉬워요. (Plans drafted inside an office without visiting the field easily end up as armchair theories.)
