퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 자업자득 mean?

  • ① putting oneself in another person's shoes
  • ② reaping what one sows
  • ③ making rapid and constant progress day by day
  • ④ the guilty party turning around to blame the innocent

자업자득 means reaping what one sows; suffering consequences of one's own doing. 거짓말을 일삼더니 결국 아무도 안 믿게 된 건 자업자득이에요. (Telling lies repeatedly and ending up with nobody trusting you is reaping what you sowed.)

Q2Which four-character idiom means "the more the better"?

  • ① 오리무중
  • ② 수수방관
  • ③ 다다익선
  • ④ 자승자박

다다익선 means the more the better. 비상식량이나 생필품은 미리 준비할 때 다다익선이라고 생각해요. (When preparing emergency food and essentials in advance, the more the better.)

Q3What does 삼십육계 mean?

  • ① a candle flickering in the wind
  • ② crowded with visitors
  • ③ mulberry fields turn into blue seas
  • ④ thirty-six stratagems

삼십육계 means thirty-six stratagems; running away is the best strategy when cornered. 동네 사나운 개가 쫓아오길래 뒤도 안 돌아보고 삼십육계로 도망쳤어요. (Because a fierce neighborhood dog chased after me, I ran for my life without looking back.)

Q4Which four-character idiom means "falling seven times and getting up eight"?

  • ① 칠전팔기
  • ② 막역지우
  • ③ 양두구육
  • ④ 호가호위

칠전팔기 means falling seven times and getting up eight; perseverance. 자격증 시험에 여러 번 떨어졌지만 칠전팔기의 정신으로 붙었어요. (After failing the certification exam several times, I passed with persistent grit.)

Q5What does 천진난만 mean?

  • ① making continuous progress without hindrance
  • ② innocent and naive
  • ③ relationship like water and fish
  • ④ indomitable

천진난만 means innocent and naive; pure-hearted; childlike innocence. 운동장에서 뛰노는 아이들의 천진난만한 웃음소리에 피로가 풀려요. (Fatigue melts away at the pure, innocent laughter of children playing in the playground.)

Q6Which four-character idiom means "treasuring dearly"?

  • ① 동량지재
  • ② 안분지족
  • ③ 애지중지
  • ④ 일거양득

애지중지 means treasuring dearly; pampering with deep affection. 할머니께서 첫 손주를 품에 안고 애지중지 돌보시는 모습이 참 따뜻해요. (Watching grandmother cradle and dearly cherish her first grandchild is heartwarming.)
