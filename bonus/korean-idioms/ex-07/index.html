Q1What does 쥐구멍에 들어가다 mean?

  • ① to be ripped off
  • ② to want to sink through the floor
  • ③ to view with prejudice
  • ④ to throw cold water on

쥐구멍에 들어가다 means to want to sink through the floor; to be deeply ashamed. 중요한 발표 자리에서 치명적인 실수를 해서 쥐구멍에 들어가고 싶었어요. (Making a critical mistake in an important presentation made me want to sink through the floor.)

Q2Which idiom means "to whet one's appetite"?

  • ① 날개 돋친 듯이 팔리다
  • ② 가슴에 못을 박다
  • ③ 입맛을 돋우다
  • ④ 벽에 부딪히다

입맛을 돋우다 means to whet one's appetite; to stimulate the palate. 새콤달콤한 봄나물 무침이 지쳐 있던 입맛을 산뜻하게 돋우어 주네요. (Sweet and sour spring greens refreshingly whet my tired appetite.)

Q3What does 귀가 얇다 mean?

  • ① to draw glares
  • ② to establish oneself
  • ③ to nod in agreement
  • ④ to be gullible

귀가 얇다 means to be gullible; to be easily influenced by others' words. 귀가 얇아서 남들이 좋다고 추천하는 물건은 바로 사 버려요. (Because I am gullible, I immediately buy anything others recommend as good.)

Q4Which idiom means "to stand by with folded arms"?

  • ① 뒷짐을 지다
  • ② 활기를 띠다
  • ③ 바통을 이어받다
  • ④ 시간을 끌다

뒷짐을 지다 means to stand by with folded arms; to remain an idle onlooker. 모두가 밤새워 일하는데 혼자 뒷짐을 지고 구경만 하면 어떡해요? (How can you just stand by with folded arms watching while everyone works overnight?)

Q5What does 성의를 다하다 mean?

  • ① to cost time
  • ② to do something in all sincerity
  • ③ to grow fond of
  • ④ to take an interest in

성의를 다하다 means to do something in all sincerity; to devote one's full sincerity. 손님 한 분 한 분을 모실 때마다 성의를 다해 친절하게 응대하고 있어요. (Whenever serving every single guest, we respond kindly with all sincerity.)

Q6Which idiom means "to be generous"?

  • ① 땀을 흘리다
  • ② 노력을 기울이다
  • ③ 손이 크다
  • ④ 입이 무겁다

손이 크다 means to be generous; to do things on a grand scale (esp. cooking or spending). 우리 어머니는 손이 커서 손님맞이 음식을 열 그릇이나 만드셨어요. (My mother is so generous that she made ten bowls of food to welcome guests.)
