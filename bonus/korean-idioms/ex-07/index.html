퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 쓴맛을 보다 mean?

  • ① to turn one's back on
  • ② to taste bitterness
  • ③ to cut ties with
  • ④ to be on the edge of one's seat

쓴맛을 보다 means to taste bitterness; to experience bitter failure or defeat. 무리하게 사업을 확장하다가 금융 위기를 맞아 혹독한 쓴맛을 보았어요. (Expanding recklessly, he tasted bitter defeat facing the financial crisis.)

Q2Which idiom means "to be humiliated"?

  • ① 등을 떠밀다
  • ② 각광을 받다
  • ③ 코가 납작해지다
  • ④ 속을 썩이다

코가 납작해지다 means to be humiliated; to have one's pride crushed; taken down a notch. 만년 일등을 자랑하던 아이가 이번 시험에서 밀려 코가 납작해졌어요. (The kid boasting of always being first got overtaken and was taken down a notch.)

Q3What does 간이 크다 mean?

  • ① to draw glares
  • ② to establish oneself
  • ③ to nod in agreement
  • ④ to be bold

간이 크다 means to be bold; to have a lot of nerve; daring or audacious. 어두운 밤에 혼자 험한 산을 오르다니 정말 간이 큰 사람이에요. (Climbing a rugged mountain alone in the dark night shows he really has nerve.)

Q4Which idiom means "to have high standards"?

  • ① 눈이 높다
  • ② 허를 찌르다
  • ③ 물꼬를 트다
  • ④ 시간을 끌다

눈이 높다 means to have high standards; to be picky or discerning. 눈이 높아서 웬만한 옷은 쳐다보지도 않고 고급만 찾아요. (Having high standards, she doesn't even look at ordinary clothes and only seeks luxury.)

Q5What does 모범을 보이다 mean?

  • ① to have free time
  • ② to set an example
  • ③ to summon courage
  • ④ to place hopes on

모범을 보이다 means to set an example; to serve as a role model. 선배들이 먼저 솔선수범하여 모범을 보여 준 덕분에 팀 분위기가 좋아졌어요. (Thanks to seniors taking initiative and setting examples, team morale improved.)

Q6Which idiom means "to wash one's hands of"?

  • ① 잠이 들다
  • ② 영향을 미치다
  • ③ 손을 씻다
  • ④ 입이 무겁다

손을 씻다 means to wash one's hands of; to reform and quit wrongdoing. 도박의 늪에서 손을 씻고 건실하게 직장 생활을 시작했어요. (Washing my hands of gambling's swamp, I began a healthy workplace life.)
