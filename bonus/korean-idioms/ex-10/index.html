퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 발목을 잡히다 mean?

  • ① to be scared out of one's wits
  • ② to be held back
  • ③ to roll up one's sleeves
  • ④ to close one's eyes tight

발목을 잡히다 means to be held back; to be trapped by one's weakness or unforeseen obstacle. 과거에 작성했던 부적절한 글 때문에 중요한 승진에서 발목을 잡혔어요. (Due to improper posts written in the past, he was held back from a key promotion.)

Q2Which idiom means "to shoulder a heavy burden"?

  • ① 시치미를 떼다
  • ② 이를 갈다
  • ③ 어깨가 무겁다
  • ④ 다리를 놓다

어깨가 무겁다 means to shoulder a heavy burden; to feel heavy responsibility. 프로젝트 팀장을 맡게 되니 책임감 때문에 어깨가 무거워요. (Being appointed project team leader, my shoulders feel heavy with responsibility.)

Q3What does 한숨을 돌리다 mean?

  • ① to lend an ear to
  • ② to have a hard time
  • ③ to nod in agreement
  • ④ to catch one's breath

한숨을 돌리다 means to catch one's breath; to take a breather. 밀려들던 주문 물량을 모두 처리하고 나서야 비로소 한숨을 돌릴 수 있었어요. (Only after clearing all surging orders could we finally take a breather.)

Q4Which idiom means "to be struck with awe"?

  • ① 혀를 내두르다
  • ② 코앞에 닥치다
  • ③ 쓴맛을 보다
  • ④ 약속을 지키다

혀를 내두르다 means to be struck with awe; to shake one's head in amazement or disbelief. 어린 신동의 놀라운 기억력과 계산 능력을 보고 전문가들도 혀를 내둘렀어요. (Watching the child prodigy's memory and math skills, experts were struck with awe.)

Q5What does 발자취를 남기다 mean?

  • ① to spend time
  • ② to leave one's mark
  • ③ to cast a glance at
  • ④ to seize an opportunity

발자취를 남기다 means to leave one's mark; to leave footprints for posterity. 평생을 봉사와 희생으로 헌신한 의사는 인류애의 역사에 큰 발자취를 남겼다. (The doctor who devoted life to service left a great mark on human history.)

Q6Which idiom means "to open the floodgates"?

  • ① 잠을 깨다
  • ② 영향을 미치다
  • ③ 물꼬를 트다
  • ④ 손이 크다

물꼬를 트다 means to open the floodgates; to pave the way; to break the ice. 양국 정상 간의 솔직한 대화는 교착 상태에 빠졌던 외교 협상의 물꼬를 텄다. (Frank talks between leaders broke the ice on deadlocked diplomatic negotiations.)
