퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 지도 mean?

  • ① to cross over
  • ② map
  • ③ to go down
  • ④ east

지도 means map. 선생님의 따뜻한 지도를 받았어요. (I received warm guidance from the teacher.)

Q2Which Korean word means "to walk here"?

  • ① 남쪽
  • ② 돌리다
  • ③ 걸어오다
  • ④ 똑바로

걸어오다 means to walk here; come on foot. 학교까지 천천히 걸어와요. (I walk slowly all the way to school.)

Q3What does 내려가다 mean?

  • ① to turn
  • ② to fall
  • ③ to slip
  • ④ to go down

내려가다 means to go down; to head down. 엘리베이터를 타고 지하로 내려가요. (I take the elevator and go down to the basement.)

Q4Which Korean word means "to turn"?

  • ① 돌다
  • ② 떨어지다
  • ③ 미끄러지다
  • ④ 나타나다

돌다 means to turn; to spin. 공원 주변을 한 바퀴 돌았어요. (I turned one round around the park.)

Q5What does 뒤쪽 mean?

  • ① being far
  • ② back
  • ③ cross
  • ④ to come down

뒤쪽 means back; rear. 버스 뒤쪽 자리로 이동해 주세요. (Please move to the seats at the back of the bus.)

Q6Which Korean word means "straight"?

  • ① 건너가다
  • ② 내려가다
  • ③ 똑바로
  • ④ 동쪽

똑바로 means straight; upright. 의자에 허리를 펴고 똑바로 앉아요. (Sit upright on the chair with your back straight.)
