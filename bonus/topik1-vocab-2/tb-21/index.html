퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 귀찮다 mean?

  • ① to miss
  • ② troublesome
  • ③ to stifling
  • ④ anxiety

귀찮다 means troublesome; annoying; tiresome. 청소하기 귀찮아도 방을 치워요. (Even though cleaning is bothersome, I tidy up the room.)

Q2Which Korean word means "with surprise"?

  • ① 느끼다
  • ② 불쌍하다
  • ③ 깜짝
  • ④ 슬픔

깜짝 means with surprise; surprisingly. 친구들이 깜짝 파티를 준비해 줬어요. (Friends prepared a surprise party for me.)

Q3What does 답답하다 mean?

  • ① to be shy
  • ② to grieve
  • ③ smile
  • ④ to stifling

답답하다 means to stifling; suffocating. 일이 잘 안 풀려서 마음이 답답해요. (Things are not working out, so my heart feels frustrated.)

Q4Which Korean word means "to be shy"?

  • ① 부끄럽다
  • ② 소중하다
  • ③ 울음
  • ④ 깜짝

부끄럽다 means to be shy; to be ashamed. 칭찬을 받아서 얼굴이 부끄럽게 붉어졌어요. (My face turned bashfully red receiving compliments.)

Q5What does 섭섭하다 mean?

  • ① lonely
  • ② to sorry
  • ③ nervousness
  • ④ date

섭섭하다 means to sorry; regrettable. 약속을 취소해서 섭섭한 마음이 들었어요. (I felt disappointed because the appointment was cancelled.)

Q6Which Korean word means "sadness"?

  • ① 그립다
  • ② 답답하다
  • ③ 슬픔
  • ④ 불안

슬픔 means sadness; grief. 친구와 기쁨과 슬픔을 함께 나눠요. (I share joy and sadness together with friends.)
