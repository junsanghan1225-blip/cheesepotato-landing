퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 빵집 mean?

  • ① Western restaurant
  • ② bakery
  • ③ Korean restaurant
  • ④ to be like that

빵집 means bakery. 빵집 앞을 지날 때 고소한 냄새가 나요. (A savory smell comes when passing by the bakery.)

Q2Which Korean word means "Japanese restaurant"?

  • ① 중국집
  • ② 이렇다
  • ③ 일식집
  • ④ 등

일식집 means Japanese restaurant. 그 일식집 초밥이 맛있어요. (The sushi at that Japanese restaurant is delicious.)

Q3What does 한식집 mean?

  • ① thick
  • ② therefore
  • ③ set
  • ④ Korean restaurant

한식집 means Korean restaurant. 그 한식집은 반찬이 잘 나와요. (Side dishes are served well at that Korean restaurant.)

Q4Which Korean word means "thick"?

  • ① 진하다
  • ② 그러나
  • ③ 개월
  • ④ 일식집

진하다 means thick; rich; dark; deep. 향기가 아주 진해요. (The scent is very deep.)

Q5What does 또는 mean?

  • ① chapter
  • ② or
  • ③ restaurant
  • ④ treat

또는 means or; either. 토요일 또는 일요일에 만나요. (Let's meet on Saturday or Sunday.)

Q6Which Korean word means "and the like"?

  • ① 양식집
  • ② 한식집
  • ③ 등
  • ④ 저렇다

등 means and the like; etc.. 연필, 지우개 등을 필통에 챙겨요. (I pack pencils, erasers, etc. into the pencil case.)
