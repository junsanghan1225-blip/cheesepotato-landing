퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 참다 mean?

  • ① attitude
  • ② endure
  • ③ to fight
  • ④ joy

참다 means endure; withstand; bear; be patient. 조금만 더 참으세요. (Please endure just a little more.)

Q2Which Korean word means "to be scary"?

  • ① 놀라다
  • ② 행복
  • ③ 무섭다
  • ④ 걱정

무섭다 means to be scary; to be afraid. 무서운 영화를 보고 깜짝 놀랐어요. (I watched a scary movie and was surprised.)

Q3What does 싸우다 mean?

  • ① to be envious
  • ② hope
  • ③ to adore
  • ④ to fight

싸우다 means to fight; to quarrel. 어제 동생과 다투며 싸웠어요. (Yesterday I quarrelled and fought with my sibling.)

Q4Which Korean word means "to be envious"?

  • ① 부럽다
  • ② 심심하다
  • ③ 궁금하다
  • ④ 무섭다

부럽다 means to be envious; to envy. 노래를 잘하는 동생이 참 부러워요. (I'm really envious of my sibling who sings well.)

Q5What does 화나다 mean?

  • ① interest
  • ② get angry
  • ③ to be excellent
  • ④ be glad

화나다 means get angry; be furious; be enraged. 화난 얼굴을 풀고 웃어 보세요. (Please soften your angry face and smile.)

Q6Which Korean word means "worry"?

  • ① 훌륭하다
  • ② 기뻐하다
  • ③ 걱정
  • ④ 화나다

걱정 means worry; concern. 너무 걱정하지 말고 힘내세요. (Don't worry too much and cheer up.)
