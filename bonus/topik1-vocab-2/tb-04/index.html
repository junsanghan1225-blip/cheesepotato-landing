퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does 올리다 mean?

  • ① move
  • ② to raise
  • ③ full
  • ④ to be slow

올리다 means to raise; to increase. 손을 번쩍 올려요. (Raise your hand high.)

Q2Which Korean word means "to reduce"?

  • ① 정도
  • ② 길이
  • ③ 줄이다
  • ④ 모든

줄이다 means to reduce; to decrease. 쓰레기를 많이 줄여요. (Let's reduce trash a lot.)

Q3What does 가득 mean?

  • ① etc.
  • ② very
  • ③ meter (unit)
  • ④ full

가득 means full; completely. 행복이 가득한 하루를 보내세요. (Have a day full of happiness.)

Q4Which Korean word means "etc."?

  • ① 기타
  • ② 똑같이
  • ③ 무척
  • ④ 줄이다

기타 means etc.; others; and so on. 과일, 채소, 기타 등등을 샀어요. (I bought fruit, vegetables and so on.)

Q5What does 더욱 mean?

  • ① weight
  • ② more and more
  • ③ to go wrong
  • ④ to be strong

더욱 means more and more; further. 열심히 연습해서 실력이 더욱 좋아졌어요. (I practiced hard and my skills got even better.)

Q6Which Korean word means "all"?

  • ① 움직이다
  • ② 가득
  • ③ 모든
  • ④ 느리다

모든 means all; every. 준비가 모든 면에서 완벽해요. (The preparation is perfect in every aspect.)
