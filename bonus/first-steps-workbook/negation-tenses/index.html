퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What is the difference between 안 and 못 in Korean?

  • ① 안 is inability; 못 is personal choice
  • ② 안 is not (choice/state); 못 is cannot (inability/circumstance)
  • ③ Both mean exactly the same thing
  • ④ 못 is only used for past tense

안 expresses simple negation or choice not to do something, while 못 expresses inability or impossibility.

Q2What is the past tense form of 마시다 (to drink)?

  • ① 마시었어요
  • ② 마셨어요
  • ③ 마시았어요
  • ④ 마시했어요

마시- contracts with -었어요 to form 마셨어요.

Q3How do you say I will study Korean tomorrow in polite future tense?

  • ① 내일 한국어를 공부했어요
  • ② 내일 한국어를 공부할 거예요
  • ③ 내일 한국어를 공부해요
  • ④ 내일 한국어를 공부하지 않아요

-(으)ㄹ 거예요 forms the standard polite future tense: 공부하다 -> 공부할 거예요.

Q4Why is 오늘 날씨가 못 추워요 incorrect in Korean?

  • ① 못 cannot be used with adjectives (describing states)
  • ② 날씨 cannot be a subject
  • ③ 추워요 is already negative
  • ④ Today should be yesterday

못 only negates dynamic action verbs (inability to act). Adjectives must be negated with 안 (안 추워요).

Q5What is the past tense form of 하다 (to do)?

  • ① 하았어요
  • ② 하었어요
  • ③ 했어요
  • ④ 했었어요

하다 always changes to 했어요 in the polite past tense.
