퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What color is a crow (까마귀)?

  • ① 빨간색
  • ② 파란색
  • ③ 노란색
  • ④ 검은색

A crow is black, which is 검은색 in Korean.

Q2What is the three-sided triangle shape called in everyday Korean?

  • ① 동그라미
  • ② 세모
  • ③ 네모
  • ④ 별

세모 is the everyday word for triangle (세 means three).

Q3What is the opposite of 무겁다 (heavy)?

  • ① 가볍다
  • ② 작다
  • ③ 짧다
  • ④ 느리다

가볍다 means light in weight, which is the opposite of 무겁다.

Q4What is the opposite of 빠르다 (fast) in the polite present tense?

  • ① 느려요
  • ② 무거워요
  • ③ 쉬워요
  • ④ 추워요

The opposite of 빠르다 (fast) is 느리다 (slow), which conjugates to 느려요.

Q5What color is clean snow (눈)?

  • ① 흰색
  • ② 초록색
  • ③ 검은색
  • ④ 파란색

Snow is white, which is 흰색 or 하얀색.
