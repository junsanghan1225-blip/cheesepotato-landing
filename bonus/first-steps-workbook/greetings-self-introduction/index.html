퀴즈 Quiz

Q1You meet your new boss for the first time. What is the best thing to say?

  • ① 안녕
  • ② 처음 뵙겠습니다
  • ③ 또 만나요
  • ④ 어서 오세요

처음 뵙겠습니다 is the formal way to greet someone you meet for the first time.

Q2A customer walks into your shop. What do you say?

  • ① 어서 오세요
  • ② 잘 부탁드립니다
  • ③ 안녕히 주무세요
  • ④ 이름이 뭐예요?

어서 오세요 means welcome. The shop says it to the person who comes in.

Q3저는 일본에서 왔어요 means:

  • ① I am going to Japan.
  • ② I came from Japan.
  • ③ I like Japan.
  • ④ Japan is far.

-에서 왔어요 means came from. 일본에서 = from Japan.

Q4Which question asks about someone's nationality?

  • ① 이름이 뭐예요?
  • ② 어느 나라 사람이에요?
  • ③ 몇 살이에요?
  • ④ 어디에 살아요?

어느 나라 사람이에요? literally asks which country person you are.

Q5Before bed, you say good night to your grandmother.

  • ① 잘 자
  • ② 안녕히 주무세요
  • ③ 또 만나요
  • ④ 처음 뵙겠습니다

안녕히 주무세요 is the respectful form of good night for an older person.
