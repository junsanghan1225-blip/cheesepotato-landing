퀴즈 Quiz

Q1When 누구 (who) is combined with the subject particle 가, how is it correctly written?

  • ① 누가
  • ② 누구가
  • ③ 누구이
  • ④ 누고가

When 누구 acts as the subject with particle 가, it must always contract to 누가.

Q2Which question word asks about location ('where')?

  • ① 어디
  • ② 언제
  • ③ 무엇
  • ④ 어떻게

어디 specifically asks about places or locations.

Q3Which word is used to ask for the price of an item in Korean (e.g., 'How much is this?')?

  • ① 얼마
  • ② 몇
  • ③ 언제
  • ④ 누구

얼마 is used to ask about monetary prices: 이거 얼마예요? (How much is this?).

Q4Which question asks 'What time is it right now?' using counter word 시 (o'clock)?

  • ① 지금 몇 시예요?
  • ② 지금 얼마 시예요?
  • ③ 지금 언제 시예요?
  • ④ 지금 어디 시예요?

몇 is used directly before counter nouns: 지금 몇 시예요? (What time is it now?).

Q5How do you ask someone 'Why are you learning Korean?'

  • ① 왜 한국어를 배워요?
  • ② 어디 한국어를 배워요?
  • ③ 누가 한국어를 배워요?
  • ④ 몇 한국어를 배워요?

왜 asks for the reason or purpose behind an action ('Why').
