퀴즈 Quiz

Q1How do you call a server over at a Korean restaurant?

  • ① 여보세요
  • ② 저기요
  • ③ 누구세요
  • ④ 어서 오세요

저기요 or 여기요 is used to politely get the server s attention.

Q2What does 계산해 주세요 mean when finishing a meal?

  • ① Please bring water.
  • ② Please show me the menu.
  • ③ Check, please. / Please give me the bill.
  • ④ One more, please.

계산해 주세요 asks the cashier or server for the bill/check.

Q3Which word describes the taste of hot spicy kimchi stew (김치찌개)?

  • ① 달아요
  • ② 매워요
  • ③ 셔요
  • ④ 싱거워요

Kimchi stew is typically spicy, which is 맵다 (매워요 in polite present).

Q4Which counter is used to order servings of barbecue meat (고기)?

  • ① 인분
  • ② 명
  • ③ 권
  • ④ 마리

인분 is the counter for food portions or servings per person.

Q5How do you read 삼겹살 2인분 when you order?

  • ① 이 인분
  • ② 두 인분
  • ③ 둘 인분
  • ④ 이 개

인분 takes Sino-Korean numbers, so 2인분 is read 이 인분.
