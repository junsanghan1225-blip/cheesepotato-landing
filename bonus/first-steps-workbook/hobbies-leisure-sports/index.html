퀴즈 Quiz

Q1Which verb is used with ball sports with rackets/clubs like 테니스 or 골프?

  • ① 해요
  • ② 타요
  • ③ 쳐요
  • ④ 마셔요

치다 (쳐요) is used for sports involving hitting a ball, such as tennis, golf, and badminton.

Q2Which verb pairs with riding a bicycle (자전거) or skis (스키)?

  • ① 타요
  • ② 쳐요
  • ③ 봐요
  • ④ 읽어요

타다 (타요) means to ride, used for bicycles, skis, and skateboards.

Q3What does 독서 mean in English?

  • ① reading books
  • ② swimming
  • ③ drawing
  • ④ cooking

독서 means reading books as a hobby.

Q4How do you ask 'What do you do on weekends?' in polite Korean?

  • ① 주말에 뭐 해요?
  • ② 주말이 뭐예요?
  • ③ 주말에 어디예요?
  • ④ 몇 주말이에요?

주말에 뭐 해요? literally asks what you do on the weekend.

Q5Which hobby involves taking pictures with a camera?

  • ① 등산
  • ② 사진 촬영
  • ③ 수영
  • ④ 낚시

사진 촬영 means photography or taking photos.
