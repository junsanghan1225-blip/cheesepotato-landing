퀴즈 Quiz

Q1Which counter is used for cats and dogs?

  • ① 개
  • ② 명
  • ③ 마리
  • ④ 권

마리 is the counter for animals like cats, dogs, and birds.

Q2How do you say one cup of green tea in Korean?

  • ① 녹차 일 잔
  • ② 녹차 하나 잔
  • ③ 녹차 한 잔
  • ④ 녹차 첫 잔

Before a counter, 하나 changes to 한, so one cup is 한 잔.

Q3Which number system is used for minutes (분)?

  • ① Native Korean (하나, 둘...)
  • ② Sino-Korean (일, 이...)
  • ③ Either one
  • ④ Neither

Hours (시) use Native Korean numbers, but minutes (분) use Sino-Korean numbers.

Q4How do you read 10,000 won in Korean?

  • ① 천 원
  • ② 만 원
  • ③ 십만 원
  • ④ 백만 원

10,000 in Sino-Korean is 만, so 10,000 won is 만 원.

Q5What happens to 둘 when placed right before a counter?

  • ① It stays 둘
  • ② It changes to 두
  • ③ It changes to 이
  • ④ It drops completely

하나, 둘, 셋, 넷 shorten to 한, 두, 세, 네 when placed directly before a counter.
