퀴즈 Quiz

Q1How many representative batchim sounds exist when final consonants are pronounced in isolation?

  • ① 5
  • ② 7
  • ③ 9
  • ④ 14

All Korean final consonants collapse into one of 7 representative sounds: [ㄱ ㄴ ㄷ ㄹ ㅁ ㅂ ㅇ].

Q2What representative sound does the final consonant in 옷 (clothes) make when spoken alone?

  • ① [ㄱ]
  • ② [ㄴ]
  • ③ [ㄷ]
  • ④ [ㅂ]

The letter ㅅ simplifies to the representative stop sound [ㄷ] at the end of a syllable.

Q3According to the liaison rule (연음), how is the phrase 부엌에 pronounced?

  • ① [부어게]
  • ② [부어케]
  • ③ [부억에]
  • ④ [부얼에]

The final consonant ㅋ moves into the silent ㅇ of 에, so 부엌에 is read [부어케].

Q4Which of the following words ends with the [ㅂ] representative sound when spoken alone?

  • ① 밥
  • ② 달
  • ③ 눈
  • ④ 강

밥 ends with ㅂ, which is pronounced as the representative bilabial stop [ㅂ].

Q5How is the phrase 집에 (to home) pronounced in natural spoken Korean?

  • ① [지베]
  • ② [집에]
  • ③ [짐에]
  • ④ [지페]

Due to liaison, the final ㅂ carries over into the following vowel 에, producing [지베].
