퀴즈 Quiz

Q1Which ending attaches to the stem of 좋다 (좋-)?

  • ① -아요
  • ② -어요
  • ③ -해요
  • ④ -여요

The stem vowel of 좋다 is ㅗ, so it takes -아요 to become 좋아요.

Q2What is the correct polite present form of 배우다 (to learn)?

  • ① 배와요
  • ② 배워요
  • ③ 배우해요
  • ④ 배우어요

The stem vowel is ㅜ, which combines with -어요 into -워요 (배워요).

Q3What is the polite present form of 노래하다 (to sing)?

  • ① 노래하요
  • ② 노래해요
  • ③ 노래하여요
  • ④ 노래와요

Verbs ending in 하다 always change to 해요 in the polite present tense.

Q4Which verb conjugates with -어요?

  • ① 살다
  • ② 앉다
  • ③ 열다
  • ④ 받다

열다 has the stem vowel ㅕ, so it takes -어요 (열어요). The others have ㅏ and take -아요.

Q5What is the polite present form of 보다 (to see / watch)?

  • ① 보아요
  • ② 보어요
  • ③ 봐요
  • ④ 보해요

보- combines with -아요 to contract into 봐요.
