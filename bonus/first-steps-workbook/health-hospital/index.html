퀴즈 Quiz

Q1How do you say 'My back hurts' in Korean?

  • ① 머리가 아파요
  • ② 허리가 아파요
  • ③ 눈이 아파요
  • ④ 발이 아파요

허리 means back (waist), so 허리가 아파요 means My back hurts.

Q2What does 어지러워요 mean?

  • ① I feel dizzy.
  • ② I feel hungry.
  • ③ I feel sleepy.
  • ④ I feel cold.

어지럽다 (어지러워요) means to feel dizzy.

Q3What does the doctor say when telling you to get plenty of rest?

  • ① 운동하세요
  • ② 푹 쉬세요
  • ③ 일하세요
  • ④ 노래하세요

푹 쉬세요 means Please get plenty of rest.

Q4Which body part is used for seeing things?

  • ① 귀
  • ② 코
  • ③ 눈
  • ④ 입

눈 means eye (and eyes).

Q5What does 배탈이 났어요 mean?

  • ① I have an upset stomach.
  • ② I have a cough.
  • ③ I hurt my leg.
  • ④ I have a toothache.

배탈이 나다 means to get an upset stomach.
