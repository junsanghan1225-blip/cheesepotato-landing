퀴즈 Quiz

Q1How do you say to transfer from one transit line to another?

  • ① 내려요
  • ② 타요
  • ③ 갈아타요
  • ④ 걸어가요

갈아타다 (갈아타요) means to transfer between buses or subway lines.

Q2Which phrase means to go straight ahead?

  • ① 왼쪽으로 가세요
  • ② 오른쪽으로 가세요
  • ③ 곧장 가세요
  • ④ 뒤로 가세요

곧장 가세요 or 똑바로 가세요 means Go straight ahead.

Q3Which vehicle flies in the sky between countries?

  • ① 지하철
  • ② 기차
  • ③ 비행기
  • ④ 버스

비행기 means airplane.

Q4How do you ask How long does it take? in Korean?

  • ① 얼마나 걸려요?
  • ② 어디에 가요?
  • ③ 누구하고 가요?
  • ④ 몇 시예요?

얼마나 걸려요? asks How long does it take?

Q5What does 길을 건너다 mean?

  • ① to cross the street
  • ② to build a road
  • ③ to lose one s way
  • ④ to take a taxi

길을 건너다 means to cross the street or road.
