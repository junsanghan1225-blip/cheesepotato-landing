퀴즈 Quiz

Q1Which letter makes the 'm' sound in Korean?

  • ① ㄱ
  • ② ㄴ
  • ③ ㅁ
  • ④ ㅂ

ㅁ (미음) represents the m sound, as in 모자 (hat).

Q2Which of the following is a horizontal vowel written below the consonant?

  • ① ㅏ
  • ② ㅓ
  • ③ ㅗ
  • ④ ㅣ

ㅗ is a horizontal vowel and is positioned directly underneath the initial consonant, like in 고.

Q3When ㅇ is used at the beginning of a syllable block (like in 오), what sound does it make?

  • ① It is silent and serves as a placeholder.
  • ② It sounds like 'ng'.
  • ③ It sounds like 'h'.
  • ④ It sounds like 'm'.

At the beginning of a syllable, ㅇ has no sound and simply holds the space for the vowel.

Q4What syllable is formed by combining the consonant ㄱ and the vowel ㅏ?

  • ① 가
  • ② 고
  • ③ 구
  • ④ 기

ㄱ (g/k) plus vertical vowel ㅏ (a) forms the syllable block 가 (ga).

Q5What is the official Korean name for the consonant letter ㄴ?

  • ① 기역
  • ② 니은
  • ③ 디귿
  • ④ 리을

The consonant ㄴ is officially called 니은 (nieun).
