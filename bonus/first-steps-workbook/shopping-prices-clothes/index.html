퀴즈 Quiz

Q1Which verb is used for wearing a ring or gloves?

  • ① 입어요
  • ② 신어요
  • ③ 써요
  • ④ 껴요

끼다 (껴요) is used for rings, gloves and glasses frames that fit snugly on your fingers or hands.

Q2Which verb is used for putting on a hat (모자) or glasses (안경)?

  • ① 입어요
  • ② 신어요
  • ③ 써요
  • ④ 메요

쓰다 (써요) is used for headwear like hats and glasses.

Q3What counter is used for suits, shirts, and general pieces of clothing?

  • ① 벌
  • ② 켤레
  • ③ 권
  • ④ 마리

벌 is the counter for outfits and pieces of clothing.

Q4What counter is used for a pair of shoes (신발) or socks (양말)?

  • ① 벌
  • ② 켤레
  • ③ 개
  • ④ 장

켤레 is the counter for matched pairs of footwear and socks.

Q5You want a paper that proves your purchase. What do you ask for?

  • ① 영수증 주세요
  • ② 봉투 주세요
  • ③ 거스름돈 주세요
  • ④ 포장해 주세요

영수증 means receipt.
