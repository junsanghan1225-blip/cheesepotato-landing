퀴즈 Quiz

Q1What does the teacher mean by 책을 펴세요?

  • ① Please close your book.
  • ② Please open your book.
  • ③ Please write in your book.
  • ④ Please read after me.

책을 펴세요 means Please open your book (펴다 means to open or unfold).

Q2When the teacher speaks too fast, what is the best polite request?

  • ① 잘 들으세요
  • ② 천천히 말씀해 주세요
  • ③ 공책에 쓰세요
  • ④ 칠판을 보세요

천천히 말씀해 주세요 politely asks Please speak slowly.

Q3What tool do you use to erase pencil writing?

  • ① 필통
  • ② 지우개
  • ③ 칠판
  • ④ 가방

지우개 means eraser (from 지우다 to erase).

Q4What does 따라 읽으세요 mean?

  • ① Please read after me.
  • ② Please write in silence.
  • ③ Please close the book.
  • ④ Please listen carefully.

따라 읽으세요 means Please read after me / repeat after me.

Q5How do you politely say I understand to your teacher?

  • ① 모르겠어요
  • ② 알겠습니다
  • ③ 아니에요
  • ④ 반갑습니다

알겠습니다 is the formal polite acknowledgment meaning Understood / I understand.
