퀴즈 Quiz

Q1How do you pronounce June (6월) in Korean?

  • ① 육월
  • ② 유월
  • ③ 륙월
  • ④ 구월

The final consonant drops before 월, so June is pronounced and written as 유월.

Q2How do you pronounce October (10월) in Korean?

  • ① 십월
  • ② 시월
  • ③ 열월
  • ④ 일월

The final consonant drops before 월, so October is written and spoken as 시월.

Q3Which day of the week is Wednesday?

  • ① 화요일
  • ② 수요일
  • ③ 목요일
  • ④ 금요일

수요일 is Wednesday (수 represents water).

Q4If today is Friday (금요일), what day is tomorrow (내일)?

  • ① 목요일
  • ② 토요일
  • ③ 일요일
  • ④ 월요일

The day after Friday (금요일) is Saturday (토요일).

Q5What does 모레 mean in English?

  • ① yesterday
  • ② tomorrow
  • ③ the day after tomorrow
  • ④ two days ago

모레 means the day after tomorrow. Tomorrow is 내일.
