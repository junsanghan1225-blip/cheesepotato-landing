Q1Which phrase acknowledges that an argument has reasonable and legitimate grounds in Q54?

  • ① 타당한 근거를 지니고 있다.
  • ② 전혀 터무니없는 주장이다.
  • ③ 아무런 이유가 없다.
  • ④ 거짓말에 불과하다.

'타당한 근거를 지니고 있다' (possesses valid grounds) formally validates an argument in balanced argumentative essays.

Q2Which connector is standard when presenting the counterargument to the preceding position?

  • ① 이에 반해
  • ② 마찬가지로
  • ③ 예를 들면
  • ④ 더불어

'이에 반해' (in contrast to this / on the contrary) introduces the contrasting opposing perspective.

Q3What does '장점을 살리고 단점을 보완하다' mean in a compromise conclusion?

  • ① To utilize the merits and remedy the demerits.
  • ② To discard both advantages and disadvantages.
  • ③ To focus only on negative aspects.
  • ④ To accept only one side entirely.

This expression describes a balanced solution: keep the strengths and make up for the weaknesses.

Q4Which sentence shows appropriate plain form essay ending for stating one's agreement in Q54?

  • ① 나는 이 제도의 점진적 도입에 찬성한다.
  • ② 나는 이 제도가 아주 마음에 들어요.
  • ③ 이 제도를 꼭 해 주시면 좋겠습니다.
  • ④ 다 같이 박수를 쳐라.

'찬성한다' is the formal plain form; polite or emotional forms (-들어요, -좋겠습니다) are not acceptable in Q54.

Q5Which term refers to reaching agreement through dialogue among diverse social groups?

  • ① 사회적 합의
  • ② 일방적 통보
  • ③ 무조건적 비판
  • ④ 개인적 편견

'사회적 합의' (social consensus) is the formal term for broad mutual agreement achieved through public discourse.
